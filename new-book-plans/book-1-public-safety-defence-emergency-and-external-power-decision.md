# Book 1 Public-Safety, Defence, Emergency, and External-Power Decision

> **Status: author-ratified on 2026-08-08; formal implementation pending.**
> This decision selects Book 1's public-safety, defence, emergency, and
> external-power baseline. It adds no predicate, rule, fact, pin, institution
> in law, chapter, release, or claim of present constitutional coverage. It
> does not reopen the internal secession path, the six ratified baselines it
> touches at the edges, or the edition gates.

## 1. Decision

Book 1 adopts one settlement across four related but non-collapsed subjects:

1. **Protective public functions are ordinary public institutions, separated
   from one another.** Policing, prosecution, adjudication, custodial
   execution, external defence, and security intelligence are six distinct
   mandates. None is an emergency exception, an inherent power, or a source of
   authority over a person that the ordinary rules do not supply.
2. **Force is the most limited thing public power does, not the most
   privileged.** Every coercive act needs a lawful trigger, an authorised
   actor, evidence, reasons, an independent reviewer, and a remedy. Certain
   acts are refused categorically and are placed inside the unamendable
   corridor.
3. **Emergency is an overlay that suspends nothing.** There is no derogation
   clause. A declaration may accelerate procedure, redirect resources,
   requisition with compensation, and impose reviewable restrictions against
   the named hazard. It may not suspend a right, an institution, an election,
   or a remedy, and it creates no standing power between declarations.
4. **The republic's external powers are enumerated and subordinate.** Borders,
   asylum, expulsion, extradition, treaties, external trade, defence, and the
   use of force abroad are common-tier competences bounded by the same
   corridor that binds domestic law. Everyone within the republic's
   jurisdiction or effective control is owed standing, the material floor, due
   process, and remedy regardless of status.

These four do not have one holder, one trigger, or one score. Each receives its
own duty, evidence, failure, continuity, remedy, audit, and correction path.

**No new taxonomy class is created.** The ratified taxonomy already places a
separately scoped defence or security mandate in Class 6 and keeps emergency as
an overlay across classes rather than a branch, a right, or an exception. Class
10 remains the last class. Every direct legal effect in this settlement is split
across the existing ten classes by its effect, never gathered into one
`security`, `emergency`, `threat`, `enemy`, `border`, or `defence` conclusion.

### What this ruling refuses at the outset

- **A derogation clause.** The alternative — enumerated suspendable rights with
  a protected core — is the conventional design and is refused. A suspension
  power is an instrument, and this project's method is to refuse the dangerous
  instrument rather than to bound it.
- **A standing emergency authority** in the Executive Council, the Civic
  President, or any protective body. Three prior rulings already forbid it and
  this one does not create an exception.
- **A security carve-out from personhood or the floor.** "Enemy", "combatant",
  "non-citizen", "security detainee", and "unlawful entrant" are precisely the
  device Chapter 7 refuses as a mechanism. Nothing here creates a status a
  person can be moved into and thereby lose the floor.
- **A risk, threat, loyalty, dangerousness, clearance, or watchlist
  predicate.** The 2026-08-02 temporary-assessment exclusion already refuses
  these and prices the refusal as an exile rather than an oversight. This
  ruling extends that exclusion by name; it does not reopen it.
- **An inherent or residual external power.** The common tier holds only
  enumerated competences. Section 19 supplies the express addition.

## 2. Separated protective functions

Six protective functions are constitutionally distinct Class 6 mandates. Each
needs its own democratic source, enumerated mandate, trigger, scope,
conflict and recusal rule, non-delegable limit, review, appeal, end condition,
and temporal status.

| Function | Constitutional job | May not do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public protection / policing | Prevent and respond to harm; investigate; protect people at immediate risk. | Charge, adjudicate, hold long-term custody, conduct security intelligence, or audit its own use of force. |
| Prosecution | Decide independently whether to charge, and on what evidence. | Investigate the case it charges as its own inquiry, adjudicate it, or execute the result. |
| Adjudication | Decide guilt and consequence. | Investigate, prosecute, execute, or finally review its own case. |
| Custodial execution | Hold a person a court has lawfully ordered held. | Decide the ground, extend the authority, or supply its own review. |
| External defence | Defend against an external armed attack under civilian command. | Hold any domestic policing, surveillance, detention, arrest, search, or interrogation mandate. |
| Security intelligence | Collect narrowly authorised information about a specific serious external or organised threat. | Arrest, search, detain, charge, adjudicate, hold custody, or write to the canonical consequential person record. |

The general separation rule already ratified applies without modification: no
actor may assert decisive case facts, decide their consequence, execute the
result, audit itself, and supply the final remedy in the same case.

**Legal defence and victim protection are not owned here.** They belong to the
non-carceral justice interface, which already carries "investigation/policing,
prosecution, defence, adjudication, execution and review" together with victim
and survivor protection, restitution, reparation, and restorative options. This
ruling names the six protective mandates and cross-references that interface; it
does not restate, narrow, or replace it. A reader must not conclude from this
section that the separation architecture is complete without the accused's side
and the injured person's side, both of which live there.

## 3. Civilian command and the anti-fusion wall

Every protective function is under civilian, democratically sourced command.

No serving member of the armed forces or of security intelligence may hold a
legislative seat, an Executive Council seat, judicial office, an electoral or
audit office, or any oversight mandate over their own function. No secondment,
joint command, shared chain of authority, unified staffing, or reserve status
may reconstitute a fused police-military-intelligence force out of separated
mandates. A body's name does not decide its classification; its actual powers do.

The armed forces may assist civil authority — disaster response, search and
rescue, engineering, transport, medical support — only where that assistance is
unarmed, individually authorised, publicly recorded, time-bounded, and reviewed.
**Assistance carries no arrest, search, seizure, detention, interrogation,
crowd-control, or surveillance power.** A soldier assisting in a flood is not a
police officer and acquires no coercive authority by proximity.

There is no military jurisdiction over civilians, in any condition, at any time.
Internal service discipline may exist for serving members and may not displace
ordinary courts for any offence against a civilian.

No secret law, secret court, secret detention site, secret budget line beyond a
narrowly scoped classified annex read in full by an independent oversight body,
and no secret rule of engagement that a court may not examine.

## 4. The policing mandate and its limits

Policing exists to prevent and respond to harm to people, not to maintain order
as such. A general order-maintenance power is refused: it is the clause that
swallows liberty, and it has no evidential trigger.

Policing carries a **presumption against coercion**. The ordinary policing act
is non-coercive — presence, information, assistance, mediation, referral. Every
coercive act is an exception that must be separately grounded, separately
recorded, and separately reviewable.

An officer exercising any power over a person must identify themselves and the
authority relied on, and give reasons the person can understand, in an
accessible form and language, at the time or as soon as safety permits. Refusal
or inability to do so is itself a reviewable failure and cannot be cured by the
outcome.

Policing may not be conditioned on, and may not collect for another purpose, a
person's immigration status, floor entitlements, care records, learning records,
or membership of a collective.

## 5. Arrest, detention before trial, search, and seizure

These are the coercive instruments a policing mandate necessarily creates, and
the present constitution names none of them. Book 1 must supply each with a
complete contract before any policing rule family lands.

**Arrest** requires an individualised, articulable, recorded ground; a
contemporaneous statement of reasons; notification of a person of the arrested
person's choosing; immediate access to legal advice and, where needed, an
interpreter and an accessibility accommodation; and a record naming its author.

**Detention before trial** requires prompt, automatic, independent judicial
review that the detained person does not have to request, and repeated review
thereafter. Liberty is the default and detention is the exception, justified
individually and never by category, status, nationality, or the seriousness of
an allegation alone. A person the state holds retains the complete floor, voice,
franchise, candidacy, and every liberty not necessarily incident to the lawful
ground of holding.

**Search and seizure** of a person, home, communications, or belongings requires
prior independent authorisation on individualised grounds, save for a narrowly
defined immediate-danger exception that is reported and reviewed after the fact.
A seizure creates a duty to inventory, preserve, account for, and return.

**No incommunicado detention, no unrecorded detention, no undisclosed place of
detention, and no detention whose existence a court may not verify.** The record
of who is held, by whom, and on what ground must be reachable by an independent
body and by the person's chosen representative.

## 6. Permissible force

Force is lawful only where it is strictly necessary for a legitimate protective
purpose, where no reasonably available less harmful means would serve, and where
the harm it does is not disproportionate to the harm it prevents. It must be the
minimum sufficient, must stop the moment its purpose is achieved or becomes
unattainable, and must be preceded by warning wherever warning is feasible.

Every use of force creates a duty to render or obtain immediate medical aid,
including to the person against whom force was used.

The evidential burden for the lawfulness of force is on the public actor, not on
the person harmed. A person who cannot account for what happened to them —
because they were unconscious, detained, a child, disabled, deceased, or
excluded from the record — does not thereby lose the claim.

**Lethal force is lawful only where it is strictly unavoidable to protect life.**
Never to protect property, never to prevent escape alone, never to disperse an
assembly, never to enforce an order, and never as punishment.

Every death, life-threatening injury, and credible allegation of torture or
disappearance involving a protective actor requires prompt, independent,
effective investigation by a body other than the deploying body, with the
family's participation, public findings consistent with privacy, and a remedy
route. Individual accountability is personal: rank, orders, or institutional
attribution does not absorb it.

**Superior orders is no defence.** A manifestly unlawful order must be refused,
refusal is protected against every professional and legal consequence, and
command responsibility attaches to those who ordered, knew, or should have known
and failed to prevent or punish.

### 6a. What force may never do

These are refused categorically — not balanced, not proportionate at any
magnitude, not available under any declaration. Section 18 places them inside
the unamendable corridor.

- torture, and cruel, inhuman, or degrading treatment or punishment;
- enforced disappearance, and secret or unacknowledged detention;
- extrajudicial, arbitrary, or summary killing;
- collective punishment, and reprisal against a family, community, or group for
  the act of another;
- indefinite detention without charge or without independent review;
- coerced confession, and any use of a statement obtained by prohibited means;
- use of a person as a shield, and deliberate attack on people not taking part
  in hostilities;
- starvation, denial of the material floor, denial of care, or destruction of
  the means of survival, as a weapon, a sanction, an inducement, or a
  bargaining instrument;
- medical, scientific, or behavioural experimentation without free consent;
- weapons that cannot discriminate, weapons causing superfluous injury, and
  autonomous systems that select and engage a human target without meaningful
  human control; and
- aggressive war.

## 7. Protective restriction is not punishment

Some protective powers restrict a person who has been convicted of nothing: a
communicable-disease quarantine, a protective exclusion order, a border hold, a
detention pending an expulsion decision. Book 1 must be exact about what these
are, because the design's central coercion claim depends on it.

**Every such restriction is a separate instrument with a separate trigger,
separate evidence, separate review, and a separate temporal contract.** It is
never punishment, and it must not:

- attach a punitive consequence of any kind;
- feed severity, placement, or any consequence of conviction;
- be recorded as, presented as, or reasoned from as a conviction;
- rely on a risk, dangerousness, threat, or character assessment — the
  temporary-assessment exclusion applies in full; or
- read, or be read by, the recognition record.

The design's claim that conviction takes movement and nothing else is a claim
about **conviction and punishment**, and it remains true. What this section adds
is not a second punishment; it is an acknowledgement that the republic holds
more than one instrument capable of restricting movement, each of which must be
independently justified and none of which may borrow a consequence from another.

### 7a. The deprivation stays a leaf; the instrument need not

The structural protection worth preserving is that **the capability a coercive
instrument removes is read by no rule** — nothing depends on a person's freedom
of movement, so removing it cascades into nothing. That is a property of the
deprivation, not of the instrument: the existing confinement conclusion is read
by several rules, and an "every coercive instrument is a leaf" invariant would
be false of the instrument the constitution already has.

Formal implementation must therefore keep every removed capability on the
no-reader list and add each new one to it in the same change, while permitting
the instrument itself to be read where the design requires it.

### 7b. Two protections that must travel with any physical holding

The constitution today derives shelter and recorded speech for a confined person
and for nobody else, and the settled 2026-07-31 ruling holds that the state
houses whom it confines. That protection is currently attached to one
instrument.

**Anyone the state physically holds, by any instrument, is owed shelter and a
recorded voice**, on the same reasoning: a society that holds a person and does
not house them has not confined them, it has abandoned them, and a society that
holds a person whose words reach no record can hold them silently. Implementing
a new holding instrument without extending both protections in the same change
is refused.

This is a floor **non-reduction** rule and an affirmative duty; it is not a
claim that protective restriction improves anyone's position, and it does not
make holding a route to the floor.

## 8. Security intelligence, surveillance, and the assessment firewall

Security intelligence is the narrowest protective mandate and carries the
heaviest controls, because its product is secret and its subject usually cannot
contest what they do not know.

**Mass, bulk, suspicionless, or population-scale collection is refused.** So is
the retention of material collected about people who were never individually
suspected, and the acquisition by purchase, partnership, or foreign exchange of
material the republic could not lawfully collect itself.

A covert measure requires prior independent judicial authorisation on
individualised, evidenced suspicion of a specific serious harm; the least
intrusive effective means; a defined subject, scope, and duration; a fresh
authorisation for each renewal; and **notification of the subject once
notification no longer defeats the lawful purpose**, with an effective route to
challenge and remedy after the fact. An authorisation that can never be
disclosed to anyone is refused.

**The assessment firewall applies in full.** No intelligence product,
assessment, risk score, threat rating, loyalty grading, dangerousness flag,
clearance level, or watchlist entry may enter the canonical consequential person
record, and none may condition standing, the material floor, personhood,
franchise, candidacy, liberty, remedy, or any allocation. A protective body may
act operationally on information; it may never convert information into a fact
about a person that the record carries forward.

No security clearance or vetting result may gate the floor, and it may gate
employment only for a specifically identified function where the requirement
meets the ratified function-specific necessity test, with reasons, evidence
disclosure so far as possible, an independent challenge, and a remedy.

Secret evidence may never be the sole or decisive basis for a conclusion
affecting a person's standing, floor, liberty, or remedy. Where material cannot
be disclosed, the consequence does not follow.

Oversight is a separate, independently appointed body with its own budget and
staff, unrestricted access including to classified material, the power to
publish findings consistent with privacy, and a duty to report. A protected
disclosure route exists for anyone inside a protective function who reports
unlawfulness, and protection does not depend on using an internal channel first
where that channel is implicated.

## 9. The emergency overlay: no derogation

An emergency declaration confers exactly the following and nothing else:

1. **procedural acceleration** — shortened steps, emergency sittings, remote
   participation, temporary substitution of a decision-maker, provided every
   substantive protection, evidence rule, and appeal survives;
2. **resource redirection** — reallocation of public capacity, facilities, and
   personnel to the named hazard;
3. **requisition with compensation** — temporary use or acquisition of property
   necessary to the response, with reasons, record, return or compensation, and
   challenge; and
4. **restriction against the named hazard** — a temporary, reviewable
   restriction on movement, assembly, or activity, each separately justified
   against that hazard and each subject to the ordinary limits in this ruling.

It confers **no** power to suspend a right, to legislate by decree, to dissolve
or prevent the sitting of the Assembly, to close or bypass the courts, to
postpone or cancel an election, to extend any mandate, to alter the franchise,
to detain without the ordinary grounds and review, to derogate from any
categorical refusal in section 6a, or to lower the material floor.

**A managed departure is a failure, not a success.** Where a response cannot
meet an existing duty, the shortfall is recorded as a breach, mitigated, and
repaired; it is never renamed as compliance, and the ecological and animal
baselines apply unchanged except through the contract in this section, with
restoration owed afterwards.

### 9a. The three powers the economic ruling reserved

- **Rationing** is not a new power. Where a genuine physical shortage exists, it
  is an instance of the ratified physical-scarcity allocation contract and runs
  through it unchanged: an authenticated, contestable, resource-specific
  finding; alternatives, reserves, substitution and coordination considered
  first; each constitutional minimum preserved wherever supply permits; **no
  redefinition of a reduced ration as the minimum**; every unmet portion
  recorded as failure; the forbidden priority keys intact; and rotation or
  lottery only among materially equal claims. A declaration does not create a
  shortage, lower the evidential threshold for finding one, or license
  allocation on any key that contract forbids.
- **Compulsory continuity** is permitted only in the narrow, already ratified
  form: it binds a provider or bargaining party to maintain a service protecting
  life, health, safety, or the floor. It may not conscript a named worker,
  criminalise an individual's refusal to work, or withdraw anyone's floor.
- **Price control** is ordinary economic law, available to the Assembly at any
  time inside the ratified economic settlement. It is **not** an emergency power
  and no declaration is needed to reach it; a declaration therefore adds
  nothing here and confers no unilateral executive pricing authority.

### 9b. Declaration, renewal, and end

A declaration must name the hazard, the territory, the affected people, and each
power relied on, and must justify each power separately against that hazard. The
narrowest sufficient scope is a condition of validity, not an aspiration.

It requires published reasons and the evidence relied on; cross-branch
authorisation, so that no body declares and exercises alone; immediate
notification to the Assembly and to the Constitutional Court; and a fresh
source-bound temporal contract **for the declaration and separately for each
renewal**, with each measure rejoining the exact declaration version it relies
on rather than a compact status tag.

Judicial review reaches both the existence of the emergency and each individual
measure, is available to any affected person and to the rights advocate, and is
not deferred until the emergency ends.

The end restores every suspended-in-fact arrangement, and triggers a mandatory
public post-hoc audit, an accounting of every requisition and restriction, and a
remedy route for harms caused by lawful as well as unlawful measures.

**Fail-safe polarity.** Current restrictive authority is a positive premise:
absent current, authenticated, source-bound renewal evidence in a fresh
evaluation, the restrictive power has no current legal effect. Missing,
withheld, stale, disputed, or corrected evidence never extends a restrictive
power and never cuts off a claimant's standing, floor, liberty, appeal, or
remedy. The floor and essential-service continuity survive the lapse of the
managing authority.

**What that polarity does not buy, stated plainly.** It defeats a renewal that
was never made. It does not defeat a **frozen or replayed** record, and in an
emergency the party who gains from freezing the record is the party the contract
exists to constrain. Distinguishing a live renewal from a frozen one requires
independently witnessed sources that Book 1 can specify and only Book 2 can
operate. Nor can Book 1 make an emergency end: it can withhold current legal
effect from an unrenewed power, and it cannot cause anyone to act.

### 9c. The alternate authorising route, and its price

A cross-branch authorisation requirement hands whoever can prevent the
authorising body from convening a veto over every lawful response — which is the
hardest case this section faces, and the one an emergency power is most often
justified by.

The settlement is the route already ratified for the Future Conditions Guardian,
applied here: a **predeclared alternate authorising route** and a **predeclared
independent substitute reviewer**, both named in advance, both bound by every
limit in this section, and both subject to immediate ratification by the
ordinary body the moment it can act. Neither absence becomes approval; neither
becomes an indefinite hold. An alternate authorisation that the ordinary body
does not ratify at its first opportunity ends.

The price is stated rather than hidden: a predeclared alternate is itself a route
that can be captured, and naming it in advance is the only protection this design
offers against that. A constitution that refuses the alternate has chosen a
different failure — a lawful response that cannot begin — and this ruling judges
that failure worse.

## 10. Conscientious objection and the duty to refuse

Compulsory service is neither required nor prohibited by the constitution;
democratic law may create it or not, inside every limit here.

Where any compulsory service exists, the right to object to a lethal or armed
role is **unconditional**. It requires no proof of belief, no tribunal of
sincerity, and no religious or organisational membership. It carries a genuinely
equivalent, non-punitive civilian alternative — not longer, not harsher, not
stigmatising — and no loss of the material floor, standing, franchise,
candidacy, employment eligibility, education, or any civil right. No one may be
punished twice for the same continuing objection.

Objection is available to a serving member, including to a specific operation,
weapon, or order, and the ratified bars on conscripted labour and on
imprisonment for debt or for refusal to work apply without modification.

A duty runs the other way too: a member of any protective function must refuse a
manifestly unlawful order, and that refusal is protected.

## 11. Jurisdiction-wide standing and the enforcement firewall

Every person within the republic's jurisdiction or effective control is **owed**
standing, the material floor, due process, and an effective remedy, regardless of
citizenship, nationality, immigration classification, documentation, manner of
entry, or the absence of any record.

This is written as a duty and a limit on power, not as a description of the
record. A person who was never entered is not recorded as missing; the record
cannot notice the absence of someone it never held. The obligation therefore
reads: **no public power may condition help, protection, or process on a record
entry, and none may treat the absence of an entry as a finding.** Serve first;
reconcile the record afterwards; "no record found" reads as pending, never as
denied.

**The enforcement firewall.** Access to food, water, shelter, health and care,
learning, communication, public space, a court, or the reporting of a crime or
of violence may never be conditioned on immigration or security status, may
never **enrol** a person into an enforcement record as the price of the service,
may never collect that status for an enforcement purpose, and may never transmit
what it holds to an enforcement function. Enrolment is named deliberately: it is
the operation the record actually performs, and a firewall that governs only
collection and transmission leaves the door the design most needs shut.

## 12. Borders, entry, asylum, expulsion, and statelessness

Democratic law chooses who may enter and settle. It exercises that choice inside
the following limits.

**Non-refoulement is absolute.** No person may be returned, removed,
transferred, handed over, or refused entry at a frontier where that would expose
them to persecution, torture, cruel or inhuman treatment, enforced
disappearance, arbitrary deprivation of life, or any act refused in section 6a —
directly or through a chain of onward transfers. Section 18 places this inside
the unamendable corridor.

**Asylum is a right to a fair determination**, with accessible information, an
advocate, an interpreter, a hearing, reasons, and an effective appeal with
suspensive effect. It is not a discretionary favour and its refusal must be
individually reasoned.

**Expulsion requires an individual decision**, reasons, and an effective appeal
with suspensive effect. **Collective expulsion is refused** and sits inside the
corridor. A person's connection to the republic, family life, length of
residence, and the position of any child affected are material to every removal
decision.

**No child is detained for an immigration purpose.** Adult immigration detention
is lawful only on individualised necessity grounds, with prior judicial
authorisation, a maximum beyond which it does not continue, periodic independent
review, and a genuinely available non-custodial alternative that must be shown
to be insufficient before detention. Detention for administrative convenience,
deterrence, or as a signal to others is refused.

**Statelessness must not be created or perpetuated.** A child born in the
republic who would otherwise be stateless acquires nationality. Deprivation of
nationality that would leave a person stateless is refused, and deprivation is
never available as a punishment or a security measure.

**Evasion is refused**, on the principle the ecological settlement already
supplies: pushbacks, interception designed to prevent a claim from arising,
externalised processing arranged so that the duty attaches to nobody, transfer
to a state that will do what the republic may not, and jurisdiction-shopping
through agreements, contractors, or funding do not relieve the republic of a
duty it would otherwise owe. Effective control, not formal territory, is the
test.

**The scarcity interface.** A genuine physical shortage is governed by the
ratified physical-scarcity contract, which indexes its finding to a named
population. **That population includes every person within the republic's
jurisdiction or effective control at the time of the finding**; arrivals cannot
be defined out of the population and thereby out of the floor. Nationality,
citizenship, immigration status, documentation, manner of entry, and length of
presence join the forbidden priority keys and may never order allocation of a
scarce floor essential.

The former resident's right to return, already ratified, is untouched: this
ruling supplies accessible evidence and border-operation limits for it, not its
existence, and it creates no nonresident ballot.

## 13. Extradition, mutual legal assistance, and transfer of a person

This is the reserved international-enforcement authority, and it is the standard
route around every protection in section 12.

Surrender, extradition, transfer, or handover of any person to another state,
international body, or foreign force requires an individual judicial decision,
disclosed reasons, and an effective appeal with suspensive effect. It is
**barred** where surrender would breach non-refoulement, expose the person to
any act refused in section 6a, or subject them to a proceeding lacking the
protections this constitution requires. A diplomatic assurance does not displace
the bar; it is evidence, and weak evidence, about what another state will do.

No person may be surrendered for an act that is not an offence here, for a
political characterisation of conduct, or where the real purpose is punishment
for identity, belief, association, or expression.

Mutual legal assistance may not be used to obtain, launder, or receive material
the republic could not lawfully obtain itself, and may not transmit material
that would enable an act refused in section 6a.

Handing a person over is a coercive act by this republic, and every limit in
sections 5 and 6 attaches to it.

## 14. Defence: force structure and civilian command

A standing defence force is permitted, not required. Its constitutional mandate
is external only.

The Assembly holds the ceiling on its size, armament, and organisation; its
funding is an ordinary legislative appropriation subject to full audit access;
and no part of it is funded, armed, or maintained outside that source. It has no
jurisdiction over civilians and no domestic mandate, subject only to section 3's
unarmed, individually authorised civil assistance.

**Private coercion.** Coercive public force may not be delegated to a private
actor. Mercenary forces, paramilitary organisations, and private armed groups
are refused, and no private body may hold arrest, detention, search,
interrogation, crowd-control, or lethal-force authority. Where private security
performs a lawful protective service, it holds no more authority than any person
holds, is bound by the equality settlement's public-facing private duties, and
never absorbs a public duty or a public immunity.

**Civilian weapons** are a matter for democratic law inside the ordinary limits:
regulation must be evidenced, non-discriminatory, proportionate, and reviewable.
This constitution creates no individual right to bear arms and no right of the
state to disarm a population as such.

## 15. Authorisation of force abroad, war, and peace

The republic may use armed force outside its territory only:

1. in defence against an actual or imminent armed attack; or
2. under a lawful collective-security or humanitarian-protection mandate.

Either requires **prior Assembly authorisation**, with published reasons naming
the objective, legal basis, scope, geography, means, duration, and reporting
obligations. An immediate defensive response to an actual attack may begin
without prior authorisation and must be submitted for ratification without
delay; absent ratification it ends. That is a fail-safe rule of the same shape as
section 9b: the authority is a positive premise, and its absence ends the power
rather than extending it.

**Aggressive war is refused** and sits inside the corridor. So does secret war:
no armed force, covert action, targeting programme, or foreign deployment may
exist outside a published legal basis and an authorisation a court and the
Assembly can examine.

**A cyber or infrastructure attack** may constitute an armed attack where its
scale and effects are equivalent to a kinetic one. Attribution must be
evidenced, independently assessable, and stated; a response follows the same
authorisation route and the same limits, and disruption of civilian life-critical
infrastructure is itself refused under section 6a's prohibition on attacks
directed at people not taking part in hostilities.

**Arms transfers** require an evidenced test that the transferred capability
will not be used for an act refused in section 6a, with independent review,
public reporting so far as security permits, and a duty to suspend on credible
evidence of misuse.

**Humanitarian duties are non-negotiable.** Rescue where life is at risk, care
for the wounded, humane treatment and identification of anyone detained,
humanitarian access, and protection of civilians attach in every condition and
are not conditioned on reciprocity, status, cooperation with an immigration or
security function, or the lawfulness of anyone's presence.

**Children in armed conflict.** No person below the general age of legal
adulthood may be recruited, enlisted, conscripted, or used in hostilities, by
the republic or by any actor it supports, supplies, or funds. A child associated
with an armed force is treated as a person owed protection and reintegration,
never as an enemy.

## 16. Corruption and abuse of office in a protective function

Abuse of a protective office is a distinct constitutional concern because the
victim usually cannot reach an ordinary remedy.

Every protective function requires an independent integrity route that does not
run through the abuser's own chain of command; a duty to record and preserve
evidence of its own coercive acts, with destruction, falsification, or
non-recording treated as a substantive failure and not a procedural one;
protection for anyone who reports; and a remedy that reaches the person harmed
rather than only disciplining the institution.

No immunity, indemnity, classification, statute of repose, amnesty, or pardon
may extinguish accountability for an act refused in section 6a. Historical
answerability survives every removal, reorganisation, and renaming of a body.

## 17. Treaties, external trade, sanctions, and no evasion

Treaties are negotiated by the executive and require Assembly ratification.
Where a treaty touches regional competence or a boundary, it additionally
requires Regions Council consent and the consent of each directly affected
region, on the same terms as the ratified amendment route. Provisional
application that pre-empts ratification is refused, and withdrawal follows the
same route as entry.

**No treaty, trade agreement, investment protection, arbitration clause, or
other international obligation may lower universal standing, the material floor,
substantive equality, due process, core liberties, the commons ceiling, the
animal core, or any protection this ruling places in the non-derogable core, or
place a dispute about them beyond constitutional review.**

**How strong that claim actually is, stated honestly.** It is a destination, not
a currently available interface. The existing amendment machinery classifies a
proposer-supplied label and does not bind that label to the candidate source's
actual effect; the amendment-semantics audit has executed that gap, showing that
a truthfully labelled harmless proposal and a concealed one-token floor deletion
are not distinguished by the register. A treaty-supremacy limit would inherit
exactly that defect. Book 1 states the limit as a rule addressed to
ratifiers, reviewers, and courts, and must not claim that any current or
foreseeable rule reads a treaty text, verifies a declared characterisation of
it, or detects a concealed lowering effect.

**No evasion.** No public or private actor may achieve through trade,
procurement, investment, contracting, an affiliate, a supply chain, a flag of
convenience, an arbitration forum, an exported enforcement arrangement, or
jurisdiction-shopping what this constitution refuses at home. Sanctions and
trade measures are ordinary law bounded by the corridor and may not be designed
to deprive a population of the material floor or of humanitarian access.

## 18. The non-derogable core and the unamendable corridor

These are two related lists that are not identical, and the difference matters.
The **corridor** limits what a majority may **amend**. **Non-derogability**
limits what an emergency may **touch**. Because this ruling creates no
derogation power at all, every corridor member is also non-derogable; the
converse does not hold, and some protections below are stated as non-derogable
without being placed in the corridor.

**Added to the unamendable corridor by this ruling, expressly:**

- every categorical refusal in section 6a;
- non-refoulement, and the prohibition of collective expulsion;
- prompt independent judicial review of detention;
- the right to an effective remedy itself; and
- the continued existence and capacity to sit of the People's Assembly and the
  Constitutional Court.

The corridor's existing members — universal standing, substantive equality and
non-discrimination, the material floor, due process, core liberties, protected
commons, direct non-human-animal protected-subject status, and the prohibitions
on severe avoidable animal suffering and dispensable killing — are unchanged and
are not narrowed by anything here.

**Non-derogable without a corridor claim:** the ordinary grounds and review for
arrest and detention; the identification-and-reasons duty; access to legal
advice and to a court; the enforcement firewall; the accessible-participation
and public-reason duties; and the ordinary franchise, which no declaration
suspends or postpones.

Detailed protective standards below that core remain democratically revisable
inside it.

## 19. External status, lawful exit, and federal allocation

### 19a. Before, during, and after a lawful exit

The internal secession path is settled and untouched: an affected-region
referendum may open negotiations; exit requires federal agreement,
constitutional-rights review, settlement, and final ratification by the affected
population; unilateral secession has no legal effect. This ruling owns only the
external and coercive consequences.

**The settlement's mandatory contents are a superset of what is already
ratified.** The existing list is quoted so that nothing is lost by omission:
settlement of **"borders, assets, debts, commons, floors, and minority
protections"**. This ruling supplements it with: the continuing protection of
people who lose membership, including their floor, standing, and remedy during
and after transition; nationality and statelessness prevention; the position of
residents who do not move; cross-border family, kin, and cultural relations;
defence and security arrangements; treaty succession; and the disposition of
records affecting individual rights. It creates no unlimited settlement power
and does not displace any existing content.

**Indigenous collective consent is a gate, not a consideration.** Where an exit
would transfer, extinguish, or irreversibly impair collective land or resource
title, transfer sovereignty over collective lands, or place a sacred site under
a different constitutional order, the ratified free, prior, and informed
**actual collective consent** requirement applies. Listing Indigenous protection
among settlement contents would understate a gate the family and plurality
ruling already closed.

**No coercive instrument stands behind territorial integrity.** Because the
defence force's mandate is external only, the republic holds **no military
instrument against a unilateral exit**. The state-form ruling said unilateral
secession has no legal effect; it said nothing about permissible response, and
this ruling decides that in the open rather than letting it emerge from a
mandate's wording. The available responses are legal invalidity, ordinary
policing under section 6's imminent-threat-to-life standard, and negotiation.
The republic may not weaponise defence, trade, border, payment, or service levers
against a region seeking or completing a lawful exit, and holds no defence
mandate over a territory that has lawfully exited.

**Recognition by other states is external and unclaimable.** Book 1 cannot make
another state recognise, cooperate, admit, readmit, or honour anything. Every
claim that depends on it is a named external assumption with an owner, an
evidence status, and a stated consequence if it fails.

### 19b. Federal competence

This decision deliberately supplements the state-form decision's enumerated
common competences. It creates **no** general security, foreign-affairs,
policing, or emergency power. In addition to its already ratified
responsibilities, the common tier owns:

- external representation, treaties, treaty succession, and international
  obligations;
- external defence, the armed forces, and the authorisation of force abroad;
- borders, entry, asylum determination, expulsion, extradition, and mutual
  legal assistance;
- external trade measures, sanctions, and arms-transfer control;
- security intelligence directed at serious external or organised threats, and
  its oversight;
- common minimum standards for policing, permissible force, arrest, detention,
  search, evidence, and oversight, which no tier may lower; and
- coordination of cross-regional and cross-border hazards and emergencies.

Regional and local tiers retain ordinary policing, civil protection, disaster
preparedness and response, and all residual competence under subsidiarity, and
may enact stronger compatible protection. No tier may lower the common floor,
the corridor, or the standards above; displacement of subnational law reaches
only as far as the reason establishes.

## 20. Oversight, review, and remedy — priced honestly

Every protective function requires an independent oversight body with its own
appointment under the ratified anti-capture contract, its own budget and staff,
full access, publication power, and a defined action duty. Initiation routes are
plural: the affected person, a chosen supporter, a qualified association, and
the rights advocate or ombud may each initiate independently, and none requires
another to be present. Ordinary courts give case-specific relief; the
Constitutional Court alone gives final general invalidation.

Remedies are individual and systemic: relief for the person, and re-audit,
redesign, recurrence monitoring, and non-repetition measures for the pattern. No
remedy may take another person's standing, floor, core liberty, due process,
political equality, or protected collective right.

**Historical endpoint at ratification; superseded by FS-CVF-016.** When this
decision was ratified, the accountability chain marked a breach, produced a
duty, and stopped at an unread conclusion. The current obligations family now
assigns exact readers, action duties, positive non-response, alternates,
continuity, individual remedy, systemic correction, affected-case re-audit,
and recurrence verification. This ruling still does not claim that abuse of
force or emergency power *is corrected*: a legal route proves no receipt,
action, completed remedy, recurrence control, or institutional liveness.

## 21. Evidence, records, and failure polarity

Every protective record — arrest, detention, search, seizure, use of force,
surveillance authorisation, declaration, renewal, border decision, expulsion,
transfer, deployment — names its authorised writer, permitted basis, visibility
and privacy boundary, independent reader, reasons, challenge route, correction,
retention and deletion control, carry, end condition, alternate route,
continuity, remedy, audit, and failure polarity.

Absence of a record is never proof that a coercive act did not happen, and
never a defence for the actor who should have written it. Non-recording,
destruction, and falsification are substantive failures attributable to the
power, not evidential misfortunes borne by the person.

Protective and intelligence records do not enter the canonical consequential
person record. A person's presence in a protective record is not a fact about
them that any other rule may read.

Aggregate patterns run through the ratified equality diagnostics: they may
create a rebuttable presumption and open systemic review, and they may never
produce an adverse individual finding, reverse a criminal burden, prove guilt,
or authorise punishment.

## 23. Formal migration and prose impact

### 23a. Predicate census — retain, replace, retire

Every name below needs an explicit card before any protective rule family lands.

- **`capture`** means *documented*, not *arrested*, and the difference is not
  cosmetic. Its two consumers today are the two-signer voiding rule, whose head
  voids a person's credibility, and the examiner-reward rule, whose head mints
  recognition. An arrest reading would therefore derive credibility-voiding and
  recognition from a police detention. Arrest needs its own name; `capture` may
  not be reused for it.
- **`severe`** is one-place and collapses to the person rather than the act. It
  may not become a threat, dangerousness, or security grading, and no protective
  restriction may read it.
- **`permits`** and **`authority`** are the existing relief and standing
  fixtures. Neither is an emergency, deployment, surveillance, or border
  authorisation, and neither may be widened to carry one.
- **`free`** is not a war, emergency, or border conclusion, and no authority
  lapse may manufacture it.
- **`travel`** is the existing movement conclusion with a single blocker today.
  Every new movement restriction adds a blocker and must be pinned as such; the
  conclusion must remain read by no rule.
- **`err` / `obliged`** were the unread audit endpoint when this decision was
  ratified. FS-CVF-016 now routes every current finding kind through an exact
  typed reader/action duty; that legal route still proves no actor acts.
- **`prisoner`** is the existing confinement conclusion, read by several rules.
  It is not a template for a protective restriction and must not silently
  acquire non-punitive holding.
- **`public`**, **`defend`**, **`show`**, and **`judge`** are narrow existing
  fixtures and may not stand in for a protective function, a legal defence, a
  disclosure duty, or an adjudication mandate.

### 23b. The floor firewall does not reach a new instrument

The compile-time refusal that stops a rule from punishing a person for lacking a
floor right works because the floor predicates sit downstream of the existing
confinement conclusion, so a rule taking a floor absence into that cone closes a
negative cycle. The refusal reaches that one conclusion and no other, as the
source itself discloses; the disclosed control shows that a rule reading the
absence of a **non-floor** fact into it loads without error.

**Therefore every new coercive instrument sits outside the firewall unless it is
deliberately put inside.** A rule restricting a person for lacking food, care,
shelter, learning, company, or belief would load. Implementation must:

- give every new coercive instrument its own rule placing it upstream of
  personhood in the same change that creates it, so the same negative cycle
  closes; and
- ship a refusal pin for **each** floor predicate against **each** new
  instrument — not one representative sample — plus a re-measurement of the
  existing refusals, because a new edge can change what the stratifier accepts.

Article 1's firewall, the closed record vocabulary, the derived-only guards, and
the verifier guards remain structural walls with distinct mechanisms and keep
their own enforcement and test obligations.

### 23c. Prose that must be re-audited before any rule family lands

This ruling changes no prose. It records four places that a protective rule
family would falsify if landed without them, and makes their audit a condition
of that landing.

1. **The single-deprivation claim** — the chapter carrying it, its Part V
   verdict, and the hand-authored chapter list in the spine document, which
   states the same claim in a region no generator and no prose gate covers. The
   claim is scoped to conviction and punishment and survives; the sentences must
   still be checked one by one against section 7, and the counted-claim
   allowlist reviewed in the same change.
2. **The two confinement rule-statements** — shelter and recorded speech deriving
   for a confined person and for nobody else. Section 7b requires both to extend
   to anyone the state physically holds; both sentences change when it does.
3. **The absent-justifications sentence** — it makes two claims, a vocabulary
   claim and a reachability claim. The vocabulary claim is false the moment
   justification vocabulary is admitted, regardless of reachability, and must be
   rewritten in the same change; the reachability claim is preserved by section
   6's scoping of the force test to the public actor's own accountability, and
   that asymmetry is a priced choice, not an oversight.
4. **The historical accountability endpoint** — at ratification, nothing read
   the duty the audit produced. Section 20 now records its supersession by
   FS-CVF-016 while preserving the external action and remedy-liveness limit.

Also recorded so it is not assumed: the counted-claim gate does not match small
cardinals or "exactly *n*". Where a protective family introduces a second or
third instrument, the counting discipline is human until that gate is extended,
and no ruling or chapter may cite it as the guard for this domain.

### 23d. Temporal contracts

Each of the following needs its **own** source-bound contract and its own
adversarial cases: the declaration, each renewal, each individual measure,
deployment, surveillance authorisation, arrest, pre-trial detention, protective
restriction, border hold, expulsion stay, transfer, treaty window, and exit
settlement. The case-bound custody contract cannot be reused or borrowed by
implication.

The polarity is the **same** as the existing custody gate — current authority is
a positive premise, and its absence withholds the restrictive conclusion. It is
not an inverse or a novel shape, and describing it as one invites exactly the
absence-derives-cessation rule this design refuses. Cessation is either a
positive recorded act or a fresh-evaluation claim over the supplied envelope; it
is never derived from silence.

## 25. Evidence and limits

The comparative material informing this settlement — constitutional emergency
clauses and their recorded failure modes, non-refoulement and
collective-expulsion jurisprudence, civilian-control doctrines, use-of-force
standards, and oversight designs for intelligence functions — **informs** the
choice and does not compel it. This ruling cites no figure and makes no
empirical claim about how any of it performed.

What this decision does **not** establish:

- that the republic can defend itself, control a border, respond to a disaster,
  or investigate a crime — those are capability claims and belong to Book 2;
- that another state will recognise, cooperate, readmit, or comply;
- that any record of an authorisation, renewal, or end is authentic, complete,
  timely, or unfrozen;
- that an oversight body will look, or that anyone will act on what it finds;
- that a proportionality standard is derivable here — the constitution has no
  ordering relation and no arithmetic, so Book 1 supplies categorical refusals
  and enumerated permitted measures, and proportionality is a standard addressed
  to an adjudicator; or
- that refusing a derogation clause prevents an abusive restriction. It removes
  one instrument. A restriction dressed as an ordinary measure remains possible
  and is what the review, evidence, reasons, and remedy contracts exist to
  catch.

**Remaining boundary:** this settlement is a set of constitutional interfaces.
It is not a security capability, a border system, an emergency response, or a
guarantee that the people holding these powers will obey the rules that bind
them.
