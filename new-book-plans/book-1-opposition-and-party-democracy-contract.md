# Opposition rights and internal party democracy

Ruling G of the 2026-09-09 integrity decision. The common integrity-record
contract supplies independent writers, exact scope, current evidence,
challenge, correction, remedy and an alternate route.

## Opposition

The qualifying record identifies an elected member or group and contains its
**own** recorded `NotSupportingGovernment` position. Neither a party label nor
registration is a premise. Alongside independent attestations, that position
derives equal access to information the government supplies to the Assembly,
Constitutional Court and electoral-administration challenge permissions, and
a barrier against majority withdrawal of a place in Assembly proceedings.
Assembly procedure supplies the mechanics, not the right.

The closed withholding kinds are `EqualAssemblyInformation`,
`IndependentConstitutionalAndElectoralChallenge`, and
`GuaranteedAssemblyProceedingsPlace`. A current withholding finding makes
the withholding act incompatible and creates a restoration duty on its
reader. It does not create a personal loyalty classification.

## Associations that field candidates

`IntegrityPartyMinimumVocabulary` consists of `MemberElectedLeadership`,
`MemberControlledCandidateSelection`, `EqualMemberVoice`, and `FreeMemberExit`.
The permission is precisely
`authority(association, InternalDemocraticCandidateSelection, selection)`.
It requires every minimum, the equality-compatible candidate-list condition,
current political-finance clearance, and nominal and controlling payer
identities. A named minimum's independently established failure withholds
permission for that selection and creates a correction duty.

The positive `FieldsCandidatesInThisSelection` premise is essential: a union,
faith, cultural, or advocacy association that fields no candidates owes no
duty under this card. A member's own ballot and candidacy by another route
remain untouched. No party register is a gate. No rule produces a power to
proscribe a party; the failure case expressly refuses that as a remedy.
Individual criminal conduct remains in the justice interface, finance in A,
and coordinated operations in F.

Cases live in `tests/pins/integrity/opposition-rights/`,
`opposition-withholding/`, `party-selection/` and `party-finding/`.
Missing position, qualification, ballot activity, any minimum or independent
review withholds the corresponding conclusion. The selection sequence
checks that a current failure changes permission for that selection only.

Book 1 projects rights and conditions, not an assertion that opposition is
heard or an association democratic. Book 2 owns Assembly procedure,
information access, internal elections, candidate selection workflows,
independent review, staffing and enforcement; those operations remain inactive
here until Gate C.
