# Knowledge, communication, culture and the free social field

Implementation contract for the author-directed TODO item of the same name,
under the 2026-08-03 expanded mandate and the 2026-09-12 verification ruling.
It implements the conditions under which a public actor may restrict these
activities, and what it owes when it has not. It establishes no school,
newspaper, library, archive, platform, gallery or club, and it certifies
nothing about what anybody said, believed, made or enjoyed.

## What the family is about

Ten fields are named and closed as a vocabulary: learning and information
access; expression and publication; conscience, religion and non-belief;
association and assembly; media and press plurality; academic and scientific
inquiry; artistic and cultural creation; language and accessible
communication; public information access; and sport, leisure, friendship and
mutual aid. A record outside that list completes nothing, which is what the
`knowledge-no-field-vocabulary` counterfactual exists to show.

The floor already carries `learn`, `expresses`, `believe` and `meets`. This
family does not touch them and supplies them no new delivery head. It governs
the other half: what public power must establish before it narrows one of
these activities, and what it owes while it has not.

## The restriction is the hard case, so it carries the conditions

A reviewed restriction needs an evidenced rights or commons harm — those two
grounds and no others, closed as a vocabulary — together with least-restrictive
effective means, a defined reach and affected population, public reasons and
accessible notice, an independent review with a suspensive challenge, no
viewpoint, belief or identity targeting, no prior permission for activity that
is not restricted, and no certification of truth, taste or creative merit.
Offence, disagreement and official truth are named in the evidence field as
what the ground is not.

Enforcement is separate and dependent. It rejoins the actual reviewed
restriction — matter, field, actor, version, period, jurisdiction, legal scope,
end condition and restriction kind — so a restriction reviewed for one matter
cannot be borrowed for another, and a restriction for one field cannot travel
to a different field. It adds proportionality to the reviewed harm only,
notice, reasons and an accessible challenge before effect, and the express
limit that nothing here reaches a floor, standing, the ballot or recognition.

## Residual freedom is a positive record, not an inference from silence

The `residual` contract is the wall the ruling asked for, and it is written the
way every other family writes a positive finding: an attested record that no
reviewed restriction applies to this activity, that no registration, licence or
approval conditions it, that no register of belief, opinion or association
membership is kept, and that nothing certifies truth, taste or creative merit.
Its conclusions are duties on the public actor — not to require permission, and
not to register belief, opinion or association membership.

The direction matters. The record does not conclude that the holder may act;
it concludes what the actor may not demand. Nothing about the activity becomes
a permission to restrict it, which its own case pins: no `authority`, no
enforcement permission and no completed restriction follows.

## What the other five contracts do

Public information carries an access, correction or accessible-form permission
for the requester and an accessible-supply duty on the holder, with refusal
confined to a reviewed ground with reasons and partial release, and no purpose
test or excluding fee. Accessible communication is separate and general:
proactive accessible formats and plain language, individual adjustment on
request without a fee, and no meaning carried only by one sense or notation.

Inquiry and creation autonomy concludes a permission to publish or perform
without approval and a duty on the actor not to direct the question, method,
result or content; funding conditions may not dictate findings.

A concentration finding is structural and actor-side. It names ownership
concentration, distribution gatekeeping or ranking and attention control, and
concludes a disclosure duty on the concentrated actor and a permission for the
challenge reader to open an independent plurality review. It adjudicates no
truth and no editorial choice, and classifies no audience and no belief — all
three are fields on the record and all three are pinned.

A reviewed defect withholds the exact record it names and creates review and
correction duties. A certified reader nonresponse moves the review duty to the
independent alternate. Neither adjudicates the underlying request, and neither
reads its own consequence.

## Polarity, separation and the corridor

Every conclusion is positive. A withheld record concludes nothing, which is the
`withheld` case for each contract, and a missing field concludes nothing, which
is the per-scope omission case for each contract and each of its scopes.

Three mutually distinct attesters — a source, an independent evidence authority
and an independent review authority — must agree, and none of them may be the
acting body. A challenge reader and an independent alternate carry their own
record-specific authorizations, are distinct from each other and from the three
attesters, and cannot be the actor. The final route is constitutional review or
its independent substitute. These are conditions on supplied credentials, not
proof of independence in practice.

Two attesters who disagree about a single-valued scope raise
`related($record, KnowledgeRecordAmbiguity)` and the record completes nothing:
a reader cannot pick whichever value would work. Every consequential head is
`derived_only` upstream, so a forged entry is refused at assertion rather than
believed; the `forged-conclusions` case pins that for all five heads this
family concludes.

Nothing concludes anything about a person. A record that merely notes somebody's
belief or association derives no adverse conclusion, leaves personhood, the
floor debt and the ballot standing, and leaves the existing person-held
barriers against compelled conscience, coerced association and involuntary
association exactly where they were. That is a pinned case, not a claim about
future rules.

## Classification and Book 2 handoff

Restriction, enforcement, public information and plurality review are Class 6
public functions; the liberties and the residual field protect Class 3;
notice, reasons, challenge and the alternate are Class 4 fair process; the
record, its defects and its corrections are Class 7. Accessibility is the
existing proactive-accessibility duty applied to communication, not a new
class. No new taxonomy class and no omnibus liberty predicate is introduced.

Book 2 owns schools, libraries, archives, broadcasters, platforms, translation
and interpretation services, format production, publication workflow, funding
administration, media-market measurement and every question of capacity.
Nibli authenticates no publication, measures no audience, detects no
concentration, decides no accessibility need and proves that no information
arrived.

## Executable cases and what they check

`knowledge-source.json` is the semantic input to `./generate.sh knowledge`. It
writes the `KNOWLEDGE-AND-FREE-FIELD-RULES` block — 129 rules — and 512
isolated cases under `tests/pins/knowledge/`. The spine is regenerated in the
same change: predicates, derived predicates, strata and the eight floor rights
are unchanged, and only the rule count moves, because the family introduces no
new relation name.

Every contract has positive, withheld, per-scope omission, unauthorized-attester,
stale, self-review, actor-as-reviewer, fused-alternate, mismatched-version,
period-drift and conflicting-value cases, and every vocabulary value is
exercised beside an unapproved one. The dependent enforcement contract adds
missing-authorization and wrong-matter, wrong-field, wrong-version,
wrong-period and wrong-restriction-kind cases.

Four counterfactuals remove one premise each — the independent-review
distinctness, the field vocabulary, the harm vocabulary, and the separation of
holder from actor — and each is paired with the positive control showing what
it prevented.

Nine integration cases carry the sequences: a reviewed restriction and its
enforcement both standing, then a defect against the restriction stopping both
while the affected person's personhood, floor debt, ballot and conscience
barriers survive; an unrelated defect leaving the record standing; a
restriction refusing to travel between fields; residual freedom licensing no
restriction; a concentration finding judging no content; a request and its
certified nonresponse; a noted belief concluding nothing; and the forged-head
refusals.
