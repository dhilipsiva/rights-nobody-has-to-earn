# Full-Society Volume, Edition, and Stopping Boundary Decision

> **Status: author-ratified 2026-08-07; reference-envelope ownership clarified 2026-08-13; external-human gate dependencies removed and claims narrowed 2026-08-15.** This is a scope,
> publication, and public-claim decision.
> It does not enact a constitutional rule, activate Book 2, or create a tag,
> preview, edition, print file, or release.

## 1. The ruling

The full-society mandate belongs to two complete but different volumes.

- **Book 1 is the destination.** It owns constitutional invariants, democratic
  steering, institutions, protected private and civic life, and the interfaces
  for ordinary function, failure, challenge, remedy, restoration, and recovery.
- **Book 2 is the road and operating model.** It owns staffing, costs, capacity,
  resources, technology, workflows, transition, deployment, empirical
  feasibility, and operation under ordinary and declared shock conditions.
- **Only the two books together may carry the full-society claim.** Neither
  volume may claim that its own assurance method proves the other volume's
  responsibilities.

Book 1 — First Edition may ship at Gate C. Book 2 remains collection-only until
that edition ships. Gate D may produce only immutable Book 2 previews or release
candidates. Book 2 — First Edition and the integrated full-society release wait
for Gate E.

The 2026-08-04 E2 + P1 + D2 ruling remains in force. Its previously open phrase
"completed expansion" now means **Book 1 after cumulative Gate C completion**.
The pre-expansion baseline remains public source and history without an edition
identity; P1 previews remain permitted; and Gate C Book 1 is the first Book 1
digital and print-on-demand edition.

## 3. Words whose scope must remain visible

- **Comprehensive** may describe Book 1's Gate B/C destination only with its
  source version and declared scope. It does not imply operational feasibility.
- **Exhaustive** may describe the disposition map only for named axes, a named
  source version, a named envelope, and a named current-source repository audit.
  It may never mean that no later material omission can be discovered.
- **Fully functional society** is reserved for Gate E and must be followed by
  "for the declared reference envelope" or an equally explicit bound.
- **Verified** names the checks actually run. Formal verification does not prove
  resources, liveness, lived effects, engine independence, or deployment unless
  a separate assurance route establishes that claim. The 2026-08-08 assurance
  portfolio names those routes and rules that a green verifier result is four
  epistemically different things — an executable engine result, a static pattern
  guard, a freshness comparison, and a reviewed inventory — of which only the
  first warrants a derivation claim. It also rules that a liveness claim may
  never be derived and that a feasibility claim may not be written in Book 1 at
  all while its routes are unavailable.

Protected private and civic life is not a blank in the map. An explicit
non-prescription or non-interference disposition counts as coverage;
completeness does not authorise a rule, record, operator, or success
certification for every harmless practice. The disposition must still check for
rights harm, dependency or concentrated power, public duty, and commons or
external effects; the residual free field may not hide coercion. It does not
authorise prescribing belief, love, friendship, art, family form, identity,
fulfilment, or another harmless personal outcome.

## 4. Versioned closure

A version may close only when all of the following are true for that gate's
permitted claim:

1. every known material domain, role, power, dependency, scenario, and failure
   has an explicit disposition in the canonical source;
2. every required projection regenerates from that source;
3. no unresolved critical gap applicable to that gate's permitted claim
   remains;
4. every non-critical residual records severity, consequence, owner, closure
   condition, and the resulting public-claim limitation; and
5. a current-source repository adversarial audit covers the exact declared
   criteria, checker controls, command chain, and Gate-A-applicable finding set.

The closure record binds the exact gate and permitted claim, canonical-source
version, reference envelope, candidate or edition identities, audit cut-off,
assurance records, residuals, and claim limitations. A semantic change after
the cut-off requires a new audit and candidate. Closure is never inferred from
a green check: the author ratifies it in a separate act.

A finding is material when accepting it could change a declared right, liberty,
power, duty, protected private boundary, cross-domain dependency, ordinary-life
account, failure/recovery path, or adequacy, accessibility/equality, continuity,
resilience, sustainability, safety, or resource criterion.

Every repository finding receives one of three outcomes:

1. **add it** to the canonical model with stable identity, applicability, owner,
   assurance route, posture, and closure condition;
2. **classify it with reasons** under an existing exact disposition, carrying
   the matching Unestablished disposition where it routes outward; or
3. **retain it as an unresolved limit** with severity, consequence, owner,
   closure condition, gate applicability, and claim restriction.

The checker validates the source consistency of those outcomes. The author owns
the substantive decision and its public claim limit. This is accountable author
ratification, not independent review and not self-authentication.

Optional external reviewer proposals use the same public outcomes if they are
recorded, but no empty external intake blocks closure. External feedback may
trigger a correction and fresh repository audit; it has no veto and is not a
gate dependency.

The assurance routes remain distinct. R7 is repository source-derived
adversarial audit; R6 is optional reader and lived-experience evidence. A green
route may not stand in for another route, and a repository audit may not be
reported as human review, reader evidence, external truth, operation, or
feasibility.

## 5. Reopening, correction, and supersession

Closure is event-driven by a release or a material challenge. It creates no
perpetual calendar-based review obligation.

- A checked post-release critical classification receives an immediate dated
  warning or public-claim qualification and blocks every later release until
  resolved and retested. Any decision to lower the proposed severity publishes
  the author decision, source basis, mechanical consistency result, and reasons.
- Immutable artifacts remain identified and accessible unless the existing
  legal, privacy, or security removal exception applies. Correction creates a
  new tag, artifact, and visible supersession path; it never rewrites an edition
  in place.
- A non-critical discovery may remain disclosed against the current edition,
  but it must receive a final disposition before the next release; retention as
  a severity-rated limit is a disposition.
- If Book 2 work exposes a critical Book 1 defect, Gate E cannot pair the
  incompatible Book 1 edition. Book 1 must publish a corrected immutable version
  and the integrated checks must run against that exact replacement.
- A material external condition that the project does not control remains a
  named external assumption with an owner, evidence status, consequence, and
  claim limit. It is not converted into a fictional guarantee to close the map.

Reopening follows the dependency chain. A critical scope or assurance omission
reopens Gate A and every dependent gate. A Book 1 defect reopens the affected
Gate B/C work and blocks Gate D/E. A Book 2 defect reopens Gate D/E. An
integration-only defect reopens Gate E. A Book 2 finding that invalidates Book 1
requires a corrected Book 1 edition, rerun affected Gate B/C work, a new POD
identity when the interior changed, a regenerated Book 2 crosswalk or candidate,
and a fresh Gate E pass. Warnings live on mutable canonical index, status, or
errata pages linked to the immutable artifacts; a correction creates a new
manifest rather than editing the old one.
