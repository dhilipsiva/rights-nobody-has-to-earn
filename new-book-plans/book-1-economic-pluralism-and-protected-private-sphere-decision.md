# Book 1 Economic Pluralism and Protected Private Sphere Decision

> **Status: author-ratified on 2026-08-07; formalized and prose-landed on
> 2026-08-28; current-source repository audit pending.** This decision selects
> Book 1's economic and protected-private-sphere settlement. Its formal
> implementation is source- and repository-bound: it proves no operating
> institution, authenticated external finding, institutional action, delivery,
> liveness, affordability, supply, capacity, stability, fiscal or monetary
> performance, scarcity, or empirical feasibility.

## 1. Decision

Book 1 adopts a **constitutionally bounded plural economy and a protected
voluntary private sphere**.

Public, cooperative, commons, mutual, nonprofit, household, and private
arrangements are lawful. The constitution entrenches no exclusive economic
system, preferred ownership form, guaranteed market share, profit, bailout,
immunity, or monopoly. Democratic law may establish, combine, regulate,
support, convert, or discontinue lawful forms above the material floor.

That choice remains inside the democratic corridor. Property, contract,
enterprise, prices, accumulation, taxation, and economic regulation are
conditional on universal standing, the material floor, substantive equality,
labour rights, core liberties, due process, anti-domination, effective remedy,
and commons limits. No ownership label makes a violation lawful.

This decision does not enlarge the unamendable democratic corridor by
implication. That corridor remains universal standing, equality, the floor, due
process, core liberties, and protected commons. The economic rules ratified here
remain constitutional rules unless lawfully amended; an amendment may alter an
ownership form, policy instrument, or other above-floor choice only while
preserving the corridor. Where a labour, private-sphere, property, or economic
rule protects a corridor right, that protected core cannot be amended away.

The constitution protects voluntary private and civic life from prescription,
outcome certification, and social scoring. “Private” is not an exemption from
equality, labour, consumer, anti-coercion, or floor-continuity duties. A private
actor may acquire function-specific public-like duties when an independent,
contestable finding establishes essentiality, dominance, gatekeeping,
dependency, lock-in, network effects, information asymmetry, or the absence of
meaningful exit.

## 2. Plural provision and the floor

The public duty to finance, secure, and maintain the material floor is
non-delegable. Privatization, insurance, contracting, charity, household
support, cash support, or another delivery arrangement cannot transfer or
extinguish it. A cooperative, public body, household, association, or
nonprofit can violate the applicable duties when it expressly undertakes or
controls a bound delivery, employment, commercial, or public-facing function.
Ordinary intimate dependency alone is not a direct constitutional delivery
duty; coercion or captivity instead reaches justice and public protection.

Prices and voluntary exchange are permitted above the floor. Price, wealth,
credit, contribution, insurance status, or ability to pay may not decide access
to a floor item. Under a declared genuine physical scarcity, those factors may
not decide priority for an essential item either.

Cash, vouchers, or insurance count only as delivery instruments. They establish
no successful floor delivery unless independent recipient-side evidence shows
timely, accessible, adequate real access to the required condition. A payment
that cannot procure the good is not delivery.

Independent recipient-side evidence may show that voluntary household, mutual-
aid, charitable, or private provision currently satisfies the required
condition. That evidence prevents forced duplicate delivery but never
extinguishes the entitlement or the public responsibility. Withdrawal, dispute,
inaccessibility, inadequacy, or failure activates immediate public continuity.

The floor remains noncontributory. Contribution-based social insurance and
other supplements may operate above it, but a missing contribution record,
employer failure, or contribution dispute cannot lower the floor.

Price and lawfully held wealth may mediate an ordinary voluntary transfer of
above-floor private property. `reward`, recognition, merit, a qualifying
threshold, a personal score, and raw work or contribution facts may not directly
qualify property access. A separately adjudicated wage or equity claim is a
distinct legal relation; its resulting money or property may be transferred
ordinarily. A separately enacted grant, prize, subsidy, restitution, or
insurance supplement may support acquisition only through purpose-specific,
equality-compatible criteria that do not read recognition or declare personal
worth. This is the 2026-08-07 refinement of the broader 2026-08-03 property
wall: it permits ordinary transfer through distinct legal claims but preserves
the prohibition on recognition, merit, thresholds, scores, and raw contribution
as direct property qualifications.

## 3. Work, labour, qualifications, and incentives

Every person is free to choose, refuse, leave, and change work. Work,
registration, contribution, compliance, obedience, or participation in a
programme may not
gate standing, the floor, political membership, or remedy. Work performed in
custody must be voluntary and receives ordinary labour protection.

Public institutions must support genuine access to decent work, training, and
occupational participation. This is an opportunity and anti-exclusion duty,
not a constitutional promise that a named person receives a particular job.
Democratic law may nevertheless create a job-guarantee programme.

Across every ownership form, workers receive:

- safe and healthy conditions;
- fair remuneration, timely wage recovery, rest, and predictable terms;
- equality, accommodation, privacy, and accessible inspection;
- freedom of association, unions, collective bargaining, and collective
  action; and
- information, consultation, or co-determination for high-consequence
  workplace decisions, with structures and thresholds set by compatible law.

A strike may be subject to a minimum-service requirement only where the actual
interruption threatens life, safety, health, or the floor. The restriction must
be necessary, narrow, independently reviewable, and paired with an effective
bargaining or adjudication substitute. It binds the responsible provider or
bargaining parties to arrange continuity; it may not conscript a named worker,
criminalize an individual's refusal to work, or remove that person's floor.
Blanket sectoral bans are refused.

Worker status follows actual control, dependency, integration, and economic
reality—not a contractor, franchise, platform, family, cooperative, volunteer,
or other label. Voluntary household care is not employment merely because it
has value; controlled commercial labour cannot be disguised as affection or
mutual aid.

Mandatory occupational licensing is permitted only for evidenced serious
safety, fiduciary, or core-public-function risk. Otherwise law should prefer
certification. Every mandatory route needs relevant standards, accessible
alternative proof, portability, review, expiry or renewal discipline, and
anti-cartel safeguards. A qualification may affect only the function for which
it is justified; it cannot become a personhood, floor, worth, or political
status.

Above the floor, law may permit wages, profits, savings, investment returns,
grants, prizes, subsidies, and bounded incentives for difficulty, skill,
innovation, maintenance, investment, or public service. These are compensation
or policy instruments, not `reward`. Recognition remains binary, arity-one,
non-ranked, and unread. No rule may read `false`, `lose`, or missing
recognition to alter wages, benefits, pensions, property, insurance, authority,
or political weight. Separately adjudicated fraud may support proportionate
restitution through due process.

## 4. Property, housing, inheritance, and knowledge

Possession, use, transfer, and inheritance are protected conditional liberties,
not absolute or natural powers over other people. Personal possessions,
ordinary home use, and lawful productive activity receive strong security.
Law may still impose taxation, zoning, tenant protection, vacancy and
speculation controls, access duties, remediation duties, and anti-concentration
measures consistent with equality, due process, the floor, and commons limits.

The housing floor guarantees an adequate secure dwelling, not ownership or
perpetual possession of a particular asset. Rental, cooperative, customary,
public, household, and private tenure are all lawful. Informality or imperfect
title does not erase a person's dwelling claim.

Before eviction or foreclosure may threaten that floor, law must provide
legality, notice, a hearing, proportionality, review, and a real public
continuity route. A high-value asset need not be preserved where adequate secure
housing and due process remain real.

Compulsory acquisition requires law, a constitutionally compatible public
purpose, necessity, public reasons, a hearing, review, and compensation
calibrated to legitimate reliance, actual use, and hardship while excluding a
publicly created windfall from the compensable amount. Illegal title, unlawful
privilege, speculative windfall, a valid penalty, and remediation liability do
not generate a compensation claim merely because they have economic value.

Inheritance is permitted subject to capacity-based taxation and anti-dynastic
or anti-concentration controls. Public power, political weight, immunity, and
personal guilt cannot be inherited. An heir is not personally liable for an
estate's debt beyond inherited estate value.

Patents, copyright, and comparable exclusivity are time-bounded statutory
incentives, not natural property. No exclusivity may block a floor or commons
duty. Democratic law must provide an effective compatible access route where
that conflict arises and may choose among public research, open standards,
interoperability, prizes, compulsory licensing, procurement, or another lawful
floor- or commons-protecting instrument.

This economic ruling did not itself decide Indigenous, customary, communal,
family, or faith-community title or detailed succession mechanics. The separate
family, dependency, reproduction, and collective-plurality ruling of 2026-08-08
now settles the family and succession interfaces and the Indigenous and
collective portions of that scope, including strong Indigenous collective title
and rights-bounded membership. Ecological ceilings and the general commons
contract were also outside this economic decision when it landed. The separate
ecological, future-generation, commons, and non-human-animal ruling of 2026-08-08
now settles that distinct planning scope. No property or collective title,
contract, price, profit, market demand, insurance, enterprise form, or collective
consent supplies permission to exceed its ceiling or evade its direct animal
protection. Claims to title not established by either later ruling remain outside
this decision, without weakening the present floor, equality, exit, anti-
exploitation, ecological, commons, or animal-protection limits.

## 5. Contracts, consumers, and enterprises

People may contract or refuse to contract. Consent is not established by a
signature alone where deception, coercion, unconscionable dependency, material
non-disclosure, or unavailable meaningful exit controls the transaction.

No enforceable contract may waive the material floor, equality, labour rights,
core privacy protection, commons duties, due process, or effective remedy.
Purpose-limited disclosure may rest on meaningful, revocable consent with data
minimization, correction, and challenge; it does not waive the underlying
privacy right. Public-facing services require accessible terms, safety,
correction, cancellation where appropriate, collective redress, and continuity
where the service is essential.

An enterprise receives only the functional legal capacities needed to own,
contract, associate, and litigate. Corporate personality is a regulated
statutory tool, not human standing. An enterprise has no floor, ballot,
candidacy, or inherent political weight. Limited liability is likewise a
conditional statutory tool. Law may trace beneficial control and pierce the
veil or withdraw a privilege for fraud, evasion, asset stripping, abusive
control, deliberate undercapitalization, or strategic liability dumping.

Enterprises may petition, testify, and publish attributed views under
transparent equal-access rules. They may not donate to candidates or parties or
purchase independent electoral advocacy. The associated people retain their
individual speech, association, petition, and political rights.

For this financing rule, an enterprise treasury means assets held through
statutory enterprise capacities, including a for-profit, public, cooperative,
mutual, or nonprofit provider. This decision does not silently classify a union
or a genuinely voluntary civic or advocacy association as an enterprise. A
later Class 5 card must govern their political finance, prevent relabeling from
evading the enterprise rule, and preserve equal ballots and individual rights.

Legitimate commercial confidentiality is protected. It cannot be invoked to
deny authorized oversight, reasons, challenge, or remedy. Required disclosure
must minimize personal data and genuinely unrelated trade secrets, while still
exposing the facts needed to investigate rights violations, safety risks, public
subsidies or contracts, regulatory compliance, and commons harm.

## 6. Function-specific public-like duties

The equality decision already binds its named public-facing private domains
without requiring a dominance finding. This section adds a different trigger:
an independent and contestable public-scale-power finding based on one or more
of the following as they affect a specific function:

- essentiality or delegated public function;
- dominance or gatekeeping power;
- network effects or control of a necessary interface;
- dependency, lock-in, or severe information asymmetry; or
- the absence of a timely, accessible, meaningful alternative.

The finding must identify the actor, function, affected people, evidence,
source, scope, review route, temporal status, and consequence of error or
expiry. Size or organizational form alone is insufficient. The function must be
public-facing, delegated, essential, gatekeeping, or systemically controlling
access beyond a freely chosen intimate relationship. Dependency or information
asymmetry inside a household, friendship, or intimate relationship does not by
itself turn that relationship into a public service. Coercion, captivity,
violence, and obstructed exit there remain subject to the anti-coercion,
justice, public-protection, and remedy rules.

A platform, landlord, lender, insurer, utility, monopoly, cooperative,
mutual-aid body, cultural body, or charity crosses the boundary only for the
affected public-facing or essential function when the evidence establishes the
trigger. A mutual-aid or cultural body that becomes the only essential
gatekeeper can cross it for that service without surrendering unrelated
expressive or internal freedom.

Duties are calibrated to that function and may include fair access,
continuity, reasons, transparency, data or service portability,
interoperability, audit, challenge, and remedy. They do not automatically govern
every internal or expressive activity of the actor.

Compatible remedies include access mandates, public options, structural
separation, breakup, receivership, licence withdrawal, or public acquisition.
Every remedy must protect workers, users, and floor recipients and preserve
essential-service continuity. Receivership and continuity administration need
their own source-bound temporal contracts. Public acquisition must also obey the
property, hearing, review, and compensation rules in Section 4.

## 7. Protected voluntary private sphere

The constitution protects voluntary gifts, resource pooling, care, domestic
production, mutual aid, lawful exchange, belief, culture, worship, friendship,
intimacy, and association from public prescription, outcome certification, and
social scoring.

Every participant retains independent standing, legal capacity, floor access,
access to lawful claims over property that remains theirs, a confidential route
to help, and free exit. No spouse,
partner, family, household, congregation, association, mutual-aid group, or
charity is presumed to supply another person's floor.

Care periods may support portable social-insurance supplements without turning
affection or household life into employment, contribution merit, or `reward`.
Privacy does not protect violence, forced labour, confiscation, document
control, economic captivity, obstruction of help, or denial of free exit.

The protected voluntary sphere neither proves nor excuses ecological or animal-
protection harm. A harmless voluntary practice does not become public merely
because it uses resources or involves an animal; the later ruling's evidence and
reach contract must be satisfied. Every human-controlled animal use remains
subject to the non-waivable welfare baseline. Where a lethal, invasive, or high-
severity use is established, a private, cultural, religious, charitable, or
commercial label does not waive the enhanced serious-purpose, necessity,
alternatives, and least-harm requirements.

The equality ruling's exception for genuinely expressive roles remains narrow
and mission-linked. It does not expand into general employment, public
commerce, housing, education, health or care, finance, utilities, transport, or
essential-service immunity.

Those family-support, kinship, guardianship, family-property, child-work,
collective-land, cultural-membership, and succession questions were open when
this economic ruling landed. The separate 2026-08-08 ruling now governs modular
pair and plural family roles, parentage beyond two legal parents, public-first
care continuity, the refusal of compulsory adult-kin personal care, supported
decision-making, bounded child-work limits, family property and succession, and
collective land and cultural membership. It preserves each person's independent
floor, free exit, no-score protection, and freedom from exploitation. See
[`book-1-family-dependency-reproduction-and-collective-plurality-decision.md`](book-1-family-dependency-reproduction-and-collective-plurality-decision.md).

## 8. Taxation, public finance, money, and insurance

The tax system as a whole must reflect capacity to contribute. Democratic law
chooses compatible rates, bases, exemptions, and mixtures, including income,
consumption, wealth, land, inheritance, resource, and transaction taxes. Every
tax needs lawful authority, public purpose, equality, privacy, reasons, audit,
and challenge.

Collection may reach above-floor income and assets. It may not use debt
imprisonment or remove essential subsistence, an adequate secure dwelling,
standing, or effective remedy.

Taxation, appropriation, spending, guarantees, and borrowing require
legislative authorization, complete reporting, audit, and fiscal-risk
disclosure. The constitution imposes no balanced-budget rule or numeric debt
ceiling. Public debt cannot waive constitutional authority, subordinate floors
to creditors, erase commons duties, or create inherited personal liability.

The state-form ruling's budget-deadlock default remains: prior enumerated
essential authority and the floor continue; no new programme or permanent
spending power arises through deadlock.

The common tier must maintain a public unit of account and an accessible
settlement backbone, including a non-digital route. Compatible law may permit
regulated complementary payment instruments.

A monetary authority may have operational independence only within a
democratically enacted mandate covering reliable payments, price and financial
stability, decent-work opportunity, and floor continuity. It must publish
reasons, undergo distributional review and audit, use cause-only removal, and
hold no veto over lawful fiscal policy.

There is no general constitutional right to credit. Credit and insurance
decisions require lawful criteria, reasons, equality, correction of relevant
data, and challenge. A floor service cannot depend on private insurance.

The noncontributory floor may be supplemented by portable contribution-based
protection for illness, disability, unemployment, caregiving, injury, ageing,
and survivors. The records remain purpose-limited and barred from reuse as
standing, worth, risk, or political scores. The earned supplement or benefit,
not merely its record, must remain protected against employer insolvency.

## 9. Debt, insolvency, and institutional failure

Personal insolvency must offer an accessible fresh start while preserving floor
essentials and ordinary tools. Debtor imprisonment is prohibited. Any fraud
exception requires an individualized finding through due process.

Unpaid wages and earned pensions require effective protection through priority,
a public guarantee, or another route that works even where the failed estate is
empty. Shareholders bear residual risk and have no guaranteed rescue return.
Law must prevent insider preference, fraudulent transfer, asset stripping, and
strategic disposal of remediation duties.

Enterprise failure cannot extinguish constitutional remedies or interrupt an
essential service without public continuity. Restructuring, receivership,
licence control, scarcity administration, and every other temporary economic
power require a fresh source-bound temporal contract. The custody T3 contract
cannot be reused.

Review silence never becomes approval. Every required reviewer must have an
alternate route if unavailable. When source, trigger, scope, review, or end
evidence is missing, the restrictive or managerial authority ends or fails
closed; the independent public floor and essential-service continuity duty
survives and transfers through the predeclared alternate route. Missing review
cannot prolong the temporary manager or terminate the service.

## 10. Genuine physical scarcity

Scarcity is a constitutional failure condition to be managed, not permission
to redefine the floor. It exists only after authenticated, contestable evidence
establishes insufficient usable supply or capacity for a named resource and
population after reasonable alternatives, reserves, substitution,
coordination, replenishment, and mutual assistance have been considered.

The finding must name the resource, population, responsible authority,
evidence, source, scope, source-bound end, review route, and
reassessment duty. Budget choice, price exclusion, administrative delay,
artificial withholding, hoarding, monopoly restriction, provider failure, and
refusal to procure are not physical scarcity.

Sector-specific protocols may differ, but each must obey this constitutional
ordering:

1. Preserve each person's equal constitutional minimum wherever the usable
   supply makes that physically possible.
2. If supply cannot meet that minimum for everyone, do not rename a reduced
   ration as the constitutional minimum. Record every unmet portion as failure.
   For a divisible resource, preserve an effective and usable equal share where
   one exists; never split a threshold-dependent resource into equally useless
   pieces. Where no usable equal share exists, use urgency, accessibility,
   imminent irreversible harm, continuity harm, and individualized resource-
   specific likelihood of benefit after accommodation to mitigate the shortfall.
3. A benefit judgment must concern this resource and this decision. It may not
   use generalized lifespan, disability, productivity, social usefulness, or
   another group proxy as a disguised worth ranking.
4. Never use wealth, contribution, recognition, conviction, family status,
   disability stereotype, expected productivity, social usefulness,
   generalized lifespan, or political favour as a priority key.
5. Use a disclosed rotation or lottery only among materially equal claims.

Every unmet floor claim remains recorded as a constitutional failure. Fair
rationing mitigates harm; it does not transform non-delivery into success.

A physical-scarcity finding is not an ecological authorization. This allocation
route cannot establish a scientific ceiling, prove that an ecological threshold
has been crossed, or permit a ceiling breach. It must operate inside the
separately ratified ecological and commons contract. Budget choice, artificial
withholding, and damage caused by violating that contract remain failures, not
scarcity and not permission.
Public reasons, independent review, challenge, interim alternatives,
accessibility, correction, replenishment, a duty to obtain and record fresh
reassessment evidence, a source-bound end, and repair remain mandatory. Book 1
does not make the reassessment clock advance or the evidence arrive.

This decision creates no standing requisition, rationing, compulsory-
continuity, or price-control power. Any emergency authority needs a separate
trigger, scope, review, remedy, and temporal contract through the
emergency ruling. That ruling landed on 2026-08-08 and disposes of all four by
name. It confers **compensated requisition** as one of exactly four emergency
powers. **Rationing** is not a new power: where a genuine physical shortage
exists it is an instance of the scarcity contract in this section and runs
through it unchanged, so a declaration neither creates a shortage, lowers the
evidential threshold for finding one, nor licenses allocation on any key this
contract forbids. **Compulsory continuity** keeps the narrow form already
ratified here — binding a provider or bargaining party, never conscripting a
named worker, criminalizing an individual refusal, or withdrawing a floor.
**Price control** is ordinary economic law available to the legislature at any
time inside this settlement; it is not an emergency power, needs no declaration,
and confers no unilateral executive pricing authority. See
[`book-1-public-safety-defence-emergency-and-external-power-decision.md`](book-1-public-safety-defence-emergency-and-external-power-decision.md).
Book 2 owns evidence collection and assurance methods,
inventories, forecasts, quantities, optimization, production, and capacity. The
authorized decision institution supplies the reviewed live scarcity finding;
Nibli may consume only its bounded projection.

## 11. Federal allocation

This decision deliberately clarifies and supplements the state-form decision's
enumerated common competences. It does not create an unlimited commerce,
spending, taxation, or pre-emption power.

The common tier owns only the following economic subjects in addition to its
already ratified floor, equalisation, portability, interregional-coordination,
and commons responsibilities:

- the common unit of account and payment backbone, and necessary common
  financial-stability safeguards;
- floor finance, social-insurance portability, and equalisation;
- interregional commerce and competition, insolvency baselines, and
  cross-regional public-scale private power; and
- common labour and consumer minima.

Residual ownership, land, enterprise, service, cooperative, municipal-
enterprise, and local-development policy remains regional or local under
subsidiarity. Subnational law may provide stronger labour, consumer, housing,
social-protection, and private-power safeguards. It may not lower the common
floor or minima, obstruct portability, impose regional waiting periods or
property qualifications, or use internal protectionism or fiscal competition
to defeat equality or residence-first membership.

Each tier may tax, spend, and borrow only under a constitutional or statutory
source within its assigned competence, with audit, review, and public scope.
Coordination and equalisation cannot become a general common power or a means of
withholding the floor from a dissenting region. Regional or local authorities
make and enforce public-scale private-power findings confined to their
jurisdiction under the common constitutional baseline; the common tier owns
cross-regional cases and common minima. A conflict rule must displace
subnational law only to the extent needed to protect an enumerated common duty;
compatible stronger protection survives.

## 12. Enforcement and remedies

An affected person, union, qualified association, ombud or rights advocate, or
authorized public regulator may initiate a claim within its proper scope.
Investigation, adjudication, execution, audit, and final review must remain
institutionally separated.

Available individual and continuity remedies include cessation, interim
provision, accommodation, wage or payment recovery, reinstatement, eviction
stay, contract reformation or invalidation, debt or record correction,
restitution, compensation, and retaliation protection.

Available systemic remedies include access orders, institutional redesign,
audit, monitoring, licence conditions or withdrawal, public options,
restructuring, structural separation, breakup, receivership, public acquisition,
and guarantees of non-repetition. Ordinary courts may decline or invalidate a
rule for case-specific relief; only the Constitutional Court may issue final
general invalidation. Public acquisition obeys Section 4, and every temporary
receivership or restructuring power needs its own temporal contract. Remedies
against an enterprise must protect workers, users, ordinary savers, and floor
recipients during the transition.

No remedy may remove another person's standing, floor, core liberty, due
process, or political equality. Economic enforcement itself may not become a
new withholding gate.

## 13. Book 1, Book 2, and engine boundary

Book 1 owns the legal forms, rights, limits, protected choices, public powers,
triggers, evidence requirements, burdens, ordering principles, continuity,
review, remedies, federal competence, and failure polarity ratified here.

Democratic law chooses the compatible ownership and provider mix, tax mix,
above-floor wage and benefit levels, insolvency priorities, inheritance
exemptions, competition thresholds, and policy instruments.

Book 2 owns rates, budgets, models, prices, production, inventories, actuarial
assumptions, monetary instruments, staffing, capacity, workflows, transition,
deployment, monitoring, and empirical evaluation. No Book 1 claim may imply
tested affordability, adequate production, stable prices, sufficient staffing,
operational delivery, or real-world deployment. Gate C permits the Book 1
constitutional-destination claim. Gate D may support only the bounded claim that
an immutable Book 2 preview or release candidate contains a reproducible
operational design for its named envelope; it is neither proof of deployment nor
an integrated full-society claim. Gate E publishes Book 2 First Edition and the
integrated manifest and alone permits the bounded fully-functional-society
claim.

Nibli may consume an authenticated bounded adjudicative projection concerning
worker status, title, liability, insolvency, tax or fiscal authorization,
dominance, scarcity, licence status, award, or review result. It must enter
through a distinct premise or result-record relation. A consequential predicate
protected by `derived_only` must still be derived and may never be asserted by
renaming the imported conclusion. Every imported finding must record its source,
scope, contestability, review, temporal status, and failure consequence.

Nibli must not be credited with valuing property, calculating tax, clearing a
market, measuring dominance or disparity, forecasting prices, inflation, or
capacity, optimizing or proving scarcity, authenticating identity or financial
records, operating a payment, or proving publication, storage, institutional
action, delivery, or an outside clock's advance. Raw financial, contribution,
insurance, credit, and equality data remain purpose-limited and outside the
canonical consequential person record. A minimal authenticated, adjudicated,
purpose-bound projection may enter only the rule family it serves, with challenge
and correction, and may not expose the raw record or be reused for another
consequence. No universal productivity, deservingness, contribution,
employability, or economic-risk score is permitted. An engine handoff is
appropriate only for a genuinely missing general reasoning capability, never
for statistics or economic modelling.

## 14. Formal migration contract

Formal work must be split by direct legal effect across the existing
constitutional taxonomy. No omnibus `economy` predicate is permitted. Before
formalization, census and give explicit retain, replace, split, or retire
decisions to at least:

`work`, `teaches`, `reward`, `lose`, `false`, `home`, `family`, `dwell`,
`secure`, `owe`, `public`, `authority`, `person`, `building`,
`obliged`, `become`, and `travel`.

Do not reuse `work`, `home`, `family`, or `public` as employer status, property
title, household eligibility, or evidence of public-scale private power.
Create separate source-bound temporal contracts for knowledge-exclusivity
expiry; occupational licences; public-scale-power findings; monetary-office
terms and succession; temporary management; and benefit, title, or liability
carry. A stated duration is outside evidence, not a clock Nibli advances.
Preserve the recognition firewall, `admits`, `derived_only`, the floor anti-
sanction firewall, and the no-score guards as distinct controls.
