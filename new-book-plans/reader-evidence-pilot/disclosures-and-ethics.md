# Pilot Disclosures and Ethics Terms

> **Status: draft template; not approved, pre-registered, or used for consent.**
> A locally valid participant-facing consent process and any required
> independent review must be completed outside the public repository.

These terms are binding admissibility conditions, not aspirations. A breach
makes the affected session inadmissible regardless of its answers. Compliance
does not establish that the study is ethical in every jurisdiction; the
operator remains responsible for applicable law and institutional review.

## 1. Mandatory study disclosures

Before consent, give each candidate an accessible participant-facing statement
that includes all of the following without promotional claims:

- this is a pilot of a reading instrument and one declared snapshot, not a test
  of the participant and not a release-suitability study;
- **The ordinary-life account rests on unimplemented families at the time of
  testing.**
- the tested snapshot's exact version identity and the formats being supplied;
- the evidence is usability evidence about the tested audience, not population
  statistics;
- the sampling and method limits bound every later reader claim;
- no reader result enters the reasoning engine or establishes a legal,
  empirical, operational, delivery, resource, or other route's claim;
- the subject matter includes imprisonment, coercion, institutional failure,
  family, dependency, and other potentially difficult material;
- the unaided section permits no content help, while access help remains
  available;
- what is kept privately, what privacy-minimal coded information may become
  public, who has access, and the pre-registered retention/destruction rule;
- participation is voluntary; declining, skipping, pausing, or withdrawing
  brings no retaliation and does not depend on giving a reason;
- the exact time expectation, compensation rate and formula, payment timing,
  accessibility support, and expense reimbursement; and
- contact routes for questions, withdrawal, access needs, data concerns, and
  independent ethics/safety concerns.

No disclosure may promise anonymity if a private identity mapping exists. Use
“confidential private custody with an identity-free public record” instead.

## 2. Informed consent and withdrawal

Consent is obtained before the snapshot or session is supplied for study use.
The candidate must have time to read or hear the disclosures, ask questions,
and choose without pressure. Consent, withdrawal, and identity records remain
private and are never represented by a public quotation.

A participant may decline any question, pause, request an accommodation, or
withdraw at any time without a reason. Withdrawal after the first prompt still
counts as an attempted session but supplies no public content outcomes. The
private consent materials must state the pre-registered point after which
already aggregated, irreversibly identity-separated public records cannot be
retrieved; no broader promise should be made.

The author, employer, teacher, service provider, public authority, or anyone
with power over a candidate must not recruit through that dependency or learn
who declined. Participation and answers cannot affect employment, education,
services, status, relationships, or later access to the book.

## 3. Accessible participation

Offer the frozen snapshot and study information in accessible HTML, EPUB, and
PDF paths and provide a usable alternative if a participant cannot use one of
them. Ask privately what format, communication mode, pacing, break, assistive
technology compatibility, interpreter, captioning, or other adjustment is
needed. Do not require diagnosis or proof where the adjustment can be supplied
without it.

Access assistance may solve mechanics but may not explain substantive content
during the unaided section. Accommodation time is compensated. An access
failure is recorded as a protocol event, never as reader misunderstanding.

## 4. Fair compensation formula

Before recruitment, freeze a locally applicable living-wage hourly rate `L`
from a named independent published source. If more than one source is
applicable, use the higher rate. If no defensible local living-wage source
exists, recruitment cannot begin until the independent ethics/safety reviewer
approves and records an equivalent rate basis.

For each participant:

`compensation = L × T`

`T` is all study time already spent: disclosure and consent, accessibility
setup, independent reading, session attendance, breaks required by the study,
and withdrawal administration. Reading time is participant-reported; other
time is privately logged. Currency rounding is only in the participant's
favour. Reasonable accessibility and connectivity expenses are reimbursed in
addition to this formula and never deducted from compensation.

Payment is due whether the session is completed, withdrawn, inadmissible,
excluded, unusable, favourable, unfavourable, or followed by a void pilot.
Compensation is not a bonus for agreement or a purchase of authority over the
participant.

## 5. Data minimisation and protection

Collect only data required for consent, recruitment variation, access,
compensation, session administration, coding, admissibility, and custody.
Separate identity/contact/payment records from response records. Restrict
access by role, encrypt private records in storage and transit, and maintain a
tested recovery copy under the same access rules.

Pre-register a retention and deletion schedule before consent. Do not keep raw
responses indefinitely “in case they are useful.” A legal or ethics duty that
requires a different schedule must be documented before recruitment or as a
deviation, never hidden.

Names, pseudonyms, identity mappings, raw responses, free text, consent and
withdrawal records, contacts, demographic/accessibility details, compensation
records, and coder identities never enter Git, issue trackers, pull requests,
chat transcripts, or public study records.

## 6. Trauma and safety safeguards

Do not recruit for personal experience of imprisonment, coercion, abuse,
poverty, disability, family conflict, or institutional failure. Do not ask the
participant to disclose such experience. Give the content warning before
consent and again before the session; allow breaks, skipping, and immediate
withdrawal without explanation.

The facilitator follows a privately frozen distress and safety procedure with
local, appropriate support contacts. The facilitator does not diagnose,
provide therapy, or pressure a participant to continue. A safety incident
stops the unaided section, prioritises the participant, and is recorded
privately with only a coded public deviation if allowed.

Use independent ethics and safety review wherever required by law,
institution, participant vulnerability, recruitment relationship, subject
matter, or unresolved risk. When applicability is uncertain, obtain review
rather than self-exempting.

## 7. Admissibility consequence

The reviewer/custodian assesses these terms independently of content coding.
Consent defects, withdrawal violations, uncompensated study time, inaccessible
participation, retaliation or dependency pressure, avoidable data exposure,
unmanaged safety risk, or missing required independent review make the session
inadmissible. Preserve the lifecycle and coded deviation; publish no content
outcomes and never relabel the breach as a misunderstanding by the reader.
