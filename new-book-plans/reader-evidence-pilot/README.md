# Reader-Evidence Pilot Materials

> **Status: draft templates; not pre-registered, frozen, run, or admitted.**
> These files contain no participant evidence, pilot result, severity taxonomy,
> release-threshold value, reviewer appointment, custody attestation, or author
> ratification. Their presence does not build or make R6 available, change
> FS-CLM-37, or satisfy Gate C.

This directory supplies the repository-controlled human materials for the
Book 1 reader-evidence pilot. The controlling decision remains
`new-book-plans/book-1-reader-evidence-protocol-decision.md`, and
`new-book-plans/reader-evidence.json` remains the sole machine-readable source
for study state. If a template conflicts with either source, the conflict is a
defect; the template supplies no alternative rule.

## Packet map

- `pilot-protocol.md` — study boundary, role separation, sequence, and invalidation rules;
- `instrument-template.md` — the public minimum contract and six ratified unaided prompts;
- `coding-rubric-template.md` — the public minimum coding contract without the private codebook;
- `sample-and-recruitment-rule.md` — eligibility, recruitment, attempt counting, and stopping rule;
- `disclosures-and-ethics.md` — consent disclosures, accessibility, compensation, withdrawal, and safety;
- `provisional-falsification-rule-template.md` — control-only rejection contract with no release-threshold values;
- `seeded-control-template.md` — private-preimage and public-commitment requirements, with no seed text or answer key;
- `custody-and-public-record.md` — private/public separation, commitments, receipts, and data handling; and
- `operator-checklist.md` — ordered execution and hand-off checklist.

## Controlled use

Before a pilot begins, the operator must complete the runnable instrument,
exact rubric codebook, diagnostic follow-up sequence, seeded transcript, and
control answer key in external private custody. Only their SHA-256 artifact or
nonce-protected commitment references enter the public pre-registration.
Merely committing these templates is not a freeze. Recruitment and data
collection may begin only after the external custody binding and the
corresponding frozen, `not-run` attempt are recorded and committed.

After that freeze, any change to a bound protocol, instrument, rubric, sample
rule, disclosure, ethics term, provisional control rule, or tested snapshot
voids the attempt. The old attempt remains recorded. A revised pilot requires
a new pre-registration and successor attempt.

## Deliberate omissions

The packet contains no severity label, core/non-core classification, release
threshold, denominator, repetition unit, missing-data verdict policy, or
pass/fail mapping for real readers. Pilot observation identifiers are aids for
instrument testing only. They cannot become ratified misconception IDs by
silence or default; the author must decide the complete later rule from the
frozen pilot decision packet and sensitivity brief.
