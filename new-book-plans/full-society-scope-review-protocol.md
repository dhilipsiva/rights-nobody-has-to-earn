# Full-Society Scope-Review Protocol — retired

The author retired this verification and commit protocol on 2026-09-12.
The current [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md) and [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) instructions
supersede every version of its hash, receipt, staged-candidate, audit-successor,
closure-successor, historical-recovery, and administrative gate requirements.

Verification now means running the substantive Nibli pins and contradiction
checks with `./verify.sh`. Prose consistency is reviewed separately. There is
no receipt or audit/closure commit prerequisite for ordinary work.

The former protocol remains available in Git history, not as executable
instructions. Its retirement does not remove constitutional requirements for
independent courts, reviewers, or other separated public functions, and does
not turn a passing test into evidence of real-world operation or delivery.
