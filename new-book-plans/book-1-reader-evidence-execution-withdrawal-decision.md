# Book 1 Reader-Evidence Execution Withdrawal Decision

> **Status: author-ratified 2026-08-11 — execution withdrawn from the current
> Book 1 program; dormant protocol preserved.** This is a scope and publication
> disposition. It records no pilot, accessibility attestation, threshold,
> ratification, holdout, reader result, route availability, Gate C pass,
> edition, or release.

## 1. Author statement and construction

The author's instruction is preserved verbatim:

> Let's clos things that require other humans. I can't find them now. Let's go
> forward without those.

This decision construes that instruction within the reader-evidence workflow
then under discussion. It does not silently dispose of every other task that
mentions external review, operational assurance, or another person.

## 2. Decision

The current Book 1 program will not pursue:

- the pre-registered reader-evidence pilot or fresh holdout;
- the reserved post-pilot severity taxonomy and release-threshold ruling;
- the independent facilitator, coder, adjudicator, and reviewer/custodian
  appointments required by that protocol; or
- the human screen-reader smoke test that was a condition of the reader-study
  accessibility path.

This is withdrawal of an objective, not completion of its work. No substitute
sample, synthetic participant, automated evaluator, repository reviewer,
author self-test, or disclosure may be relabelled as the missing human
evidence. The automated accessibility and artifact checks retain only their
mechanical warrant.

## 3. Preserved evidence state

The sole machine-readable reader-evidence source continues to record no admitted human study event:

- threshold status remains `pending-pilot` and the taxonomy and values remain
  empty;
- pilot and seeded-control status remain `not-run`, with no attempt;
- holdout status remains `not-frozen` and result remains `not-run`;
- R6 remains neither built nor available;
- FS-CLM-37 remains `Unestablished/route-unbuilt`; and
- Gate C remains false and receives no reader evidence.

No `void` attempt is created because nothing was frozen or run. The dormant
contract, checker, evaluator, admission-gate component, public-minimum kit, and
snapshot tooling remain available as specifications and tools. Their presence
does not change the state above.

## 4. Publication consequence

The existing C-then-E publication boundary is not weakened. Without the
declared accessibility and reader-balance evidence, Book 1 does not pass Gate
C. It may not publish as **Book 1 — First Edition**, ship the first-edition POD,
claim suitability for a tested audience, or activate Book 2.

Public source, Git history, and the map and test program may continue. If every
independent Gate A and Gate B condition and every applicable preview
snapshot-specific gate later passes, an immutable Gate B preview may publish
under its existing narrow claim. It must state that no admissible human reader
pilot, holdout, or human screen-reader validation is recorded or established
and may make no reader-suitability, accessibility-for-users, feasibility,
liveness, deployment, or functioning-society claim.

## 5. Tracker disposition

The author-gated threshold item and the reader-session item may be removed from
the current tracker only as **withdrawn by this decision**, never as completed.
The accessibility item may be narrowed to machine-checkable artifact work; its
human-validation absence remains a public claim limitation. The tracker change
lands separately and cites the commit containing this ruling.

## 6. Revival

A later author ruling may revive the reader route. Revival begins at the
original protocol's first unmet step: finish the private and accessible study
packet, obtain the external custody binding, freeze and commit a `not-run`
pilot attempt, and only then recruit or collect. No future evidence may be
backfilled into the withdrawn period, and no threshold may be chosen before a
valid completed pilot packet exists.

The 2026-08-09 reader-evidence protocol decision remains the controlling
method if revived. This decision changes only whether the current Book 1
program will execute it.
