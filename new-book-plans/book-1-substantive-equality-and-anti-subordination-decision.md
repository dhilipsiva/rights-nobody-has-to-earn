# Book 1 Substantive Equality and Anti-Subordination Decision

> **Status: author-ratified on 2026-08-07; formal implementation pending.**
> This decision selects Book 1's equality and anti-subordination baseline. It
> adds no predicate, rule, fact, pin, institution in law, chapter, release, or
> claim of present constitutional coverage.

**Statistical/diagnostic implementation note, 2026-09-12.** The separate
`book-1-official-statistics-contract.md` now implements bounded statistical
authority, the section 8 data wall, aggregate remedial presumptions and
rebuttal, private access, and independent request/nonresponse routes. Its
`statistics-source.json` and ordinary pins supplement the existing
person-held barriers. This does not implement the remaining accommodation,
positive-measure, historical-repair or equality-operation contracts. The
2026-09-12 verification ruling replaces retired receipt/audit workflows.
The chapter 1 section "Counting without ranking people" received separate

## 1. Decision

Book 1 adopts **universal substantive equality with an anti-subordination
layer**.

Every person is equal before and under the constitution, receives equal and
effective protection, and must be able to use every constitutional right and
public route without discrimination. Identical words or treatment do not satisfy
this guarantee where their purpose or predictable effect preserves a barrier,
dependency, stigma, segregation, exclusion, or domination.

The guarantee protects everyone. It does not require a claimant to prove that a
group has always been subordinated before arbitrary discrimination can be
challenged. Where reliable evidence shows durable structural disadvantage, the
constitution imposes stronger duties to prevent recurrence, remove the barrier,
and repair its continuing effects.

This is not a promise of identical lives, equal income, or equal outcomes. It is
a rule about equal constitutional status, genuinely usable rights, justified
distinctions, and the correction of institutions that reproduce subordination.

## 2. Forms of discrimination

A distinction, exclusion, restriction, preference, omission, barrier, or
practice is discriminatory when it is connected to a protected ground and has
the purpose or effect of impairing equal standing, protection, participation,
liberty, access to the material floor, due process, political equality, or
remedy.

The future rule families must distinguish these forms without requiring a
claimant to force one experience into only one category:

- **direct discrimination:** less favourable treatment, an adverse
  classification, or an exclusion connected to a protected ground; a motive to
  harm is unnecessary;
- **indirect discrimination:** an apparently neutral rule, design, omission, or
  practice that creates a disproportionate or predictable barrier;
- **systemic discrimination:** connected rules, practices, institutional
  arrangements, or persistent conduct that create relative disadvantage or
  privilege, including where no single decision supplies the whole injury;
- **multiple discrimination:** disadvantage operating on more than one ground;
- **intersectional discrimination:** grounds interacting inseparably to create a
  distinct form of disadvantage, with no single-ground comparator required;
- **associative discrimination:** disadvantage because of a person's
  relationship with, support for, or perceived connection to another person or
  group;
- **denial of accommodation:** failure to make an effective, necessary,
  person-specific adjustment within the accommodation contract in section 7;
- **segregation or exclusion:** imposed separation, inferior parallel access, or
  exclusion from common rights, institutions, or public-facing opportunities on
  a protected ground;
- **harassment:** unwanted ground-linked conduct whose purpose or reasonably
  predictable effect is intimidating, hostile, degrading, humiliating,
  coercive, or exclusionary; one grave act or a cumulative pattern may qualify;
  and
- **retaliation:** a material disadvantage imposed because somebody asserted,
  supported, investigated, reported, refused to participate in, or sought a
  remedy for discrimination.

Protected disagreement, criticism, belief, or expression is not harassment by
label alone. Threat, coercion, targeted degradation, or exclusion from a domain
that equality directly binds remains reviewable even when expressed in words.

## 3. Protected grounds

The protected-ground roster is deliberately open. It includes, without being
limited to:

- race, colour, descent, caste, ethnicity, and Indigenous or minority status;
- sex, pregnancy, reproductive status, sexual orientation, gender identity or
  expression, and sex characteristics;
- disability, health status, and genetic status or predisposition;
- age;
- language and communication mode;
- religion, belief, conscience, and political or other opinion;
- nationality, citizenship, national or social origin, and migration, refugee,
  asylum-seeking, displaced, or stateless status;
- family, marital, parental, caregiving, and birth status;
- housing or homelessness status;
- conviction, custody, or other legal status; and
- property, wealth, and socioeconomic status.

Protection reaches an actual, past, anticipated, attributed, perceived, or
associated ground, and any inseparable combination of grounds. An analogous
ground is protected where using it to distinguish people is irrelevant to the
claimed function or is capable of imposing arbitrary exclusion, stigma,
dependency, domination, or durable structural disadvantage.

Grounds are legal handles for detecting and repairing discrimination. They are
not constitutional descriptions of a person's worth, risk, entitlement, or
character. No person must adopt an identity label to retain standing, the floor,
core liberty, due process, political equality, or remedy.

## 4. Public and private reach

The equality guarantee directly binds every common/federal, regional, and local
body; every public office and institution; every contractor performing a public
function; and every actor exercising delegated public authority. Delegation or
privatisation cannot weaken the duty.

It also expressly binds private actors when they control access through:

- employment, recruitment, workplace organisation, or professional entry;
- sale, rental, financing, or administration of housing;
- education or training;
- health, care, disability support, or insurance;
- credit, lending, or other public-facing financial services;
- utilities, transport, communication, or another essential network;
- goods or services offered to the public; or
- a dominant platform, monopoly, gatekeeping intermediary, or dependency in
  which meaningful exit is unavailable.

Actor size may affect which accommodation is unduly burdensome and how a remedy
is delivered. It does not create permission to discriminate in a bound domain.
The separately author-ratified economic/private-order decision adds
function-specific public-like duties for independently established concentrated
or essential power; it does not retract this equality duty or make dominance a
condition of equality protection.

Public institutions must also prevent, investigate, and remedy private
interference with protected equality outside those directly bound domains, using
evidenced and least-restrictive responses that preserve harmless private choice.
This preserves the taxonomy's settled distinction between an express private
prohibition and the public duty to protect.

## 5. Protected private and associative life

The constitution does not prescribe friendship, affection, family formation,
belief, culture, worship, art, or intimate choice. A genuinely voluntary
cultural, affinity, safety, faith, or expressive association may organise around
its purpose where participation and exit are free and common rights and services
remain undiminished. Unless it also performs a directly bound function, the
association need not itself provide those common services; its existence or a
person's exit cannot reduce the services owed elsewhere.

The separate family, dependency, reproduction, and collective-plurality ruling
protects modular pair and plural family forms while keeping parentage, care,
household, support, property, and decision-agent roles legally distinct. It
permits more than two legal parents, creates no marital privilege, makes care
continuity public-first, and imposes no compulsory personal-care or inherited
support debt on adult kin. Every form remains fully subject to equality,
standing, due process, free exit, and remedy; no family, cultural, or religious
label supplies an exception.

The separate ecological, future-generation, commons, and non-human-animal
ruling creates a distinct individual environmental right, independent commons
and ceiling duties, and direct sentience-based protection for non-human animals
as Class 10 protected subjects. The individual environmental right is not a
material-floor item, and non-human animals do not acquire human standing or
equality status. This equality ruling nevertheless governs discriminatory
exposure and displacement and unequal access to information, participation,
protection, challenge, and remedy for people.

Protected voluntary belief, culture, association, and private choice is neither
proof of ecological or animal-protection harm nor an exemption from evidenced
harm. A rule affecting people remains subject to this decision's distinction,
burden, accessibility, and remedy tests. A serious-purpose animal-use claim may
not be granted or refused solely because a purpose is associated with a
protected group, and no cultural, religious, occupational, or economic label
waives the separate necessity and least-harm requirements.

A mission-linked status requirement is permissible only for a genuinely
expressive role where that characteristic is essential to performing the role.
The exception is role-specific. It does not extend to general employment,
commercial services, housing, education, health care, utilities, or another
public-facing or dependency-creating function merely because an organisation
claims an expressive, cultural, or religious identity.

Imposed status segregation and inferior parallel institutions are prohibited.
A voluntary affinity, cultural, or safety space is not imposed segregation when
it preserves free choice and exit, does not assign inferior status, and does not
replace or diminish equal access to common institutions. A claimed protective
separation remains subject to evidence, necessity, the least-discriminatory
alternative, and independent review.

## 6. Permissible distinctions and proof

The constitution uses structured tests rather than one loose balancing clause.

For a **direct status distinction**, the respondent must prove a genuine and
determining requirement of the particular function, reliable evidence, and the
absence of a less discriminatory effective alternative. Customer preference,
stereotype, administrative convenience, inherited practice, speculative risk,
or cost alone is insufficient.

For an **indirect distinction**, the respondent must prove:

1. a public or otherwise constitutionally compatible purpose;
2. reliable evidence connecting the measure to that purpose;
3. necessity rather than convenience;
4. no equally effective, less discriminatory means; and
5. proportionality between the remaining burden and the compatible purpose.

No distinction may balance away universal standing, the material floor, core
liberties, due process, equal political weight, or an effective remedy. An
express constitutional distinction remains confined to its ratified purpose:
ordinary residence and general adulthood govern the popular ballot, so minors
and nonresidents receive no ballot while every adult ordinary resident receives
one equal ballot; equal territorial weight belongs only to the Regions Council's
limited mandate. Neither exception creates a general status hierarchy, and
citizenship or migration classification adds no distinction among ordinary
residents.

Progressive taxation, need- or urgency-based support, accessibility resources,
genuine function-specific qualifications, and lawful positive measures are not
discriminatory merely because they treat unlike circumstances differently. Each
still remains reviewable for pretext, overbreadth, and effects outside its lawful
purpose.

Accommodation, need-responsive support, and positive measures do not use the
ordinary adverse direct-distinction test. Accommodation follows section 7.
Positive measures follow section 9's remedial objective, evidence, necessity,
proportionality, review, and end-condition contract; they need not pretend that a
protected characteristic is a determining qualification for the underlying
role.

A claimant establishes a prima facie case with credible evidence of adverse
treatment, a barrier, an exclusion, or a relevant group pattern connected to a
protected ground. The burden then shifts to the respondent to disclose the
measure's actual basis and prove the applicable test. Intent and a single close
comparator are unnecessary.

That shift governs civil, administrative, constitutional, and other remedial
equality proceedings. A diagnostic pattern never reverses a criminal burden,
proves individual guilt, authorises punishment, or establishes an adverse
individual fact.

Where decisive facts are wholly or mainly controlled by the respondent,
withholding, destruction, or unexplained absence of those facts cannot defeat the
claim. The future evidence contract must define the resulting presumption,
challenge, and correction route without treating missing claimant documentation
as proof against the claimant. Bare absence from a supplied record is not itself
proof of withholding or discrimination. An adverse inference requires
authenticated notice, a real opportunity to respond, a source-bound deadline, an
authorised finding of non-production or destruction, and review. Interim
continuity protects the claimant while that process runs.

## 7. Accessibility and reasonable accommodation

Accessibility is a proactive, systemic duty. Public systems and directly bound
private systems must use universal and inclusive design across physical,
sensory, cognitive, linguistic, digital, geographic, financial, and procedural
access. Equal standing may require unequal resources.

Reasonable accommodation is an individualized duty arising when a person needs
an effective adjustment to use a right, service, role, or procedure. A request is
not the only possible trigger: the duty also arises where the responsible actor
knows or reasonably should know that a barrier requires engagement. The actor
must consult the affected person, consider the person's stated requirements,
offer an effective adjustment, give reasons, and provide a prompt challenge
route. The person does not bear the accommodation's cost.

An accommodation may be refused only when the duty-bearer proves that it is
ineffective, impossible, or imposes a disproportionate or undue burden after
considering the resources and capacities of the responsible institution as a
whole, available public support, effects on other rights-holders, and effective
alternatives. An isolated office, contractor, branch, or budget line cannot
manufacture inability by withholding resources.

Procedural access to standing, urgent floor support, voting, challenge, hearing,
and remedy requires an effective alternate route; local incapacity or reviewer
silence cannot close it. If one provider genuinely cannot supply the exact
adjustment, the public continuity duty must secure an effective alternative
without making the person restart, surrender privacy, accept inferior status, or
lose the underlying right. Substitute access must be equivalent in timeliness,
quality, dignity, privacy, and cost to the person.

Accessibility and accommodation are continuing equality duties. They are not
temporary positive measures and do not expire merely because a corrective quota
or programme ends.

## 8. Equality diagnostics and the data wall

Book 1 authorises equality diagnostics only for a declared equality purpose and
under necessity, minimisation, privacy, security, access, challenge, correction,
retention, deletion, independent-methodology, and anti-retaliation controls.
Identity evidence used for diagnostics must remain separated from the canonical
constitutional person record and from operational records used to decide
standing, floors, liberty, political weight, sanctions, risk, or worth.

Privacy-safe aggregate and intersectional patterns may establish a rebuttable
presumption, open an independent audit, and support systemic correction. They do
not establish that every individual decision was discriminatory and may never
produce an individual entitlement or adverse verdict by themselves.

The operational design must protect small groups and unusual intersections from
re-identification, permit people to inspect and correct person-level data where
such data lawfully exists, and publish methods, uncertainty, exclusions, and
known undercount without exposing individuals. Non-response or refusal to join a
diagnostic dataset cannot reduce any constitutional right.

Ecological, commons, and animal-protection evidence associated with a person or
community retains this data wall, including purpose limitation, minimisation,
privacy, correction, challenge, and anti-retaliation. A disparity in exposure
or enforcement may open an equality audit; it may not become a reusable
identity, risk, worth, floor, sanction, or eligibility score. Evidence about a
protected animal remains governed by the separate Class 10 contract.

Statistical collection, linkage, calibration, privacy technology, sampling,
thresholds, and empirical evaluation belong to Book 2. Nibli may reason from an
authenticated, contestable diagnostic finding supplied as a premise. It may not
be credited with discovering a population disparity, authenticating identity,
or proving that an institution acted.

## 9. Positive measures

Lawful positive measures are permitted and are required where reliable evidence
shows that ordinary equal treatment, accessibility, and accommodation are
insufficient to dismantle continuing structural disadvantage. Their purpose is
substantive equality, not reward, collective guilt, permanent preference, or a
new rank.

A positive measure must state:

- the evidenced barrier or continuing disadvantage;
- the equality objective and affected domain;
- why ordinary rules and less targeted measures are insufficient;
- the eligible class and minimally necessary evidence;
- the measure, its expected equality effect, and its effect on other rights;
- the authorised decision-maker, independent reviewer, and challenge route;
- its monitoring evidence and source-bound temporal status; and
- the condition showing that the objective has been sustainably achieved.

Permissible measures include barrier removal, targeted outreach, investment,
support, restitution, training, placement targets, and bounded quotas. Ordinary
law selects the compatible means and operational values. The constitution does
not set a quota percentage or reparation formula.

For political representation, measures may govern candidate lists, nominations,
outreach, and appointments while preserving one equal ballot, overall
proportionality, and the ratified chamber structure. In the People's Assembly,
regional legislatures, and ordinary local representative bodies, this ruling
creates no protected-ground separate electorate, unequal vote, or reserved-seat
exception. The separately ratified 2026-08-08 collective-plurality contract now
governs internal membership and selection through self-identification plus lawful
collective acceptance. Such a body may not reduce anyone's standing, individual
rights, access to common services, or equal ballot in general
government. Appointment measures must also preserve open nominations, reasoned qualification review,
divided selectors, staggered nonrenewable mandates, cause-only removal, and the
legal incompatibility of majority appointment control by one current government,
chamber, party coalition, profession, or appointing source.

A separately enacted measure may use voluntary self-description where feasible
and only minimally necessary, contestable evidence for its bounded eligibility
decision. That evidence remains purpose-limited and outside the reusable
constitutional person record. It may not become a standing, floor, sanction,
risk, or worth field, and an inaccessible document cannot be the sole route to
eligibility. Declining voluntary self-description creates no adverse inference;
where a person seeks the bounded measure, an accessible alternative evidence and
challenge route must exist.

A measure continues only while current, independently reviewable evidence shows
that its equality objective remains unmet and the measure remains necessary and
proportionate. It ends when the objective has been sustainably achieved. A
calendar alone neither ends nor renews it; its future formal rule needs a
source-bound temporal contract naming the evidence version, reviewer, affected-
party challenge, alternate reviewer, and fresh-evaluation boundary. Review
silence cannot certify continuation. Without a current continuation finding, the
measure may not authorise a new preference or enlarge its scope. A predeclared,
bounded continuity rule may preserve already-acquired positions and prevent
abrupt claimant harm while alternate review proceeds; it cannot renew the
measure indefinitely. Ordinary equality, accessibility, accommodation, and
accrued remedies continue regardless. Book 1 cannot claim that an outside clock,
review, alternate, or replacement measure arrives.

## 10. Historical barriers and repair

Public institutions must identify and dismantle continuing effects of slavery,
colonisation, caste, segregation, dispossession, forced displacement,
institutionalisation, discriminatory law or administration, and other evidenced
systems of subordination. Repealing an old rule does not complete that duty where
its barriers or losses remain operative.

Democratic law may choose compatible means including restitution, community or
institutional investment, expungement, reparation, access audits, public truth
and record correction, and institutional redesign. Each means remains bound by
evidence, affected-person participation, privacy, due process, equality, the
floor, core liberties, and independent review. This decision supplies no fixed
beneficiary roster, valuation, debt-cancellation rule, apology, memorial, or
reparation formula.

Correction, sealing, or expungement of an adverse personal record must preserve
the provenance required to audit official action and the permanent historical
public answerability of office-holders. Privacy and non-use can protect a person
without rewriting the fact that public power acted or erasing a public official's
answerability. Any sealing or deletion remains subject to a lawful record-
disposition, provenance, and historical-answerability contract.

Reconciliation and forgiveness cannot be compelled. Historical repair addresses
continuing constitutional harm; it does not assign inherited personal guilt.

## 11. Standing, institutions, and remedies

An affected person may initiate equality review directly or through a supporter
or representative of their choice. A qualified association and the independent
rights advocate or ombud may bring representative or systemic proceedings.
Representation may not erase the affected person's voice, settle away their
floor or core rights, or expose their identity without lawful necessity.

The ratified institutions divide the work:

- administrative decision-makers must prevent and correct discrimination in
  their own lawful remit but may not supply final review of themselves;
- the rights advocate or ombud may receive complaints, assist claimants,
  investigate patterns, and initiate systemic review;
- audit and integrity functions may test public systems and report patterns;
- ordinary courts provide case-specific relief; and
- the Constitutional Court gives final general rulings on unconstitutional
  rules, public structures, and democratic-corridor violations.

No equality body may assert the decisive facts, adjudicate them, execute the
result, audit itself, and provide final remedy alone. An unavailable or silent
reviewer requires a predeclared alternate route; silence is never approval.

Individual remedies may include interim continuity, cessation, protection,
effective accommodation or substitute access, restoration of a lost status or
opportunity, record correction or deletion, restitution, compensation,
rehabilitation, and reparation. The route must protect claimants, supporters,
witnesses, and investigators from retaliation.

Systemic remedies may stop a practice, reopen or re-audit materially related
cases, require institutional redesign, preserve services while correction
occurs, monitor recurrence, publish privacy-safe reasons and results, and require
measures directed at non-repetition. A related case may reopen only where a
publicly reasoned shared defect is materially relevant, with notice, voice, and
individual review; no systemic finding automatically supplies personal
liability. A structural order may identify the unlawful condition, required
constitutional result, and bounded reporting or monitoring. The responsible
democratic or administrative body chooses among lawful means; a court may not
run ordinary administration. Ordinary courts supply case-specific relief;
the Constitutional Court alone gives final general invalidation or suspension of
an unconstitutional rule. An ombud or rights advocate may initiate and support a
case but not adjudicate or execute it; audit and integrity bodies may monitor but
not impose the final remedy. No institution may promise that recurrence becomes
impossible.

No remedy may remove another person's standing, material floor, core liberty,
due process, or equal political voice. No adverse finding or sanction may rest
solely on group membership or inherited guilt. Personal sanctions require their
own lawful evidence, relevance, proportionality, review, and remedy contract;
ordinary law supplies their form.

## 12. Federal and democratic effect

The equality baseline binds the common/federal, regional, and local tiers and
every intergovernmental arrangement. A subnational constitution or law may
provide stronger protection. It may not lower the common baseline, use
subsidiarity to preserve exclusion, or make portability depend on a protected
ground. Equalisation must respond to unequal capacity without converting a
place or population into an inferior constitutional class.

The common equality power remains limited to the enumerated guarantee,
portability, equalisation, and cross-boundary correction. It does not absorb
regional residual or protected local competence where those tiers can satisfy
the common baseline; subsidiarity remains justiciable in both directions.

Ordinary law, initiatives, constitutional amendments, secession terms, and
emergency measures remain inside the unamendable corridor. The public-safety
ruling of 2026-08-08 strengthens rather than qualifies that: it creates no
derogation clause, so an emergency measure is an ordinary measure judged by the
ordinary tests, and it adds the categorical refusals on force, absolute
non-refoulement, the ban on collective expulsion, prompt independent judicial
review of detention, and the right to an effective remedy to the corridor
itself. Nationality, citizenship, immigration status, documentation, and manner
of entry gate nothing and join the forbidden priority keys. A discriminatory
majority result does not become valid through turnout, territorial support,
delay, or reviewer silence. The future compatibility route must test both a
proposal's text and its bounded semantic effects. The unamendable corridor
protects equal standing, effective non-discrimination, the named minimum reach,
accessibility, and effective remedy. It does not freeze statistical methods,
quota percentages, workflows, or remedial techniques; a technique may be
replaced only while equal or stronger protection remains.

## 13. Book 1, Book 2, and formal migration

Book 1 owns the equality definitions, protected people, directly bound actors,
prohibited conduct, permissible-distinction tests, evidence and burden rules,
data wall, accommodation invariant, positive-measure authority and end
condition, review, continuity, individual remedy, systemic correction, and
failure polarity.

Book 2 owns data collection and statistical methods; staffing, training, costs,
capacity, and workflows; accommodation operations; inspection and enforcement;
monitoring calendars; privacy technology; operational programme design within
the enacted legal contract; and empirical evaluation of disparity,
effectiveness, recurrence, and feasibility. Book 2 may not choose protected
reach, an eligible class, a quota or preference, rights effects, the burden rule,
or the continuation default.

Before a formal equality rule family lands:

1. write separate cards for equal status, each prohibited form, accessibility,
   accommodation, diagnostic evidence, positive measures, historical repair,
   individual remedy, and systemic correction;
2. split the work by direct legal effect across every applicable named class,
   including Class 2 for accessible floor delivery and Class 8 for territorial
   equality and allocation, rather than creating one omnibus equality predicate
   or rule family;
3. name every writer, evidence basis, authorised consumer, challenger, reviewer,
   continuity route, remedy, temporal status, negative test, and Book 2 handoff;
4. census all current and proposed producers and consumers before choosing
   predicates or reusing a status relation;
5. keep group evidence and person-level eligibility evidence in distinct,
   purpose-limited contracts;
6. test every express private duty beside its matching public prevention,
   investigation, continuity, and remedy duty; and
7. review every claim whose truth depends on the current absence of an
   equality or remedy reader, even where its chapter-local derivation is
   unchanged.

At minimum, the impact review covers the opening note, every numbered chapter,
Part V's State and rotation verdicts, `method.md`, every affected TRUE and FALSE
pin, every narrowness and counted-claim guard, and every additional artifact
found by the producer/consumer census. A claim is not preserved merely because
its own chapter-local derivation did not change.

`admits`, `derived_only`, Article 1's anti-sanction firewall, recognition's
non-operation, the no-score guards, and the current temporal assurance remain
distinct controls. A broad equality reader must not silently weaken, absorb, or
misdescribe them. Current family, home, maturity, conviction, custody,
official-status, placement, voiding, and recognition distinctions receive an
explicit retain, replace, narrow, or retire decision under the new tests.
Protection against conviction- or custody-based collateral subordination does
not erase lawful, individualized custody limits; those remain governed by their
own evidence, due-process, necessity, proportionality, review, and T3 contracts.

No new engine handoff is required merely to ratify this decision. If later
formal work exposes an engine limitation, file a neutral minimal probe then.
Statistical computation and institutional action remain external by design, not
unimplemented Nibli features.

## 14. Acceptance cases for later implementation

The later rule families and assurance programme must distinguish at least:

- direct exclusion and a neutral rule with a disproportionate effect;
- an institution-wide pattern and an inseparable intersectional claim;
- actual, perceived, anticipated, and associative grounds;
- an unregistered or undocumented claimant retaining standing and floor access;
- physical, sensory, cognitive, linguistic, digital, geographic, financial,
  and procedural barriers;
- a valid institution-wide undue-burden case and a local-budget pretext;
- public action, delegated action, a directly bound private service, intimate
  choice, and a genuinely expressive role;
- imposed segregation and a voluntary affinity or safety association;
- one grave harassment act, a cumulative hostile pattern, protected expression,
  and retaliation;
- aggregate disparity opening a presumption and audit without creating an
  individual verdict;
- a lawful quota and another lawful positive measure, premature withdrawal,
  unjustified continuation, review silence, bounded interim continuity,
  sustainable completion, and
  prohibited identity reuse;
- candidate-list or nomination measures preserving equal ballots and
  proportionality, plus separately ratified rights-bounded internal collective
  membership and selection;
- lawful record disposition preserving provenance and public answerability;
- an individual remedy, a properly and improperly reopened related case, a
  bounded structural order that does not administer the institution, and
  correction after repeated discrimination;
- a discriminatory law, initiative, amendment, regional rule, secession term,
  or emergency measure refused by the corridor; and
- current family, home, maturity, conviction, custody, and official-status
  distinctions surviving or failing the applicable test for stated reasons.

Passing a logical fixture proves only the conclusion from the supplied premises.
It does not prove that a population roster, identity claim, diagnostic result,
clock, institution, remedy, or positive measure exists or operates outside the
model.
