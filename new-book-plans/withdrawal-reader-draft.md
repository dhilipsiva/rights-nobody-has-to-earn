## When the finding itself turns out to be wrong

The markers in this chapter watch what the design does to people. There is a
second kind of failure, and it belongs here too: the finding that licensed the
doing was no good.

Take a restriction on what people may say or publish. Somebody established an
evidenced harm, an independent reviewer agreed, reasons were published, a
challenge route existed, and on that basis a public body was permitted to act.
Now an independent review establishes that one of those things was not so — the
harm ground was never evidenced, or the reach was drawn wider than the harm, or
the restriction was aimed at a viewpoint after all. What the record does next is
the whole content of this section.

The restriction stops. Not "is criticised", not "is scheduled for review" — the
conclusion that it was a reviewed restriction no longer follows, and with it
goes the permission to enforce, because enforcing rejoins the exact restriction
that was reviewed and there is no longer one to rejoin. A duty to review and
correct lands on the challenge reader, and a duty to publish the correction
lands on the body that imposed it.

The same shape appears wherever a record licenses something. A record kept for
one purpose, watched under one authorisation, fed to an automated decision: find
the holding defective and the watching and the automated decision stop with
it, because each of them rejoined the holding. A shortage that an independent review establishes was a
budget choice, a price, a delay, held stock, a monopoly, a provider that failed
or a decision not to buy any — the finding stops, the allocation built on it
stops, and what was withheld is owed back.

What does not happen is the point, and it is worth taking in turn.

Nothing happens to the person. Across every one of those sequences, somebody
stands in the record throughout — before the finding, while it stood, and after
it fell. They remain a person, they are still owed the floor, they still hold
the vote, and nothing in the collapse makes them a liar or a prisoner or a loser
of anything. A finding that was wrong about a restriction is not a finding about
them.

Nothing spreads. The defect names the exact record it is about. A defect against
one holding leaves another holding standing; a restriction reviewed for one
matter was never licensing the other one anyway. The design has no way to say
that a body's findings are generally unreliable, and this chapter is the wrong
place to wish for one: that would be a standing judgement about an institution,
written by whoever writes it, and every argument this book has made against
scoring people applies to scoring bodies.

And nothing is undone. The restriction stops; it does not unhappen. Whatever was
enforced while it stood was enforced. What the record produces is a duty to
correct and a duty to publish the correction — which is a determination, and
this chapter has already been honest about what determinations are worth. The
design can now say that a finding was wrong and that somebody owes a correction.
It still cannot say that the correction was made.
