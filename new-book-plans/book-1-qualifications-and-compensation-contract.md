# Book 1 Qualifications and Compensation Contract

**Status: Specified contract card, session-drafted and author-approved
2026-09-05, including the choices in section 11.** This is not a new author
ruling. It is the
contract card for the qualification, licensing, compensation, and incentive
interface under the ratified economic settlement of 2026-08-07 — its work,
labour, qualifications, and incentives section, its property section's wage-
and-equity sentence, and its insolvency section's fraud clause — and under the
taxonomy's fifth structural refusal. It reopens none of them. Where it chooses
between shapes those rulings leave open, the choice is named in section 11 and
stands only once approved.

## 1. Decision

A certificate is a function-only attestation: a body authorised as certifier
for a person attests a named function for that person, the body is not the
person, and the conclusion is read by no other rule — it gates no work, no
floor, no standing, no vote, no liberty, and no recognition. Mandatory
licensing stays exactly where the economic family put it, as a separately
justified public power, and this card adds no gate to it. Above the floor,
compensation of every named kind — wage, profit, return, grant, prize,
subsidy, and bounded incentive — is one record relation with the kind as a
constant, and it concludes only when an attester authorised for that person
and distinct from the payer attests the same kind at a stated basis, under the
Court's absent individualised finding of compensation fraud on that basis. The
compensation conclusion is read by no rule; no compensation rule reads
recognition, the credibility verdict, or the loss leaf. Neither a certificate
nor a compensation conclusion passes to a child, a spouse, or anyone else. A
Court finding of compensation fraud on an attested basis concludes restitution
by the person to the payer for that basis and nothing wider, and touches no
floor entitlement.

## 2. What already exists, and is extended rather than restated

The mandatory occupational licence power carries the serious-risk trigger,
relevant standards, accessible alternative proof, portability, anti-cartel
safeguards, function-only effect, certification preference, and the ban on any
person, floor, worth, or political effect; licence withdrawal and temporary
licence control carry their own contracts; expiry ends restrictive effect while
ordinary rights survive. The economic barriers already refuse recognition as a
property qualification, qualification spillover into personal status, and any
coupling of compensation to recognition, the credibility verdict, or the loss
leaf; public status including a licence cannot pass by succession. The
acceptance matrix already executes valid and pretextual licensing, alternative
proof, credential-cartel safeguards, expiry, and compensation independence.
What did not exist was the positive side: a certificate that can be shown to be
function-only because it derives and is then read by nothing; a compensation
conclusion that can be shown to arrive from a record and an independent
attestation and from nothing else; and executable tests for inherited
advantage, metric gaming, and proportionate restitution.

## 3. Contract fields

| Field | Contract |
| --- | --- |
| Holder | Every person for whom a certifier attests a function, and every person to whom a payer has promised compensation of a named kind. Personhood is not a premise of either rule. |
| Source | For a certificate, the certifying body and the certification scope. For compensation, the promise record's kind and the attester's stated basis; a promise of one kind attested as another kind derives nothing. |
| Writer | The certificate record is the certifier's attestation, and the certifier must be distinct from the person. The compensation record is the payer's promise; the attestation is by a writer authorised as compensation attester for that person and distinct from the payer. Both distinctness conditions are conjuncts in the rules. |
| Evidence rule | Certificate: authorised certifier plus attestation at certification scope. Compensation: promise plus authorised independent attestation of the same kind at a basis, plus the Court's absent fraud finding on that basis. Either half alone derives nothing. |
| Scope and permitted effects | Two derived conclusions, a certificate and a compensation, each read by no other rule. Neither carries an amount, a rank, a tier, or a comparison; amounts are outside evidence. |
| Independent reader | The attester is the independent reader of the basis; the Court is the seat for the individualised fraud finding; the integrity-and-audit function and the ombudsperson keep their general challenge routes. |
| Privacy boundary | The compensation record is readable only by the compensation and restitution rules, enforced by a verifier guard with a watched failing control. A metric or performance attestation has no reader at all and cannot reach the floor, standing, the vote, liberty, or recognition. |
| Challenge | Record, attestation, and finding are each contestable; nothing in this family retracts, because derivation is monotone. |
| Correction | A corrected promise or attestation is a replacement entry that derives on its own terms. |
| Carry | Adjudicated compensation carries under the existing economic benefit carry contract; this card adds no carry route. |
| End condition | The certificate and the compensation conclusion derive while their records are supplied; the absence of a current attestation withholds and derives no loss. Licence expiry keeps its own contract. |
| Alternate route | None is needed for a certificate, which gates nothing. Wage and pension protection through insolvency keep the economic family's routes. |
| Continuity | The floor is never a premise, a consequence, or a casualty of any state of this family; a person with no certificate and no compensation record keeps everything. |
| Remedy | Restitution for adjudicated compensation fraud is basis-bound and person-bound; individual remedy otherwise runs through the general economic remedy interface. |
| Failure polarity | Withholding on missing or mismatched evidence, never loss. The fraud guard is shield polarity: an accusation not yet adjudicated protects the person; only the Court's individualised finding on the attested basis blocks that compensation and concludes that restitution. |

## 4. The named instruments

Wage, profit, return, grant, prize, subsidy, and incentive — the kinds the
economic ruling lists — are constants on the one compensation record. Adding a
kind is a constant, not a predicate. Restitution is a constant on the same
conclusion relation, with the person as payer and the payer as recipient.

## 5. Formal migration contract

- **Add exactly one writable relation.** The compensation record uses the
  corpus name `promise` at arity three: promiser, promised, beneficiary. The
  second place is the instrument kind. `pay` stays reserved to contribution
  records and its guard is untouched; `compensate`, `hire`, `employ`,
  `contract`, `award`, `prize`, and `certify` are not corpus names; `deserve`
  is refused again for its wage-for-work places.
- **Add two derived relations.** The compensation conclusion uses `provide` at
  arity three: provider, provided, recipient. The certificate uses `grant` at
  arity three: agent, action, resource — the certifying body, the function,
  the person. Both are conclusion-only and read by nothing.
- **Reuse the existing role, attestation, distinctness, and seat idiom.**
  Certifier and compensation attester are role constants on the existing
  authorised-role relation; attestations are the existing two-place-and-scope
  relation; the Court is the seat for the fraud finding exactly as it is for
  the contribution fraud finding.
- **Three rules.** The certificate rule; the compensation rule with the
  negated Court finding; and the restitution rule, which reads the promise,
  the attestation at a basis, and the Court's finding on that same basis.
- **One verifier guard extension.** The engine accepts a rule conditioning
  confinement on the absence of a compensation record, for the same reason it
  accepts one on the contribution record. The purpose-limited-record guard
  therefore covers both `pay` and `promise`, each with its own watched failing
  fixture, and the no-reader list gains `insure`, `provide`, and `grant`.
- **Refused forms:** any amount, ratio, tier, or ranking; a certificate read
  as a gate on work or on anything else; a metric or performance record read
  by any rule; compensation or a certificate passing by parentage, marriage,
  or succession; any compensation rule reading `reward`, `false`, or `lose`;
  restitution wider than the attested instrument and found basis; any effect on
  the floor.

## 6. Executable cases

| Case | Formal behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Valid certificate | Authorised certifier, attestation at certification scope, certifier distinct from the person: the certificate derives and nothing else moves. |
| Self-certification | The person as their own certifier derives nothing. |
| Uncertified person | Identical floor, standing, liberty, and recognition conclusions to a certified person. |
| Compensation of each kind | A promise of a kind plus an independent attestation of that kind derives that kind and no other. |
| Payer self-attestation | The payer attesting its own promise derives nothing. |
| Metric gaming | An attestation of a metric where an incentive was promised derives nothing; no rule reads a metric attestation. |
| Inherited advantage | A parent's certificate or compensation derives nothing for the child. |
| Recognition, credibility, loss | Never read by any rule of this family; recognition stays unchanged for every compensated or certified person. |
| Proportionate fraud restitution | The Court's finding on the attested basis blocks that compensation and concludes restitution by the person to the payer; a finding on another basis does neither; the floor is untouched throughout. |
| Zero records | No certificate, no compensation, no restitution, and every floor entitlement intact. |

## 7. What this does not establish

No certificate proves competence, no compensation conclusion proves payment,
no restitution conclusion proves repayment, and no amount exists anywhere.
Whether a certifier or attester exists or acts, whether wages are adequate, and
whether anything is paid are Book 2's questions or liveness claims that may
never be Derived.

## 8. Book 1, Book 2, and the engine

Book 1 owns the interface. Book 2 owns certification and licensing
administration, examination and standards bodies, wage-setting, incentive
programme design and evaluation, restitution enforcement, and every empirical
question. The engine derives bounded conclusions from supplied records and
performs no institutional act.

## 9. Assurance posture

The interface claim is a safety claim, Derived through formal entailment over
supplied records, with an enumerated non-extension clause: competence,
adequacy, payment, repayment, and operation. The operations claim is
Unestablished and routed to Book 2. They are separate ledger rows.
