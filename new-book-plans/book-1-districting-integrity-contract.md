# Electoral districting integrity

Ruling D of the 2026-09-09 integrity decision. The common
`book-1-integrity-record-contract.md` supplies writers, current source,
privacy, failure polarity, review, alternate, correction, end and remedy.

`IntegrityDistrictPurposeVocabulary` closes the purposes to
`PartyOrCoalitionEntrenchment`, `IncumbentEntrenchment`, and
`ProtectedGroundDilution`. A finding must name the affected electoral plan
and the party/coalition, incumbent, or protected ground targeted. An outcome
is not evidence of purpose by itself: the disposition must attest that the
purpose was independently established on evidence.

The lawful-plan contract requires examination of every named purpose and
positive clearance. Its conclusion is
`authority(FSBOD_06, ElectoralDistrictPlan, plan)`. A current purpose finding
withholds that permission and owes independent electoral review and
correction. Protected-ground dilution also owes referral to substantive-
equality review; it supplies no new equality metric or individual finding.

The electoral configuration in state-form card 10 now binds the district
plan and the same examined purposes. The abandoned draft put these fields on
card 35, which allocates court and oversight appointments, not electoral
districts. They have been moved to the electoral branch. No court-seat
allocation is treated as a district plan. `DistrictMagnitudeScope`, the
existing proportionality testability fields, and delegated numeric choices
remain unchanged. There is no arithmetic or compute-backend route.

Executable cases are `tests/pins/integrity/district-plan/`,
`district-finding/`, and the state-form FSACC-022 cases. The adverse-finding
sequence shows a previously permitted plan losing permission; the equality
case checks the separate review duty. Removing independent review permits the
self-review control only in its explicit counterfactual.

Book 1's reader consequence is a ban on entrenchment and protected-ground
dilution with independent challenge, not a promise that boundaries are fair.
Book 2 owns drawing, evidence collection, methodology, staffing, hearing,
publication, implementation and any measurement chosen by ordinary law.
