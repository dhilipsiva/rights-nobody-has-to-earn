## 2. Question 1 — the thesis is adopted in the restated structural form

The proposed sentence — *"A system owes safety and dignity to the people most
vulnerable to its failures, and designing for them—rather than for a presumed
typical user—tends to surface defects that would otherwise harm many others
too."* — is adopted **as restated**, not verbatim. The ratified form
constraints, each traceable to the brief:

- **The verb is structural, never axiological.** The thesis states what the
  design does: the floor's absence can never be a lawful ground for sanction,
  and the interfaces are built to demand nothing of the person receiving them
  (`new-book-plans/book-1-thesis-framing-and-second-stress-case-brief.md::**Available restatement, keeping the author's meaning and losing the collision:**`).
  "Owes" does not survive verbatim: a thesis whose verb is *owes* on value
  grounds imports exactly the justification the floor's admission criterion
  refuses — the source says in terms that the formalism makes the commitment
  precise and unretractable, never right.
- **`dignity` may not become a load-bearing thesis noun.** It is an undefined
  bare prose word in the current source, and promoting an undefined term to the
  most permanent point in the book is the failure mode the title ruling already
  caught once and ruled against.
- **`safety` may not be a thesis word.** It already names one of the seven
  fixed functional criteria bound to the reference envelope, which is Book 2's
  to calibrate; reusing it invites the reader to hear the envelope criterion.
- **The methodological half splits into two claims**, per the one-posture rule:
  the general claim ("designing for them tends to surface defects") takes
  **Reasoned at best**, permanently weaker than Derived and never citable as
  it; beside it stands a **named bounded instance** that is executable — the
  universal-standing family, where designing standing for the person who cannot
  produce a record yielded routes covering the foundling, the undocumented
  adult, the person present without documents, and the person under effective
  control. The honest thesis form is *method plus a measured instance*, never
  *it tends to* alone.
- **"Designing for the most vulnerable" has exactly one lawful
  implementation: unconditionality plus interfaces that require nothing of the
  recipient.** Never targeting. A rule keyed on "is vulnerable" needs a status
  entry naming who is, which is the finding-with-no-finder class chapter 1
  concedes, and both the vulnerability-score refusals and the
  temporary-assessment exclusion bind it.
- **Placement is the exempt elements only** — the opening note and Part V,
  beside the existing stress-test framing. The sentence is normative plus
  empirical and cannot pass the derivation gate; that is a placement fact, not
  a demotion.
- **The exact wording follows the 2026-08-20 prose-supply protocol** at the
  sequenced prose step. A session may present a candidate; only explicit author
  approval of that exact displayed version makes it canonical. `tmp.txt` is
  optional scratch context, not the approval record. This record still fixes
  the form constraints.

## 3. Question 2 — the infant joins as the paired second stress case

The infant without a reliable caregiver joins the book as a **second stress
case paired with the prisoner**, stated in the opening note's existing
stress-test section (`book-1/00-opening-note.md::### Why the prisoner appears so early`)
as testing the opposite failure mode, and carried in the pinned portfolio at
whatever density the landed rules actually support.

The pair is the argument, and the brief's table is the ratified statement of
why — the two subjects fail in opposite directions:

| | the prisoner | the infant without a caregiver |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the state has | acted upon them | not acted upon them |
| presence in the record | necessary | not guaranteed |
| floor actualities that derive | shelter and expression | none |
| formalisation | dense, mostly protective limits on custody | barriers no rule reads |

Either subject alone is a monoculture, and the thesis's own claim is what says
so.

**The case's four durable jobs**, ratified as its consumers:

1. **The delivery-lifecycle acceptance test** — the sharpest subject for the
   recipient-side receipt gap
   (`TODO.md::Resolve receipt for a recipient who cannot acknowledge through any channel.`).
2. **The firewall-scope case** — the measured boundary where confinement for a
   missing family status loads while confinement for a missing floor right is
   refused
   (`TODO.md::The same firewall boundary already bites on an existing conclusion through an`).
3. **The standing-root liveness case** — the natural subject for exercising the
   dormant universal-standing family when the pinned portfolio is rebalanced.
4. **One named row** in the reader-experience coverage ledger under family,
   dependency and care, at whatever posture and trajectory the landed rules
   support — not a posture chosen in advance.

## 4. "Primary stress test if possible" — the ratified reading

The landed rulings partition what "primary" can mean, and the conditional
resolves against that partition:

- **Granted now — framing primacy.** In the exempt elements, the thesis is
  about designing for the person most vulnerable to the system's failures, and
  the caregiverless infant is that thesis's sharpest subject — the person
  public power has *not* acted upon. The stress-test framing may lead with the
  infant, with the prisoner as the paired case testing the opposite direction.
  This is exempt prose and lands at the sequenced prose step only through
  exact-version author approval under the 2026-08-20 protocol.
- **Not allocatable — substance primacy.** Chapter order is computed from the
  dependency stratification; portfolio density follows what the landed rule
  families support; and "the design's hardest stress test" is a measured
  property of the source. Today that property holds of the prisoner, because
  custody is what the present source formalises most deeply. Whether the
  infant *becomes* the structurally central case is an outcome of the delivery
  and receipt families landing — re-measured then, never asserted in advance.
  The opening-note section's hardest-stress-test sentence is a source-property
  claim and must track the source when it is re-audited at the prose step.
- **Refused and unchanged — the through-line.** The agency clause
  (`TODO.md::The agency clause is what decides any proposed second lead case`),
  the anti-monoculture guards, and the narrative-register ruling stand.
  An infant can occupy only the *receives* and *is acted upon* postures, so it
  cannot carry a protected private or civic domain. Primary-in-framing is not
  through-line-in-substance, and the former Option 2B remains unavailable
  without amending three landed rulings — which this ruling does not do.

## 5. Refused by name

So nothing trickles back one piece at a time:

- the infant as the book's through-line;
- a vulnerability score, ranking, or targeting status in any record that
  reaches standing, floor, liberty, or remedy;
- interior state attributed to the infant — the register ruling's
  highest-risk subject, because the reader supplies the affect without the
  prose asserting it;
- "primary" read as an allocation of chapter order, pinned-portfolio density,
  or the hardest-stress-test property;
- the thesis verbatim with the value collision carried openly (the former
  Option 1B);
- `dignity` as a load-bearing thesis noun; `safety` as a thesis word;
- settling the recipient-side receipt gap or the family-status firewall gap
  here — each keeps its owning tracker item, and in particular the receipt gap
  may not be closed by letting a provider attest on the recipient's behalf;
- any edit to the grandfathered Part V passage that bypasses its register,
  trace, or exact-version author-approval requirements.

## 6. Sequencing, which is forced and unchanged

The brief's order stands
(`new-book-plans/book-1-thesis-framing-and-second-stress-case-brief.md::## 7. Sequencing, which is forced`):
the delivery and receipt families land first, including a resolution of the
receipt gap; the pinned case portfolio is rebalanced second; the
reader-experience coverage ledger is built third; and only then is a framing
candidate prepared and made canonical through exact-version author approval.
Ruling now settles the frame so it cannot be
settled by accident later; it does not accelerate any implementation step.
Adopting the framing in prose before the source can carry it would produce a
book whose thesis its own source cannot support, and the receipt and
counted-claims gates would catch it at the end of the program rather than the
start.
