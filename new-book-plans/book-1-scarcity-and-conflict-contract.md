# Physical scarcity, priority, and cross-domain conflict

Implementation contract for the author-directed TODO item of the same name,
under the 2026-08-07 economic ruling's physical-scarcity contract, the
2026-08-08 ecological and protective rulings that repeat it, and the
2026-09-12 verification ruling. It supplies the conditions for calling a
shortage physical and for allocating under one, and the typed resolution of the
named cross-domain conflicts. It counts nothing and holds nothing.

## Most shortages are failures, and they are named as such

The refused grounds are a closed vocabulary: budget choice, price exclusion,
administrative delay, artificial withholding, monopoly, provider failure and
refusal to procure. A reviewed finding that a shortage is independently
established to be one of these withholds the finding it targets and creates
duties to treat it as failure and to restore or procure what was withheld. Each
of the seven has its own named case.

The admissible ground is one: authenticated, contestable evidence specific to
this resource and this population. A reviewed physical-scarcity finding
additionally requires that alternatives, reserves, substitution, coordination,
replenishment and mutual assistance were considered; public reasons and
accessible notice; independent review; fresh reassessment evidence and a
source-bound end; and the express statement that no standing emergency power
follows. Its conclusions are the manager's authority to administer the shortage
and duties to publish and to hear challenge.

## What a finding licenses, and what it may never decide

Allocation is a dependent record that rejoins the actual finding — resource,
population, manager, version, period, jurisdiction, legal scope and end — so a
shortage found for one resource or one population licenses nothing about
another. It requires every constitutional minimum preserved wherever usable
supply permits, an effective usable equal share where one exists, never dividing
a threshold resource into equally useless pieces, no forbidden priority key and
no personal-worth ranking, rotation or lottery only among materially equal
claims, and surviving interim alternatives and continuity.

The permitted mitigation keys are closed: urgency, accessibility, imminent
irreversible harm, continuity harm, and individualized resource-specific benefit
after accommodation. The forbidden keys are declared and each has its own case
proving it allocates nothing — wealth, contribution, recognition, conviction,
family status, disability stereotype, expected productivity, social usefulness,
generalized lifespan, political favour, nationality, citizenship, immigration
status, documentation and manner of entry. The last five come from the
protective ruling, which added them when it put everyone within jurisdiction
inside a scarcity finding's named population.

A recorded shortfall is the third record. It is recorded **as failure, never as
a redefined minimum**, carries continuity, repair and reassessment duties and a
reader's duty to verify it was recorded as failure, and states that it is no
adverse fact about the person who went without.

## The other conflicts, resolved by a rule stated in advance

Six cross-domain conflicts are named and closed as a vocabulary: property
against floor or commons, expression against evidenced harm, privacy against
public accountability, local choice against portability, present claims against
future conditions, and emergency action against non-derogable protection. Each
carries its stated resolution, the express statement that the rule was stated in
advance rather than being a hidden priority, the survival of the non-derogable
protections and the corridor, and public reasons with challenge and independent
review. Each of the six has its own case.

## Polarity, separation and the person wall

Every conclusion is positive; a withheld record and a missing field conclude
nothing. Three mutually distinct attesters must agree and none may be the
manager; a challenge reader and an independent alternate are distinct from each
other, from the attesters and from the manager. Disagreeing attesters raise a
record ambiguity and nothing completes. Every head is `derived_only` upstream,
so a forged finding is refused at assertion.

Nothing concludes anything about a claimant. In the sequence case a person
stands beside a finding, an allocation and a recorded shortfall, and keeps
personhood, the floor debt and the ballot while deriving no voiding, no
confinement and no loss — before and after the shortage is established to have
been a choice.

## Relation to what was already there

`FSPOW_081` and `FSPOW_082` in the economic constitution already carried a
physical-scarcity finding and allocation with their review, alternatives,
allocation-record and temporal machinery, and the public-safety family binds
rationing to an unchanged finding. What was missing was the part the ruling
states most insistently and the formal source did not: the forbidden priority
keys by name, the equal-share and useless-division rules, the shortfall recorded
as failure, the refused grounds by name, and the typed cross-domain conflicts.
This family adds those and does not replace the existing cards.

## Classification and Book 2 handoff

The finding, the allocation and their oversight are Class 6 public functions;
the preserved minimum and the surviving continuity route protect Classes 1 and
2; reasons, challenge, review and the alternate are Class 4 fair process; the
record and its defects are Class 7. No new taxonomy class is introduced, and the
family adds no relation name, so the spine's predicate, derived-predicate and
stratum counts and the eight floor rights are unchanged.

Book 2 owns evidence collection and assurance, inventories, forecasts,
quantities, reserves, production, capacity, queues, workflows and empirical
evaluation. Nibli counts no stock, forecasts no supply, measures no shortage and
proves no procurement, delivery, restoration or repair.

## Executable cases and what they check

`scarcity-source.json` is the semantic input to `./generate.sh scarcity`. It
writes the `SCARCITY-AND-CONFLICT-RULES` block — 124 rules — and 362 isolated
cases under `tests/pins/scarcity/`. Every contract has positive, withheld,
per-scope omission, unauthorized-attester, stale, self-review, manager-as-reviewer,
fused-alternate, mismatched-version, period-drift and conflicting-value cases,
and every vocabulary value is exercised beside an unapproved one. The two
dependent contracts add missing-authorization and wrong-resource,
wrong-population, wrong-version, wrong-period and wrong-manager cases.

Three counterfactuals remove one premise each — the independent-review
distinctness, the ground vocabulary and the conflict vocabulary — and a fourth
removes the mitigation-key vocabulary, which is what the forbidden-key wall rests
on: its paired control shows wealth allocating once that membership check is
gone.

The integration cases name things one at a time rather than in bulk: each of the
fifteen forbidden priority keys, each of the seven refused grounds, each of the
six cross-domain conflicts, the finding refusing to cover another resource or
population, the sequence in which a finding, an allocation and a shortfall all
stop when the shortage is established to have been a choice, a request and its
certified nonresponse, and the forged-head refusals.
