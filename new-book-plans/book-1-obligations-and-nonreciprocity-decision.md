# Book 1 Obligations and Non-reciprocity Decision

**Author-ratified 2026-08-24.** This record settles Book 1's obligation
architecture: who may be bound, where a duty comes from, what survives breach,
conflict, delegation, or excuse, and why no right becomes payment for performing
a duty. It is the stable source and owner for the family after its tracker item
is removed.

The ruling is **ratified and, at the moment of writing, unimplemented**. It
creates no predicate, rule, pin, fixture, claim, power, body, status,
allocation, receipt, institutional act, delivered remedy, or public assurance
by itself. `OBL-B1-v1` remains the byte-exact prose-approval record; it is not a
constitutional source and this decision does not replace or revise it.

## 1. Decision

Book 1 adopts one source-bound, non-reciprocal obligation architecture.

Public institutions must respect protected choices and conditions; protect
against expressly covered public and private interference; fulfil positive
entitlements; continue protection and provision through dispute, breach,
delegation, or institutional failure; and provide review and remedy. When a
contractor performs a public function, the contractor receives the identical
duty for that function and the public principal remains bound. Delegation
creates neither public status nor authority in the contractor and never
discharges the principal.

A private duty exists only where an express substantive source creates it and
an independently reviewable record binds its exact reach. Subject matter,
relationship, dependency, ownership, market activity, or proximity alone never
creates horizontal constitutional effect.

Each obligation belongs to exactly one duty class: owed to a person; owed to a
Class 9 common or future condition; attached to a role; or assumed through a
bounded voluntary commitment. The class selects the appropriate breach and
remedy effects. It is never a priority key, a status hierarchy, a worth scale,
or a reason to discharge another duty.

No protected entitlement is consideration for duty performance. Work, payment,
identity, compliance, contribution, score, approval, role, civic duty, private
duty, voluntary commitment, or a failure in any of them cannot gate
personhood, an entitlement or public debt, political voice, evidence and
challenge, continuity, remedy, or the lawful source and process required for a
restrictive power. A separate lawful consequence remains possible only through
the affected right's or power's own source, evidence, process, review, and
remedy contract.

An excuse may shift only an exact slice of immediate performance for a bounded
period or review event. The original obligation, bearer accountability,
protected claim, prior breach, retained public-principal responsibility, and
remedies remain. Silence, convenience, strategic refusal, self-created
incapacity, conflicting evidence, self-certification, or an indefinite or
unreviewed record is no excuse.

Every formal finding receives a source-bound route to an independent recipient
with a duty to read, record receipt, choose only permitted actions, give
reasons, and expose review. Certified nonresponse is a positive finding with an
alternate escalation route. Common-cause investigation, responsible-control
correction, affected-case re-audit, and recurrence verification accompany but
never delay claimant continuity or individual relief. These are legal effects
over supplied records, not evidence that a reader, institution, remedy, or
recurrence control ever acts.

## 2. Source-bound origin and materialisation

Every duty origin binds the bearer, bearer mode, duty, standard, beneficiary or
protected object, exactly one duty class, public function or voluntary
commitment, record, exact origin, source, source version and epoch,
jurisdiction, legal scope, temporal bounds, challenge, correction, remedy, and
the independent source, record, and temporal reviewers.

An origin materialises only when the bearer-mode certificate and the class
certificate rejoin the same raw current-source tuple. A later rule for
delegation, conflict, excuse, breach, continuity, or remedy must repeat that
origin join. The compact reader-facing `obliged(bearer, duty, standard)`
conclusion is never sufficient provenance by itself. Divergent origin, class,
version, jurisdiction, scope, priority, or excuse records collide and fail
closed.

The existing two-place `obliged(reader, subject)` findings remain a compatibility
surface and feed the same source-bound reader route. The relation is
conclusion-only at every arity; neither spelling may be asserted as a ground
fact.

## 3. Public, delegated, and private reach

A public-duty record names the exact public function. Delegation can derive the
contractor's identical duty, standard, target, and function only while the
principal's same-origin duty, continuity, and remedy stay live. Contractor
performance does not transfer public status, lawful authority, source power, or
ultimate accountability.

Express-private evidence is a prerequisite, not an independent duty mint. It
must rejoin a substantive duty and class certificate from the same current
origin. A source that merely says a private actor is in a domain, relationship,
market, ownership position, or dependency relation establishes no duty.

## 4. Duty classes and remedies

The four classes have distinct effects and no fixed hierarchy:

- A duty owed to a person preserves continuity, reasons, independent review,
  restoration, and an individual remedy.
- A Class 9 common duty supports cessation, protection or restoration,
  accounting, and recurrence review. Each person's separately established harm
  remains separately remediable. The scientific evidence role is not the duty
  bearer, source, adjudicator, or remedy executor.
- A role duty supports recusal or reassignment, correction, accounting,
  affected-case review, and claimant continuity. Breach does not automatically
  punish the office-holder or remove status.
- A voluntary duty supports lawful performance, cure, unwinding,
  restitution or compensation, or exit. It never authorises indefinite personal
  service, loss of the floor, or waiver of a non-waivable protection.

## 5. Priority and conflict

Only a current, pair-specific, independently reviewed conflict record may
sequence performance. Duty class is forbidden as a priority key. Neither
original obligation is discharged, and protected entitlements and immediate
continuity take priority over the conflict disposition. Deferred or unmet
performance remains recorded for repair.

Missing, stale, divergent, or conflicting priority evidence creates no
preference. An independently certified tie or missing-priority finding assigns
continuity and review while preserving both duties. Rotation or lottery is
permitted only for one indivisible choice among materially equal claims; it is
not a general hierarchy and cannot make an unmet duty disappear.

## 6. Excuse

An excuse must bind the exact original duty and origin, a source-enumerated
impediment, the affected performance slice, onset, a finite end or review event,
independent evidence and reviewer, notice and challenge, an alternate route,
continuity, and remedy. The bearer cannot be the source, evidence authority, or
reviewer.

Only immediate performance moves. The original duty and accountability,
protected claim, prior breach, public-principal responsibility, and remedies
remain. Silence, convenience, strategic refusal, self-created incapacity,
conflicting evidence, or an indefinite or unreviewed record creates no excuse.

## 7. Finding-to-action accountability

Every current `err/2` finding kind must map exactly once to a route that binds
the finding kind, subject and case, independent recipient, receipt evidence,
permitted action set, reasons, review, positive nonresponse, alternate
escalation, continuity, individual remedy, review of prior affected decisions,
common-cause inquiry, responsible-control correction, affected-case re-audit,
recurrence verification, and non-delay of individual relief.

The repository checker enumerates the current finding rule heads and rejects an
uncovered kind, a wrong recipient, missing positive nonresponse, missing
alternate, or a route that lets systemic work gate individual relief. Formal
coverage proves only that the legal route follows from supplied records. It
does not prove that a reader receives the record, acts, gives reasons, repairs a
case, corrects a control, or prevents recurrence.

## 8. Formal migration contract

- Make `obliged` relation-wide `derived_only`. Preserve `obliged(reader,
  subject)` and expose `obliged(bearer, duty, standard)` as the typed public
  conclusion.
- Reuse the current-source rejoin, raw-tuple, witness, collision, and structural
  validation patterns already used by the state-form family. A compact status
  or duty conclusion never substitutes for the raw tuple.
- Reuse the material-floor, standing, fair-process, and consequential-record
  profiles as overlays. Adapt the existing liberty, economic-private-power, and
  Class 9 common profile field sets; do not duplicate their definitions.
- Add no claim, power, body, status, allocation, institutional proxy role, or
  omnibus duty predicate. Institutions, contractors, principals, readers, and
  alternate recipients are fields in the source-bound profiles.
- Reuse the existing protected-person, claimant, victim, and record-subject
  roles. Use the ecological-scientist role only as evidence/checking context for
  FS-CCE-207, never as a general obligation actor.

## 10. Book 1, Book 2, and the evidence ceiling

Book 1 owns the legal origin, bearer mode, class, scope, delegation, private
reach, conflict, priority, excuse, breach, continuity, remedy, reader,
nonresponse, escalation, correction, re-audit, and recurrence interfaces. Book
2 owns staffing, assignment, service and case workflows, notification,
publication, storage, clocks, operational escalation, enforcement, capacity,
resources, and empirical performance.

Formal entailment may establish only the bounded legal consequences of supplied
records. It cannot authenticate a source or witness, advance a clock, make a
record arrive, cause a person or institution to read or act, deliver continuity,
complete a remedy, correct a responsible control, or establish non-recurrence.
No real receipt, action, delivery, remedy, recurrence control, or institutional
liveness follows from this decision or from the completed formal family.
