> author rulings given the same day.** It is the controlling record for the tracker item "Protect democratic and
> administrative integrity." It adds no predicate, rule, fact, pin, finding,

The neutral inventory that preceded this record is

## 1. Decision

On 2026-09-09 the author ruled:

- **A.** A constitutional money-and-influence record exists and is **fully
  consequential**: a reviewed finding drawn from it may withhold public
  answerability for an office or candidacy.
- **B.** Disclosure is a **constitutional duty**, formalised through the landed
  typed `obliged` bridge, knowing its arrival half can never be established.
- **C.** Conflicts of interest, gifts, and revolving doors land as **one
  family** under this ruling, not only the office-bound recusal half the
  state-form ruling already implies.
- **D.** Districting receives a **qualitative prohibition with no metric**: a
  plan drawn for a named forbidden purpose is a legal incompatibility.
- **E.** Procurement integrity **stays Book 2**.
- **F.** Coordinated information manipulation receives an **actor-side finding
  only, never a person-side one**.
- **G.** **Genuine opposition rights** are formalised and any association that
  fields candidates owes **internal democratic minima**; **no power to
  proscribe a party** is created.

Two of these (A and C) took the stronger fork the brief offered; the author
chose them with the brief's stated costs in view, and those costs are carried
into each family's contract card rather than argued away here.

## 2. What binds every family

These are not new; they are the walls the brief's section 2e named, restated
once so no card has to rediscover them.

- **The corpus gate decides the shape.** `bribe`, `corrupt`, `donate`, `fund`,
  `finance`, `lobby`, `campaign`, `advertise`, `disclose`, `gift`, `procure`,
  `conflict`, and `benefit` are not corpus names. Every family is built the
  way FS-POW-064 and the state-form family are built: an attested record
  through `observe/4`, closed `member/2` vocabularies for the kinds a finding
  must name, `complete/3` for the reviewed result, and `authority` withheld
  as the consequence. `influence` (`xlura`) and `control` (`jitro`) are the
  only native names that could carry this domain; a card may admit one only
  by stating why the `observe/4` idiom cannot express the same finding.
- **Three mutually distinct authorised attesters on the result** are this
  domain's wall, as they are the state-form family's. A finding attested by
  one writer, or by a writer identical to its source or subject, derives
  nothing.
- **Findings, not scores.** No family may create a personal integrity,
  probity, exposure, susceptibility, or loyalty score, a ranked list of
  persons, or an aggregate that reads into any consequence. The 2026-08-02
  temporary-assessment exclusion, extended by name on 2026-08-08, is extended
  by name again here to every product this domain could generate about a
  person's mind or associations.
- **Consequences reach public answerability, never personhood.** A finding
  under any family may withhold `authority` for an office, a candidacy, a
  plan, or an actor's public-scale function. It may never touch universal
  standing, the material floor, the equal ballot, core liberties, due
  process, or remedy — the entrenched corridor is unchanged.
- **Numbers belong to democratic law.** Thresholds, amounts, periods,
  cooling-off durations, publication floors, and district metrics are chosen
  by ordinary law inside the corridor. The constitution names kinds and
  forbidden purposes; it supplies no value and routes nothing through the
  compute backend.
- **Polarity is positive.** A current adverse finding is a positive premise
  that withholds the conclusion. Its absence withholds the adverse consequence
  and manufactures nothing: silence never disqualifies, and a withheld,
  starved, or never-made finding cannot become one.
- **Liveness is never Derived.** A duty to disclose, to read, to act, or to
  correct is a legal conclusion. That anyone disclosed, read, acted, or
  corrected is an arrival claim, Unestablished until operational assurance
  exists, and no family may write it as fact.
- **Book 2 owns operation**: registers, filing systems, audit staffing,
  investigation, enforcement workflow, publication platforms, and every
  capacity or cost question. **Nibli** consumes a bounded authenticated
  finding and performs no institutional act: it authenticates no payment,
  detects no coordination, proves no disclosure arrived, and advances no
  clock.

## 3. Ruling A — the money-and-influence record

### What it creates

A constitutional record of money and influence in public decision-making,
attested at source grain: who paid or influenced, by what kind of instrument,
to what kind of recipient, in what scope. The kinds are closed `member/2`
vocabularies fixed by the contract card and are the finding's examined
dimensions, on the appointment anti-capture pattern:

- **payer kind** — at minimum a person, an enterprise treasury as the economic
  ruling defines it, a union, a voluntary civic or advocacy association, and a
  source outside the republic's jurisdiction; the card must also require the
  attester to name the *controlling* party where the nominal payer is
  controlled by another, which is the shell-actor test;
- **instrument kind** — at minimum a contribution, an expenditure, an in-kind
  provision, a loan or guarantee, and purchased independent advocacy;
- **recipient kind** — at minimum a candidate, a party or coalition, a ballot
  question, an office holder, and a public decision.

The record is **consequential**: a reviewed finding that a recipient received
an instrument of a kind the corridor or the economic ruling prohibits from
that payer kind withholds `authority` for the affected office or candidacy,
on the same gating idiom that withholds it for majority appointment control.
This is what makes the three inherited barrier constants (FS-CCE-266,
FS-CCE-268, FS-CCE-367) catchable for the first time: today each is one rule
of the form `person($x) -> prevents($x, <Constant>)` reading no treasury,
payment, candidate, or party.

Permitted writers are the independent electoral administration and the audit
and integrity function the state-form ruling already mandates, plus the
recipient's own filing as one of the three attesters where the card so
chooses; a payer's self-report alone is never a finding. Correction and
challenge run through the landed anti-capture and Constitutional Court
interfaces, with the predeclared alternate panel where the Court's own
composition is at issue.

### What it refuses by name

- Reading the record into universal standing, the material floor, the equal
  ballot, or any allocation. A person who paid, or was paid, keeps every
  right the corridor protects.
- A donor register that is itself a re-identification surface: the record
  fixes what a finding must attest, not what is published. Proportionate
  publication thresholds are democratic law, and small-payer identity below
  them is protected by the equality-diagnostics data wall.
- Reversing the economic ruling: enterprise petition, testimony, and
  attributed publication remain lawful; the Class 5 union and civic-
  association finance card the economic ruling promised is **this family**,
  and it must prevent relabelling without treating a union as an enterprise.
- Any inference from amount to guilt, or from association to a finding.

### What it does not establish

That any payment occurred, that any register is complete, that any writer is
independent, that any challenge is heard, or that any disqualification is
executed. It changes what a finding must name and what the source can refuse.

## 4. Ruling B — disclosure as a constitutional duty

### What it creates

Exact disclosure duties derived through the FS-CVF-016 typed
`obliged(bearer, duty, standard)` bridge, which `verify.sh` already confines
to one allowlisted consumer. Bearers are office holders, candidates, parties
and coalitions, and any actor the money record or the manipulation family
names. Each duty names its reader — the audit and integrity function — and
its predeclared alternate, the ombudsperson or rights advocate. The reader's
**non-response is itself a positive derived duty** on the alternate, as it is
in the landed obligations family; audit starvation therefore produces a
derivable duty on a second office rather than silence.

### What it refuses by name

- Writing a disclosure's arrival. The family states who owes what to whom and
  the failure polarity; whether the filing exists is a liveness claim and
  remains Unestablished. Prose derived from this family uses the owed/duty
  register and never says a disclosure "is made."
- A disclosure that publishes what the equality-diagnostics data wall or the
  protected private sphere shields: the duty is proportionate by
  construction, naming the kind owed and leaving the publication floor to
  democratic law.
- Treating non-disclosure as proof of the undisclosed fact. A missing filing
  derives a duty on the reader; it derives no adverse finding about the
  bearer.

## 5. Ruling C — conflicts, gifts, and revolving doors

### What it creates

Three incompatibilities, each an attested finding by named kinds that gates
`authority` for the holder-side act, and each carrying the state-form ruling's
already-mandated recusal to an alternate:

1. **Conflict.** An office holder with an attested material interest — own,
   household, or controlled-entity — in a matter before the office owes
   recusal; acting on that matter while conflicted is a legal incompatibility
   for the act. The interest kinds are a closed vocabulary; the card names
   them.
2. **Gift.** Receipt by an office holder, from a party the office regulates,
   contracts with, adjudicates, or appoints, of a benefit of a kind the card
   names is a legal incompatibility for the holder's acts toward that party.
   Whether a benefit is *de minimis* is a democratic-law threshold; the
   constitution names the kinds and the relationship, never an amount.
3. **Revolving door.** A former holder's move into a party the office
   regulated, contracted with, adjudicated, or appointed during a
   democratic-law cooling period is a legal incompatibility for the *former
   office's* dealings with that party in that period, and a former holder's
   representation of that party before the office is refused. The
   constitution names the relationship kinds; the period is ordinary law.

The private employer, donor, or contracting party is not the bearer of these
rules; the office is. Prohibited conduct on the private side is reached by the
money record (A) and, where the actor is public-scale, by FS-POW-064.

### What it refuses by name

- A conflict, gift, or employment record that reads into standing, floor,
  ballot, or any allocation, or that creates a personal probity score.
- Guilt by kinship or association: the interest must be attested as the
  holder's own, the household's, or a controlled entity's, with the kinship
  vocabulary the 2026-08-02 batch fixed, never inferred from a name.
- A blanket bar on post-office work. The incompatibility is relationship- and
  period-bound; every person keeps the liberty to choose, refuse, leave, and
  change work.
- A constitutional amount or period.

## 6. Ruling D — districting

### What it creates

A district plan drawn for a **forbidden purpose** is a legal incompatibility,
and the plan's `authority` is withheld. The forbidden purposes are a closed
vocabulary: entrenching a named party or coalition; entrenching a named
incumbent; and diluting a protected ground, which routes to the substantive-
equality machinery rather than duplicating it. The finding is adjudicated on
attested evidence by those named kinds, with three distinct attesters, and
challenge runs through the independent electoral administration and the
Constitutional Court's ratified review of election procedure.

This is the appointment anti-capture shape applied to plans: zero arithmetic,
no metric, no threshold, no tolerance. `DistrictMagnitudeScope` — the
testability slot the state-form ruling requires — is untouched and remains a
requirement on the electoral card, not a substantive rule.

### What it refuses by name

- Any constitutional metric, threshold, tolerance, efficiency gap, or
  compactness value, and any route through the compute backend. Democratic
  law chooses whatever measure it uses to *evidence* a forbidden purpose; the
  constitution names the purposes.
- A rule that forbids proportional *outcomes* from varying: the corridor
  already requires proportional outcomes and this family does not restate it.
- Any inference from an outcome to a purpose without an attested finding.

## 7. Ruling E — procurement integrity stays Book 2

No family. Every ratified mention routes procurement to Book 2 or lists it
under no-evasion, and this ruling leaves that seam where five decisions put
it. Two things nonetheless reach public contracting from Book 1 without moving
it: the office holder who awards a contract is bound by the conflict, gift,
and revolving-door family (C), because those are office duties; and a
contracting party that is public-scale is bound by FS-POW-064. Procurement
*process* integrity — tender design, evaluation, thresholds, debarment,
challenge windows — remains `book-2/TODO.md` work.

## 8. Ruling F — coordinated information manipulation, actor-side only

### What it creates

A reviewed finding that a **coordinated actor** — a funded operation, a
network under common control, an enterprise, or an actor under the control of
a source outside the jurisdiction — is conducting coordinated manipulation of
public decision-making. The actor kinds are a closed vocabulary, and `control`
(`jitro`) is the natural native carrier for the common-control test if the
card admits it. The finding routes into the landed concentrated-private-power
and public-scale machinery: its consequences are actor-side duties of the kind
FS-POW-064 already calibrates — disclosure of coordination and funding,
attribution, access, audit, challenge, and remedy — and, for an actor that is
also a payer under A, the money record.

Media and platform concentration is reached through the same route: a
dominant communication or platform service is already an express direct
private duty-bearer under the equality ruling and a public-scale function
under the economic ruling. This family adds the manipulation finding as a
further ground; it invents no media regulator.

### What it refuses by name — and this list is the ruling

- **Any person-side head, premise, or vocabulary.** No relation may take a
  person as the subject of a manipulation, exposure, susceptibility, belief,
  or influence finding. `src/checks/repository.rs` must hold this as a guard
  with a watched failing fixture, on the pattern of the `pay` reader guard.
- Population-scale or suspicionless collection to detect coordination; the
  2026-08-08 refusal is unchanged, and a finding under this family must rest
  on attested actor-side evidence.
- A truth adjudicator: the finding names *coordination and control*, never
  the truth or falsity of what was said. Core liberties of expression,
  belief, and association are unchanged, and an uncoordinated speaker, however
  wrong, is outside this family entirely.
- Any product the temporary-assessment exclusion keeps out of the
  consequential record. That exclusion is extended by name to this domain.

## 9. Ruling G — opposition rights and party internal democracy

### What it creates

**Opposition rights.** The corridor's "genuine opposition rights" become a
card: any elected member or group not supporting the government holds equal
information access to what the government supplies the Assembly, standing to
challenge before the Constitutional Court and the electoral administration,
and a guaranteed place in the Assembly's proceedings that the majority cannot
withdraw. Withholding any of these is a legal incompatibility for the act that
withholds it. The Assembly's own procedure chooses the mechanics.

**Internal democracy.** Any association that fields candidates for a public
ballot owes internal democratic minima toward its members: member-elected
leadership, member-controlled candidate selection under the equality ruling's
candidate-list measures, equal member voice, and free exit. Failure is an
attested finding by named kinds and gates the association's `authority` to
field candidates in the affected selection, never a member's ballot or
candidacy through another route.

### What it refuses by name

- **A power to proscribe a party.** None is created, and this ruling records
  the refusal because the instrument is the one a captured state turns on the
  opposition the rest of the family protects. Individual criminal conduct is
  reached by the justice interface; a coordinated actor by F; a payer by A.
- Any duty on an association that fields no candidates. Association liberty
  is unchanged, and a union, faith, cultural, or advocacy body that does not
  enter a ballot owes nothing under this family.
- A party register as a gate on candidacy: registration is evidence, not the
  source, exactly as the state-form ruling holds for the person.
- A definition of "opposition" by party label. The right attaches to not
  supporting the government, attested by the member's own recorded position.

## 10. Formal migration contract

Each family lands as its own contract card and its own coherent assurance
batch through the protocol-v6 chain. In the sequence the dependencies permit:

1. **C** (conflicts, gifts, revolving doors) — nearest neighbour of the landed
   anti-capture slice; extends the state-form JSON source and its generated
   family.
2. **A** (money and influence record) — supplies the payer, instrument, and
   recipient vocabularies that B, F, and G read.
3. **B** (disclosure duties) — reads A's kinds and C's interest kinds through
   the `obliged` bridge.
4. **D** (districting) — independent; may land in any position after C.
5. **G** (opposition and internal democracy) — reads A for finance and the
   equality ruling for candidate lists.
6. **F** (manipulation, actor-side) — last, because it reads A and FS-POW-064
   and carries the repository guard that must see every other family's
   vocabulary before it can be shown non-vacuous.

Each card must state: the exact vocabularies and their anchor, the three-
attester wall as applied, the `authority` it gates and the holder it gates it
for, the failure polarity, the alternate route, the counterfactual fixture
that removes one named conjunct and its own pin file, the ledger rows and
scope-audit successor, and the derived prose. `promise/3`, `pay/4`, and the
purpose-limited-record guard are not reused for money in politics: a
compensation or contribution record is not a political payment, and joining
them would let the floor-side records read into public answerability.

## 12. What this decision does not establish
