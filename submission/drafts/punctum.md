Dear Eileen and Vincent,

*The Rights Nobody Has to Earn* is a complete book of about 98,000 words. It
asks what a society owes a person who can offer it nothing in return. Its
answer is a constitution built from a record that holds a child's birth and
nothing else. Being a person gives standing and a floor of nine essentials,
owed by named public bodies, on no condition of work, wealth, citizenship or
conduct. Each chapter states a rule plainly and argues it against its strongest
alternative. Every rule is also published as executable logic with public
tests, so a reader can run the constitution rather than take it on trust. The
book is political theory, law and formal method at once, written for a reader
who is none of those specialists.

It is already public under CC BY 4.0, at
https://dhilipsiva.dev/rights-nobody-has-to-earn/. It was written with AI
assistance throughout: the design's rulings are mine, and an AI assistant
drafted the prose, the rules and their tests, which I approved. I am offering it
to other presses at the same time.

Would you like me to send the complete manuscript in your next window?
