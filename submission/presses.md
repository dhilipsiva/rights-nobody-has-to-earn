# Adapting the proposal to seven presses

This is a working note for the author, prepared on 25 September 2026 from each
press's public pages. **Nothing here has been sent, and no press has been
contacted.** Each submission, and every fact about the author below marked
*author to supply*, is the author's to decide and write.

The [publisher-neutral proposal](README.md) describes the manuscript at
`be51db06`. Each section says:

- how the press wants to be approached;
- what it asks for, and how the proposal maps onto that;
- what stands in the way;
- what the author must supply or decide.

The requirements were read on the dates shown and may change. Check each press's
own page again immediately before sending.

## The decisions that come first

conflicts below, and then decided not to approach UCL Press. The messages to

## Texts every approach can use

### Synopsis in 150 words

What does a society owe a person who can offer it nothing in return, and how
can that person hold it to the promise? The book opens on Santoshi Kumari,
eleven, who died in Jharkhand in 2017 after her family's ration card was
cancelled for not being linked to a biometric identity number. It then tests a
record that holds a child's birth and nothing else. Its answer is a
constitution. Being a person gives standing and a floor of nine essentials,
owed by named public bodies. Work, wealth, citizenship and good conduct are not
conditions. Each rule is stated plainly and argued against its strongest
alternative, with evidence and cost. Each is also
published as executable logic with public tests, so a reader can check what it
entails. The book claims its rules have the consequences it describes, not that
the society would work or is affordable.

### Description in 150–200 words

This version is for a press that asks for back-cover copy.

What does a society owe a person who can offer it nothing in return? *The
Rights Nobody Has to Earn* answers with a constitution built from that person
outward. Being a person gives standing in law and a floor of nine essentials,
from food and shelter to care and company. Named public bodies owe them. Work,
wealth, citizenship and good conduct are not conditions. The rest of the design
protects that floor: government divided by place and function, a vote resting
on residence, records bound to their purpose, emergencies that suspend no right,
and a core no majority may amend away. Each chapter states its rule plainly,
with cases, then argues it against the strongest alternative. The evidence
comes from the Grootboom eviction to the Dutch childcare-benefits affair and
*ADM Jabalpur*. Every rule is published as executable logic with public tests,
so readers can check for themselves what the constitution entails. The book is
openly licensed, and its companion runs every chapter's cases in the browser.

### Disclosure of AI assistance

Every approach should disclose the AI assistance plainly and early, in the
book's own terms:

> The design's rulings are the author's. An AI assistant drafted the prose of
> the chapters, their arguments, the synthesis, the method, the opening note
> and the English rendering of the epigraph. The author approved it, much of it
> under a standing approval of recommended revisions rather than passage by
> passage. The same sessions wrote the formal rules, their tests and the
> companion, and located and checked the sources. The book's opening note says
> this in full.

## MIT Press (Direct to Open)

Read on 25 September 2026 from archived copies of the press's pages, because
the live site refuses automated reading.

- **Approach.** Send an inquiry first, to one editor. It "should simply
  describe the project, the author, and the reasons for writing the book- in a
  few paragraphs". Send no chapters unless invited; if invited, send about a
  quarter of the work.
- **Editor.** None is listed for politics or law. The nearest fits are
  Philip Laughlin (philosophy and bioethics) and Justin Kehoe (science,
  technology and society), because of the executable rules.
- **Proposal if invited.** A prospectus covering description, features,
  competition, apparatus, audience, author, market, status with length in
  words, and suggested reviewers; a detailed table of contents; two to four
  sample chapters; and a CV.
- **Open access.** Direct to Open is diamond open access with no author fee.
  A 2021 report describes it as covering "professional" books "intended
  primarily for specialists, as distinguished from 'trade' titles". The same
  report names CC BY as ideal but NC-ND as the default. The book's
  non-specialist reader and its CC BY licence are both questions for the
  inquiry.
- **AI.** "Authors who use AI tools to produce text … must inform their editors
  of this use and be transparent about it in their manuscripts." This is
  compatible with disclosure.
- **Author to supply.** The author paragraph, the reasons for writing, and the
  choice of editor.
- **Sources.** Archived copies of the [proposal
  guidelines](https://web.archive.org/web/20260115031452/https://mitpress.mit.edu/submitting-book-proposal/),
  [prospective authors](https://web.archive.org/web/20260922125615/https://mitpress.mit.edu/prospective-authors/),
  [acquisitions staff](https://web.archive.org/web/20260202223524/https://mitpress.mit.edu/about/acquisitions-staff/)
  and [Direct to Open](https://web.archive.org/web/20260827220222/https://direct.mit.edu/books/pages/direct-to-open).

## punctum books

- **Approach.** One PDF, emailed to both co-directors, is accepted only from
  1 May to 31 July. It must be a complete manuscript with a brief proposal,
  names, affiliations and contact details. Outside the window, send a very
  brief pitch with nothing attached.
- **Proposal.** The book's backgrounds and what it challenges, its aims, and
  its organisation with chapter summaries. No CV or letters of recommendation.
- **Open access.** No fees, and "open to authors choosing their own Creative
  Commons license", so CC BY is possible. Their FAQ allows authors to
  incorporate earlier work "as long as the original publication is properly
  credited".
- **AI.** No policy found. A May 2026 post notes "an increasing amount of
  LLM-generated materials" in submissions. Disclose in the pitch.
- **Simultaneous submission.** Allowed if punctum is told at the time.
- **Author to supply.** Whether to send a pitch now, and affiliation and
  contact details.
- **Sources.** [submit](https://punctumbooks.com/submit), [2026
  call](https://punctumbooks.pubpub.org/pub/inviting-manuscript-submissions-for-20272028/release/1),
  and the archived [submission
  guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20260308023626/https://docs.punctumbooks.com/books/about-submitting-your-manuscript/export/markdown).
