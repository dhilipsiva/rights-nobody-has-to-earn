PSYCHOLOGICAL CLAIMS, GROUNDED BY NOT NAMING A STATE

None of the 313 duty constants compels or certifies believing, eating,
learning, accepting treatment, a relationship, fulfilment, satisfaction,
happiness, trust or loyalty, and there is no second compliance duty. The one
that names compliance is an institution carrying out a court's final protective
direction, allowlisted by name.

The interesting half is where the guard is not. Probing the corpus showed that
`happy` and `deserve` compile while `trust`, `wellbeing`, `satisfaction`,
`compliance`, `motivation`, `attitude` and `loyalty` do not. So this test is
what keeps the first two out, and the closure is what keeps the rest out. The
test now asserts BOTH, so it fails rather than passing silently if the corpus
ever admits one of them. Writing the check before probing would have produced a
test that looked strong and guarded almost nothing.

The empirical half — autonomy, voice, non-humiliation, relatedness, retaliation,
status competition, learned helplessness, care burden, the effects of being
watched — is route-unbuilt. Nothing here tests it and no service record may be
read as evidence of it.
