# The revision plan's sources, checked — 2026-09-25

The [revision plan](../new-reviwes/revision-plan-9.5.md) named about 150
sources in §8.2, §8.5, §9.1, §9.2 and §10, and said they were written from
memory. Each was checked against a primary source, or the most authoritative
copy that could be reached, before any later item may use it (TODO item 46).
The checked sources are entries in [`claims.json`](claims.json), each with a
locator a reader can follow and notes saying what was actually read: the full
text, an official page, an abstract or a publisher's description, and the
pinpoint verified. A book whose bibliographic record verified but whose page
could not be read is registered with that limit stated in its notes, never with
an invented page.

The registry's lessons governed the check: an identifier is not a source, and a
court is not a judgment. Under rulings D2 and D3 these sources may support the
opening, the Part openers, the argument sections and Part V, never a derived
section.

## Where the plan misdescribed its source

Each registered claim states what the source supports, not what the plan said
it supports. Where they differed, the entry's notes give the detail; these are
the differences a later item must not reintroduce.

**Lineage.**
- `anderson-1999-point-of-equality`: Anderson's guarantee is conditional, the
  opposite of this book's floor. It covers "all law-abiding citizens" (p. 289),
  and access to functionings requiring an income may be made conditional on
  working (p. 318).
- `cescr-gc12-1999-right-to-food`: the duty to provide applies when people
  cannot enjoy the right "for reasons beyond their control" (para. 15).
- `shue-1980-basic-rights`: "unconditional" was not verified; the third duty is
  aid to "the deprived".
- `crpd-2006-article-12`: the treaty never says "supported decision-making";
  the phrase is the Committee's (`crpd-gc1-2014-legal-capacity`, whose para. 21
  "best interpretation of will and preferences" matches the book's bounded
  route).
- `crc-1989-article-12`: the treaty sets no minimum age; the reading that there
  is no age limit is General Comment 12's (paras 20–21).
- `dreze-sen-1989-hunger-public-action`: the book's own terms are entitlement
  protection, cash and employment support, and support-led security, not
  "entitlement versus delivery".
- `sen-1981-poverty-and-famines`: the availability explanation is rejected for
  Bengal 1943, not for every famine studied.
- `rockstrom-2009-safe-operating-space`: the boundaries and their
  transgression are 2009 estimates. `ostrom-1990-governing-the-commons`: the
  design principles are observed practices, not guarantees.

**Animals, the commons and the core.**
- `tfeu-2012-article-13-animal-welfare`: a duty to pay regard to welfare
  requirements in listed policy areas, qualified by customs; not a right.
- `uk-animal-welfare-sentience-act-2022`: the Act presupposes sentience rather
  than declaring it.
- `awbi-v-union-of-india-2023`: the Maharashtra and Karnataka amendments concern
  bullock-cart races and kambala, and the Court proceeded on the basis that
  animals hold no fundamental rights.
- `bverfg-2021-climate-order-intertemporal-freedom`: future generations hold no
  present rights; the duty toward them is objective only (paras 109, 146), and
  Article 20a confers no subjective right (para. 112).
- `waldron-2006-case-against-judicial-review`: the argument targets strong
  review only and holds under four stated assumptions.

**Franchise, executives and documented cases.**
- `goodin-2007-all-affected-interests`: the principle cuts against a
  residence-bounded vote; cite it as the alternative, not as support.
- `lopez-guerra-2014-disenfranchisement`: the book also defends an
  "enfranchisement lottery".
- `chile-constitution-art14-foreign-residents-2025`: the plan is out of date.
  Ley 21.773 (2025) requires ten uninterrupted years from a permanent residence
  permit, not five.
- `uruguay-colegiado-1952`: 1952–1966, not 1967; the source argues the failure
  was political leadership rather than the collegial form.
- `gilmore-2007-golden-gulag`: the widely repeated growth figures were not
  verified; do not quote them from this entry.
- `adm-jabalpur-1976`: the Court held detainees had no standing to seek habeas
  corpus; "habeas corpus suspended" is loose.
- `hussainara-khatoon-1979`: the finding about detention beyond the maximum
  sentence is in the 4 May 1979 judgment, (1980) 1 SCC 115, not the first one.
- `ccca-2010-collateral-consequences-act`: redesignated a Model Act in 2022.
- `pratt-2008-scandinavian-exceptionalism`: Finland, Norway and Sweden only.
- `bhuller-2020-incarceration-recidivism-employment`: the gains in reoffending
  and employment are for defendants not working before prison; for the
  previously employed, employment fell. The estimates concern defendants whose
  sentence turns on the judge assigned.

**Floor, delivery, scarcity and remedy.**
- `statelessness-reduction-convention-1961`: Article 8(2)–(3) permits
  deprivation that leaves a person stateless in listed cases.
- `mgnrega-audit-of-schemes-rules-2011`: the rules set out recipient-side
  checks; they do not show that the checks happen.
- `persad-2009-complete-lives-system`: its main feature is age-based priority;
  life-years enter through prognosis. `emanuel-2020-covid-allocation` ranks
  life-years below saving the most lives.
- `hhs-ocr-alabama-ventilator-2020`: closed without a finding of liability, and
  about age as well as disability.
- `grootboom-2000-reasonableness`: the Court declined to define a minimum core
  on its record; it did not reject the idea. `mazibuko-2009-water-quantity`:
  the Court refused to fix 50 litres a day as the right's content.
- `pucl-right-to-food-order-2001`: "converted into legal entitlements" is the
  campaign's wording, not the order's.
- `colombia-t025-2004-displacement`: a review chamber, not the full court.
- `siracusa-principles-1984`, `paris-principles-1993`,
  `nelson-mandela-rules-2015`, `undrip-2007-article-32`: none is binding law.

**Work, money and equality.**
- `ilo-c029-forced-labour-1930`: Article 2(2)'s exceptions mean it does not ban
  all compelled work. `ilo-c105-abolition-forced-labour-1957`: forced labour
  for five listed purposes only.
- `ilo-cfa-compilation-2018-minimum-services`: para. 872 accepts requisitioning
  some striking workers, so it does not support a rule against conscripting a
  named worker.
- `bverfg-2010-hartz-iv-subsistence-minimum`: the right is subsidiary and means
  tested. `bverfg-2019-benefit-sanctions`: the rules were declared
  incompatible, not void, and a 30% reduction was upheld.
- `german-basic-law-debt-brake-2009`: amended again in 2025; say "as amended in
  2009".
- `griggs-v-duke-power-1971`: statutory (Title VII), not constitutional.
  `sffa-v-harvard-2023`: the holding concerns two programmes.
- `nao-2016-benefit-sanctions`: the evidence is mixed, not simply adverse.

**Environment, roles and belonging.**
- `urgenda-hoge-raad-2019`: an obligation to take measures, not to guarantee a
  result. `wales-future-generations-act-2015`: the Commissioner advises and
  recommends.
- `mental-capacity-act-2005-best-interests`: the Act itself requires helping
  people decide (ss 1(3), 4(4)); what differs is who decides once capacity is
  found lacking.
- `refugee-convention-1951-article-33`: Article 33(2) allows exceptions, so it
  cannot support calling non-refoulement "absolute"; the book's absolute rule
  is its own choice and needs its own argument.
- `ilo-c169-1989-consultation-consent` and `orissa-mining-niyamgiri-2013`: no
  general consent veto.

**Institutions and method.**
- `eu-whistleblower-directive-2019-art21-5`: a rebuttable presumption of
  retaliation.
- `hirst-v-uk-no2-2005`: blanket disenfranchisement only.
  `august-v-electoral-commission-1999` did not decide whether a bar could be
  justified.
- `syri-hague-district-court-2020`: the system was not disproportionate in
  itself; its safeguards failed Article 8(2).
- `elkins-ginsburg-melton-2009-endurance-of-constitutions`: do not take a
  lifespan figure from this entry; the sources conflict.
- `hildebrandt-2020-law-for-computer-scientists`: an introductory textbook
  whose critical material is in chapters 10 and 11, not a critique of
  computational law.
- `apt-blair-walker-1988-stratified-programs`, `denning-1976-lattice-model`,
  `myers-liskov-1997-decentralized-information-flow`: checked through
  publication data and abstracts; `sergot-1986-british-nationality-act-logic-program`
  was read in full, and its abandoned-infant default bears directly on the
  child case.

## Limits of the check

Several official sites refused automated access; the entries say where an
archived or other official copy was read instead. Many books were checked at
chapter level from the publisher's description, and their notes say so. A
locator was confirmed to lead to the document or its official page on the day
of the check; whether it still resolves later is a network fact no test here
can establish.
