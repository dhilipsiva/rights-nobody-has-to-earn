# Pilot Sample and Recruitment Rule

> **Status: draft template; not pre-registered or frozen.** No person has been
> screened, recruited, enrolled, excluded, or compensated by this file.

## 1. Audience and eligibility

The pilot recruits adults who can give informed consent and can examine the
frozen Book 1 snapshot in an offered accessible format and language they
understand. They are non-specialists: they have not authored the book or its
formal sources, worked on its assurance machinery, or supplied the in-repo
reviewer corpus.

Professional title, education, reading speed, agreement with the book, and
political opinion are not eligibility tests. Recruitment must not target or
exclude someone because the operator expects favourable or unfavourable
answers.

The pilot is not the fresh holdout. Prior exposure is screened and retained
privately as a design variable, but pilot participation itself makes a person
ineligible for the later holdout. Anyone who authored, edited, formally
reviewed, or previously received detailed coaching on the tested snapshot is
excluded from this pilot so the in-repo reviewer corpus cannot be relabelled as
reader evidence.

## 2. Maximum-variation recruitment

Recruit through more than one channel and seek variation in self-described
reading confidence, language background, and accessibility needs. These data
remain private. They guide invitations only; there is no quota, weighting,
stratified verdict, or claim of demographic representativeness.

Recruitment copy states the purpose as testing a reading instrument, not
testing intelligence, political agreement, or the participant. It discloses
the time demand, formats, content warning, compensation formula, withdrawal
right, privacy boundary, and the fact that coded outcomes may enter a public
privacy-minimal record.

The operator logs every screening and invitation decision privately before
seeing any response. Replacements may be invited only under the stopping rule,
never because an answer is inconvenient.

## 3. Counting definitions

- **Screened:** eligibility was assessed. Screening alone is not an attempt.
- **Enrolled:** informed consent was obtained and the frozen snapshot was
  supplied. Enrollment alone is not an attempt.
- **Attempted session:** the facilitator delivered the first unaided prompt.
  A later withdrawal, disconnection, exclusion, or inadmissibility still
  counts toward the attempted-session ceiling.
- **Admissible completion:** all six unaided prompts were delivered and closed,
  and the reviewer/custodian found the session `admissible` under the frozen
  ethics and protocol terms. Saying “I do not know” or declining a particular
  answer is a response state, not automatic inadmissibility.
- **Withdrawn:** the participant exercised the withdrawal right. A withdrawn
  attempt supplies no public coded content outcome and is never recoded as an
  exclusion.
- **Inadmissible:** a completed or partial session breached a binding ethics,
  role, identity, snapshot, facilitation, or protocol term. Its lifecycle and
  coded deviation remain; its content outcomes do not enter admitted evidence.

## 4. Fixed stopping rule

Recruitment and sessions aim for 10 admissible completions. Stop conducting
sessions at the earlier of:

1. 10 admissible completions; or
2. 12 attempted sessions.

At the stop:

- 8 or more admissible completions satisfy the pilot sample minimum;
- fewer than 8 admissible completions make the attempt invalid and require a
  preserved void record plus a newly pre-registered successor pilot; and
- no session beyond the stop may be added to improve, balance, or reverse the
  observed coding.

Scheduled people who have not reached the first unaided prompt when the stop
occurs are cancelled promptly and compensated for any study time already
spent. The private recruitment log preserves that action without exposing
identity.

## 5. Compensation and withdrawal

Compensation follows `disclosures-and-ethics.md`. It covers all study time
already spent, including consent, accessibility setup, reading, session time,
and withdrawal administration. It is paid regardless of admissibility,
answers, completion, control result, or later use of the record. Accessibility
costs are reimbursed separately and never deducted from compensation.

## 6. Claim boundary

This purposive, bounded pilot supports instrument revision for the tested
audience and snapshot only. It cannot support prevalence, population,
demographic-comparison, causal, or release-suitability claims. The later fresh
holdout uses its own frozen recruitment and sample rule and excludes every
pilot participant.
