# Seeded-Control Private-Preimage Template

> **Status: public construction contract only; not a seed, transcript,
> preimage, answer key, commitment, or control run.** No exact synthetic answer
> or expected code mapping belongs in this repository while the private
> commitment is active.

## 1. Private preimage contents

The reviewer/custodian creates one canonical UTF-8 private preimage containing:

- an opaque control ID, construction time, custodian identity, and random
  nonce;
- the exact frozen instrument and private-codebook SHA-256 values;
- one complete synthetic answer record under the six ratified prompts;
- deliberate semantic plants spanning ordinary constructive life, democratic
  and private choice, normal public function, delivery and repair, the
  prisoner's stress-test role, and external/model limits;
- a mechanical private key mapping every plant to its expected pilot
  observation and acceptable adjudicated state; and
- a statement that the transcript is synthetic, false, excluded from the
  sample, and incapable of supplying a release result.

The precise phrasing, plant count, mapping, examples, and key remain private.
The custodian checks that no plant depends on participant identity, political
agreement, demographic inference, or a severity/threshold that has not been
ratified.

## 2. Commitment

Before recruitment, compute the nonce-protected commitment over the exact
canonical private preimage and bind it into the pilot freeze under named
private custody. The public record contains only the opaque control or
commitment ID, commitment SHA-256, bound artifact digests, freeze time, and an
identity-free custody attestation.

Do not commit the nonce, preimage, exact transcript, answer key, or private
codebook. Do not put them in a commit message, issue, pull request, chat, test
fixture, generated report, or public deviation narrative.

## 3. Controlled use

Present only the transcript portion to coders under an opaque ID and preserve
their independence. Reveal the private answer key to the reviewer/custodian
only after both initial coder records and adjudication are locked. Determine
the outcome under the exact private rule satisfying
`provisional-falsification-rule-template.md`.

The public pilot record may state `watched-failing`, `failed-to-fail`, or
`indeterminate` and bind the control transcript digest. It does not reveal the
preimage or turn the control into participant evidence.

## 4. Change and successor rule

Any change to the private seed, key, nonce, instrument, codebook, or expected
mapping after freeze voids that attempt. Preserve the old commitment and
attempt. Create a new nonce, preimage, commitment, pre-registration, and
successor pilot; never overwrite or silently re-key the existing control.
