# Pilot Instrument Public-Minimum Template

> **Status: public template only; not a runnable instrument, pre-registration,
> or freeze.** The completed participant script, exact diagnostic follow-ups,
> sequencing details, and as-run form remain in external private custody. The
> public record carries only their exact SHA-256 artifact reference or
> nonce-protected commitment and an identity-free custody attestation.

## 1. Fixed public content

The runnable instrument must ask these six author-ratified unaided prompts,
without substantive alteration:

1. **What do people do in ordinary life?**
2. **What may they choose privately and democratically?**
3. **What do public bodies do when nothing has failed?**
4. **How does something owed arrive and get repaired?**
5. **Why is the prisoner present?**
6. **What remains operational or externally assumed, and what can no model guarantee?**

The private instrument freezes the prompt order before recruitment. It keeps
the unaided answers closed before any diagnostic feedback begins.

## 2. Minimum facilitator constraints

The privately frozen script must:

- identify the exact tested snapshot and confirm informed consent;
- state that the session tests the instrument, not the participant;
- repeat the privacy, compensation, withdrawal, accessibility, and content
  safeguards from `disclosures-and-ethics.md`;
- permit only verbatim prompt repetition and procedural/access assistance
  during the unaided section;
- prohibit examples, substantive definitions, chapter pointers, leading
  follow-ups, approval signals, correction, and answer repair;
- allow “I do not know,” refusal, pause, accommodation, and withdrawal;
- close every unaided response before diagnostic feedback; and
- record all assistance and deviations rather than silently editing them.

The runnable participant-facing wording and diagnostic sequence are private
instrument content. They must not be reconstructed in Git, an issue, a pull
request, or a public study report before the relevant contamination boundary
permits disclosure.

## 3. Private instrument contents

External private custody must retain the exact:

- opening, consent-confirmation, transition, pause, withdrawal, safety, and
  closing scripts;
- prompt delivery and neutral procedural-response instructions;
- diagnostic feedback questions and their sequencing;
- facilitator deviation and access-event form;
- private as-run response-record structure; and
- version, canonical byte encoding, artifact SHA-256, freeze time, and custody
  provenance.

The private artifact must satisfy this public minimum and the controlling
protocol decision. A digest match cannot cure a substantive conflict.

## 4. Seeded-control use

The private control transcript is never read to a participant and never
counted as a session. It enters the established coding workflow under an opaque
control ID. Coders remain blind to each other's initial records; control status
and the private answer key remain withheld until those records are locked.

## 5. Claim boundary

Publishing the six prompts and this template creates no instrument run,
participant result, severity taxonomy, release threshold, route availability,
or Gate C evidence.
