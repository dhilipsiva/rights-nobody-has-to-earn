A domain needs ordinary operation **and** a credible failure, abuse or
boundary. Whether a passage states a boundary is read out of its own
prose — what it says it does not establish — rather than declared here,
so no row can claim a disclosure its section does not make.

This is a map of what a reader meets, not a claim about whether they
understand it. No reader has been asked anything; R6 is unbuilt, and
nothing here is reader evidence.
