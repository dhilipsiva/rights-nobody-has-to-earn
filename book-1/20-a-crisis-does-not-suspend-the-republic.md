# A Crisis Does Not Suspend the Republic

Every design in this book has an easier version of itself that appears the
moment something goes badly wrong. The easier version has a clause that says:
in an emergency, the rest of this may be set aside. This design does not have
that clause, and this chapter is what stands in its place — what a declaration
may actually do, what it may never do, what happens at a border where power
conventionally grows, and what the record does when one of these powers breaks
the rule it was operating under.

The shape to watch for is the same throughout. A power completes only
while its own record holds together; a measure taken under it rejoins the exact
authorisation it was taken under; and when an independent finding knocks out the
authorisation, everything that rejoined it stops. What never moves is the
person the power was aimed at.

Everything here is a legal effect over supplied records. Nothing in this
chapter detects a threat, assesses necessity, ends an emergency, recalls a
deployment, or establishes that anybody acted.

## What a declaration may actually do

An emergency declaration can do four things: accelerate specified procedures,
redirect public resources, requisition necessary property with return or
compensation, and authorize a narrowly justified restriction against its
named hazard. It cannot suspend a right or institution, delay an election,
extend a mandate, create government by decree, or leave a standing power
behind. Rationing remains within the ordinary physical-scarcity rules;
redirecting capacity cannot conscript a named worker. Price control needs no
emergency declaration.

The declaration, each renewal and each measure have separate current windows.
A measure must identify the exact declaration revision and, when applicable,
its fresh renewal. No general status label lends authority to another case.
A predeclared alternate authorizer or independent substitute reviewer keeps
the same limits and requires ratification at the ordinary body's first
opportunity. Before then, the record needs positive evidence that the
opportunity has not yet arrived. Silence is not approval, and an unratified
alternate cannot keep acting after that opportunity passes.

Missing authority withholds the conclusion; it does not write a cessation act
or release a person. A positively recorded end requires restoration,
evidence preservation, review and remedy. Identical frozen evidence can still
produce the same answer when replayed. The rules neither keep time nor force
the next record to arrive.

## When the emergency broke the rule it was under

The section above says what an emergency declaration may not do. This one says
what the record does when one does it anyway.

Start with a declaration that completed: a named hazard, a named affected
population, published reasons and evidence, its own current window, an
authorisation no single body gave itself, and immediate notification to the
Assembly and to the Constitutional Court. On that declaration a requisition
completed — specified property necessary for that hazard, with an inventory, a
route to return or compensation, and a real challenge. While both stood, so did
what they carried. The person named in the measure was restrained from using the
specified property. The loss of that use was recorded. The operator owed an
accounting: inventory the property, return it or compensate for it.

Then an independent review establishes the defect that declaration cannot
survive. It suspended something, or it governed by decree.

The declaration stops. Not criticised, not scheduled to lapse — the
conclusion that a non-derogating emergency declaration exists no longer follows.
The requisition goes with it, because a measure rejoins the exact declaration
revision it was authorised under and there is no longer one to rejoin. The
restraint stops. The recorded loss stops.

And the accounting duty stops as well, which is the uncomfortable half and
belongs in the open rather than in a footnote. That duty was derived from a
lawful requisition; when the requisition stops being lawful, the design stops
saying anyone owes that particular accounting. What survives is what the finding
itself had to establish before it could be made at all: that reliance on the
defective record ends, that the evidence is preserved, and that care, challenge
and an independent judicial remedy stay open. So the design can now say the
taking had no authority behind it. It cannot establish, from that alone, that
the property came back.

What does not move is the person. Before the finding, while it stood, and after
it fell, whoever is named in that record is a person, is owed the floor, and is
not a prisoner. An emergency that broke its own rule is a fact about the
declaration. It is not a fact about them.

## Power does not grow at the border

Defence stays under civilian command, Assembly limits on size and armament,
ordinary appropriation and full independent audit. No private force or outside
funding route can acquire a public coercive power. Force abroad needs actual
Assembly authorization, except the immediate response strictly necessary
against an actual attack before the Assembly can act. That response must be
submitted for ratification at its first opportunity. A predeclared alternate
or substitute reviewer carries the same limits while that opportunity is
still ahead. Once it passes, continuing force needs actual Assembly
authorization; the immediate-response exception cannot renew itself.
Cyber attribution and equivalence to an armed attack need independently
assessable evidence; they
create no separate response power. Credible evidence of prohibited arms use
requires suspension. Children may not be recruited or used in hostilities.
Civilian protection, humanitarian care and access apply in every condition.

Treaty negotiation belongs to the executive; ratification belongs to the
Assembly, with the Regions Council and each directly affected region's
consent where a protected competence or boundary is touched. Withdrawal takes
the same route. Provisional application cannot pre-empt it. Ratifiers,
reviewers and courts must examine actual effects against the constitutional
limits. Nothing in this constitutional record independently establishes what
a treaty will do. That judgment remains the ratifiers', reviewers' and courts'
responsibility. Trade, sanctions, investment, procurement, contractors,
affiliates, flags and arbitration forums cannot export a harm that public power could not
lawfully impose at home.

These are enumerated common competences, not a residual power over anything
called security or foreign affairs. Regional and local functions, residual
competences and stronger compatible protections remain.

Lawful exit still requires the actual internal route: federal agreement,
rights and minority review, a complete settlement and final ratification by
the affected population. Borders, assets, debts, commons, floors and minority
protections must be settled together with nationality, statelessness,
nonmoving residents, families, records, defence and treaty succession. A
consent-required collective effect needs actual prior informed consent for
the same holder, title, settlement revision and effect; an ordinary
consultation is not a substitute. Neither military power nor manipulated
trade, border access or services may be used against lawful exit. Foreign
recognition, cooperation and readmission remain external assumptions.

## When a public power was handed to a private force

The same shape reaches the armed side.

A defence structure completed: inside the Assembly's ceiling on size and
armament, under civilian command, funded by ordinary appropriation, open to
independent audit. On that structure an authorisation of force abroad completed
— a ground of defence against an actual or imminent attack or a lawful
collective-security or humanitarian mandate, a prior actual Assembly
authorisation, and a published objective, legal basis, scope, geography, means,
duration and reporting. While both stood, the body holding the structure owed a
duty to keep defence inside those ceilings, appropriations and that audit, and
the authorisation carried its own restraint on a named person's physical
autonomy, with the loss recorded.

Then an independent review establishes that a public coercive power had been
delegated to a private actor.

The structure stops. The authorisation for force abroad goes with it, because it
rejoins the exact structure it was raised under. The restraint stops and the
recorded loss stops. So does the duty to keep inside the ceilings, which existed
only because there was a lawful structure to keep inside.

The person named through the sequence keeps personhood, keeps the floor owed to
them, and does not become a prisoner. Whatever was wrong with the structure was
wrong with the structure.

What the collapse does not do is recall anybody. No rule here ends a deployment,
brings anyone home, or stops a private contractor being paid. The record says
the authority is not there. Whether anything in the world stopped is a different
kind of claim, and this design does not make it.

## The child with nobody

The child with nobody is the reason this chapter is written in the negative.

An emergency is where a design of this kind usually acquires the power to treat
somebody as a case rather than a person, and the ordering is always the same:
first the exception, then the question of who falls inside it. Here there is no
exception to fall inside. The declaration cannot suspend a right, and the
rights it cannot suspend are owed on personhood alone — so for a record holding
one birth entry, a declared emergency changes nothing at all. Not because the
child is exempted. Because there is no clause that reaches them.

The withdrawal sequences above are then worth reading for what they hold
steady rather than what they undo. Through each one — a declaration found to
have governed by decree, a defence structure found to have handed public
coercion to a private actor — the person named in the record keeps personhood,
keeps the floor, and does not become a prisoner. The child with nobody is the
limiting case of that same invariance: the design's most extreme powers are
built so that they cannot read a person, which is exactly why a person the
record barely knows is no more reachable by them than anyone else.

## What none of this ends

Capability, doctrine, training, equipment, forensics, border and reception
operation, incident command, stockpiles, restoration, procurement and treaty
and sanctions administration all belong to the second book. Other states'
cooperation and recognition are external assumptions rather than anything this
design can establish.

And the limit the withdrawal sequences make unavoidable belongs here in plain
words. When an authorisation falls, the design can say the power was not there.
It cannot say that the property came back, that the deployment ended, that the
contractor stopped being paid, or that anybody was released. A record that
withdraws an authority is not an event in the world, and this chapter's
strongest results are all of the first kind.
