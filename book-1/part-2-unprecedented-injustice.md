# Unprecedented Injustice

*A documented case, told from the report of a Dutch parliamentary inquiry.*

Parents in the Netherlands who pay for childcare can claim a public allowance
towards its cost. In December 2020 a parliamentary committee, which had heard
its witnesses under oath, reported on how that allowance had been administered.
It called its report *Ongekend onrecht*: unprecedented injustice.[^ongekend]

The committee found that fundamental principles of the rule of law had been
violated. The benefits service of the tax administration had dealt with
parents as a group rather than one by one, taken an approach the committee
called all-or-nothing, and classed parents as acting with intent or gross
negligence; together, it said, these seriously breached the principle of doing
justice to each person's situation. Parents were wrongly branded as
intentional fraudsters. The reproach did not stop at the administration. The
legislature had passed a rigid law with no hardship clause and too little
attention to proportionality, the administrative courts had upheld its rigid
execution for years, and parliament had repeatedly been given information
late, incompletely and incorrectly.[^ongekend-findings]

The allowance belonged to ordinary working life: parents at work, children in
care, a claim the law itself offered. The committee's findings describe rules
that left no room for a particular family's circumstances, applied to parents
as a group, and a chain of institutions each of which upheld the one before.

Part II is about that ordinary life: what a person may do without asking
anyone's permission, what work, pay and contribution come to, what money cannot
buy, the same route to what is owed for everyone, the conditions and creatures
a life depends on, the roles people hold in each other's lives, and arrival. It
begins with one week in which the rules touch a single person and mostly stay
out of sight. The chapters show where each rule reaches and where it stops, and
why a pattern seen across a group decides nothing about any one person in it.
