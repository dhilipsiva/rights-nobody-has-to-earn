**Ruling D9 argued — item 58, 2026-09-25.** Chapter 13 now defines the three

## 1. Decision

Book 1 adopts three related but non-collapsed protections:

1. every person has an independently enforceable right to a clean, healthy,
   and sustainable environment;
2. ecological commons and the future ecological capability they support are
   protected against exhaustion, destruction, and unjustified regression; and
3. credibly sentient non-human animals are directly protected subjects with
   bodily, life, and species-appropriate interests, without becoming
   constitutional persons.

These protections are not one score and do not have one holder. The
environmental right belongs to every person. Commons and future conditions are
protected through the existing Class 9 contract. Non-human-animal welfare and
integrity form a distinct Class 10. Each receives its own duty, evidence,
failure, continuity, remedy, audit, and correction path.

The existing material floor remains unchanged. A healthy environment is not a
ninth floor item and does not alter the current floor predicates. It is a
distinct right, power limit, public duty, and commons interface. The floor may
not be withheld in the name of ecology, and ecological ceilings may not be
erased in the name of floor delivery.

## 2. The environmental right

Every person has the right to live in environmental conditions compatible with
health, bodily integrity, secure use of the material floor, culture,
participation, and an independently chosen life. The right protects clean air,
safe water, healthy soil and food systems, protection from hazardous exposure,
and the ecological conditions on which those interests depend.

The right binds every public tier, public institution, contractor, delegated
public function, and private actor whose conduct creates, controls, authorizes,
finances with material control, or materially conceals the relevant harm. A
public body remains responsible for prevention, continuity, enforcement, and
repair when a private provider, owner, or contractor fails.

The right includes:

- timely access to comprehensible environmental information, including known
  uncertainty, cumulative effects, conflicts, and source limitations;
- meaningful and accessible participation before a high-consequence decision
  is made, with additional routes for people and collectives most exposed to
  the proposed harm;
- prior assessment of reasonably foreseeable direct, indirect, cumulative,
  distributional, cross-boundary, and long-latency effects;
- public reasons addressing alternatives and material objections;
- independent review, interim protection where necessary, correction, and an
  effective remedy; and
- protection from retaliation for reporting, investigating, contesting, or
  seeking a remedy for ecological harm.

No claimant must prove ownership of the affected place or show a completed
injury where credible evidence establishes threatened serious or irreversible
harm. Persistent unequal exposure is evidence for the substantive-equality
route, not an environmental worth or vulnerability score.

The right does not promise a pristine environment, forbid every risk, compel a
single lifestyle, or turn every policy disagreement into a constitutional
case. Ordinary democratic law chooses compatible means above the protected
minimum and inside valid ecological ceilings.

## 3. Protected commons and future ecological capability

Class 9 protects, at minimum:

- the climate system and atmosphere;
- clean air;
- surface water, groundwater, wetlands, coasts, seas, and oceans;
- living and productive soil;
- biodiversity, genetic diversity, habitats, ecological relationships, and
  ecosystem integrity and resilience;
- forests, grasslands, reefs, and other systems that sustain ecological
  functions;
- regenerative and waste-absorption capacity; and
- the continued availability and responsible use of land, non-renewable
  resources, and shared ecological inheritance.

This list identifies protected conditions, not a claim that every resource has
the same owner, value, or permissible use. A common condition may cross public,
private, cooperative, customary, household, or Indigenous title. Commons
protection limits destructive use but does not silently nationalize land,
extinguish collective title, or displace the separately ratified Indigenous
consent and self-government rules.

Future generations receive no current `person` entry and no official may claim
to know their preferences. The present duty is narrower and checkable: preserve
the ecological capability, institutional knowledge, and reasonable range of
options needed by whoever later exists to exercise human standing, the floor,
liberty, equality, culture, democratic choice, and remedy.

Any present person, qualified association, the rights advocate, and the Future
Conditions Guardian each has an independent initiation route for a commons or
future-condition duty. No route depends on another claimant being present,
proving a private proprietary injury, or obtaining an entry from an unborn
person.

## 4. Versioned ecological ceilings

Democratic law must enact and publish versioned ecological ceilings, minimum
conditions, and resource budgets for the protected commons. The constitutional
contract supplies no number. Each operative ceiling must identify:

- the protected common, pressure or minimum condition, territory, affected
  population, and legal scope;
- its source version, evidence base, assumptions, uncertainty, and independent
  scientific review;
- cumulative, distributional, cross-regional, supply-chain, and long-latency
  effects;
- the authorized writer, decision body, readers, challenge route, and public
  reason;
- how use is allocated without converting a permit into a right to breach;
- monitoring, correction, restoration, review, supersession, and continuity;
  and
- its fresh source-bound temporal contract.

Ceilings are multidimensional. Climate, water, pollution, soil, biodiversity,
habitat, extraction, and waste may not be collapsed into one sustainability,
productivity, or social-value score. Compliance with one dimension does not
excuse breach of another.

An independently reviewed scientific process proposes or authenticates the
evidence. Democratic law enacts the operative legal ceiling and may choose a
more protective value or method. It may not enact a weaker limit unless public,
independently reviewed evidence establishes that the replacement preserves or
improves the protected outcome. The scientific body supplies evidence, not law;
the legislature supplies law, not scientific truth; the Guardian advocates and
challenges; and an independent court finally reviews legality.

Missing, expired, withheld, or materially incomplete ceiling evidence is a
constitutional failure, not a finding of ecological safety. It cannot authorize
a new or increased high-consequence activity whose compatibility has not been
shown. Existing essential activity needed for immediate floor continuity uses
the least-harm interim route in section 8 while a lawful alternate supplies the
missing review. Review silence never becomes approval.

## 5. Precaution, assessment, and non-regression

Where credible evidence identifies a plausible serious or irreversible harm,
uncertainty does not count as proof of safety. The proponent of the proposed
activity must disclose relevant evidence, assess reasonable alternatives, and
show compliance with the applicable right, ceiling, and least-harm sequence.
The burden concerns the proposed authority; it is not a declaration that the
proponent is guilty of harm.

The precautionary rule is proportional to the threatened consequence,
reversibility, exposure, controllability, and quality of evidence. It is not a
zero-risk rule, a general ban on ordinary life, or authority to withhold the
human floor.

Before a high-consequence authorization, assessment must address:

- direct, indirect, cumulative, compound, distributional, and foreseeable
  cross-boundary effects;
- feasible avoidance and materially less harmful alternatives, including the
  option not to proceed;
- effects on the environmental right, every relevant ceiling, non-human
  animals, protected collectives, workers, and floor continuity;
- local, scientific, and Indigenous knowledge without treating any source as
  self-authenticating;
- uncertainty, dissenting evidence, conflicts of interest, and correction; and
- prevention, monitoring, containment, restoration, and failure response.

Non-regression protects outcomes rather than freezing a particular technology,
agency, permit form, or policy instrument. A replacement is lawful when public
evidence shows equal or stronger protection. Profit, administrative
convenience, a budget choice, or political preference alone cannot justify a
weaker outcome. Corrected science may support recalibration through the same
evidence, participation, reason, and review contract; it cannot operate as an
unstated exception.

No existing permit, title, contract, subsidy, or investment expectation is
permanently grandfathered against a later valid ceiling. Legitimate reliance
may affect a transition or compensation decision under the economic ruling,
but cannot manufacture authority to continue serious harm.

## 6. Avoidance, minimization, restoration, and compensation

Ecological decisions follow this ordered sequence:

1. **Avoid** the harm where a feasible compatible route exists.
2. **Minimize** every unavoidable effect at its source and over its full
   duration.
3. **Restore in place** the affected ecological function, habitat, and access
   as far as restoration is possible.
4. Use **compensatory ecological measures** only for a residual effect that is
   genuinely replaceable and only after the first three duties are met.
5. Provide human and collective compensation or reparation for remaining
   rights harm without treating payment as ecological compliance.

An offset, credit, payment, or restoration elsewhere may not:

- enlarge or excuse breach of an ecological ceiling;
- substitute for an irreplaceable, unique, sacred, or functionally
  non-substitutable system;
- erase local environmental, equality, health, cultural, title, or consent
  harm;
- rely on double counting, an unauthenticated baseline, impermanent protection,
  or harm merely shifted to another place or time; or
- turn an irreversible loss into a successful outcome.

Where equivalence is lawfully claimed for a replaceable residual effect, it
requires independently reviewed evidence of additionality, durability,
location and function relevance, no double counting, monitoring, correction,
and failure repair. Book 2 must operate those measurements; Nibli does not prove
them.

## 7. Tiered liability and restoration

Prevention, cessation, containment, and continuity do not wait for a final
liability judgment or proof of fault. On credible evidence of threatened harm,
the responsible public authority must protect people and animals, prevent or
stop the effect, and begin necessary restoration while causation, contribution,
culpability, and cost allocation are decided. Those no-fault protective duties
do not themselves establish civil liability or personal guilt.

Liability is tiered:

1. The operator and legally responsible controller of an inherently hazardous
   activity bear strict liability for containment, restoration, and reasonable
   response costs when the activity's causal connection to the harm is
   established. Intent, negligence, regulatory compliance, a permit, and
   contractual risk allocation do not defeat that restorative liability. An
   activity is inherently hazardous where its nature leaves a material risk of
   serious, widespread, long-lived, or irreversible harm despite competent
   care; the classification, causal connection, scope, and costs remain
   evidence-based, public, challengeable, and independently reviewable.
2. Outside that strict tier, a public or private actor that causes, directs,
   authorizes, or exercises material control over the harmful act bears
   responsibility in proportion to its adjudicated contribution and control.
3. A controlling owner, parent enterprise, financier, or beneficiary bears
   responsibility only where an adjudicated finding establishes direction,
   material control, knowing facilitation, concealment, evasion, asset
   stripping, or deliberate undercapitalization. Investment, employment, or
   membership alone is insufficient.
4. Where the responsible actor is unknown, insolvent, absent, or unable to act
   in time, the public restoration duty continues immediately. Law may recover
   costs later without making recovery a condition of repair.

Liability requires evidence, notice, reasons, a hearing, proportionality,
review, and correction. No person inherits ecological guilt, and no worker,
resident, shareholder, family member, or collective member is liable solely by
status. Enterprise form and limited liability remain statutory tools, not
permission to evade restoration. Strict restorative or contribution-based
liability does not establish criminal guilt or authorize personal punishment.
Any punitive or criminal consequence requires a separately adjudicated
culpable act and mental state under the full justice and due-process contract.

Remedies may include cessation, prevention, cleanup, restoration, replacement
of an unlawful permit, record correction, monitoring, a restoration bond,
licence action, structural separation, receivership, restitution,
compensation, reparation, and non-repetition. An ecological remedy cannot
remove another person's standing, floor, core liberty, due process, equal
political weight, or protected collective title.

## 8. Dual floor-and-ceiling continuity

The material floor and ecological ceiling constrain each other without either
becoming an excuse to erase the other.

- A ceiling cannot be met by denying food, water, housing, care, energy needed
  for the floor, mobility access, or remedy to people selected by wealth,
  status, disability, geography, custody, migration, contribution, or score.
- A floor cannot be declared delivered by exhausting water, soil, climate,
  biodiversity, habitat, or another condition required for future delivery.
- Where no currently available route satisfies both, the public body must
  preserve immediate human continuity through the least-harm route, record the
  floor and/or ceiling shortfall honestly, obtain alternatives, repair harm,
  and operate a source-bound transition.
- A managed breach remains a constitutional failure. Neither a reduced floor
  nor an exceeded ceiling may be renamed success.
- Scarcity ordering remains governed by the economic decision. Budget choice,
  delay, monopoly, artificial withholding, or refusal to procure is not
  physical or ecological scarcity.

Ecological policy may never authorize coerced sterilization, forced pregnancy
or abortion, birth or family penalties, eugenic selection, discriminatory
migration or custody, loss of human standing, or a productivity, lifespan,
genetic, contribution, or ecological-worth score. The separately ratified
reproductive, equality, and collective-rights rules remain intact.

No standing emergency suspension or requisition power is created here. A
temporary ecological or animal-protection departure during a genuine emergency
requires the emergency ruling's trigger, scope, non-derogable core,
reasons, fresh temporal contract, review, continuity, remedy, and restoration.
That ruling landed on 2026-08-08 and supplies each of those, with one result
worth stating here: it adopts **no derogation clause at all**, so an emergency
never suspends an ecological ceiling or an animal protection. It confers only
procedural acceleration, resource redirection, compensated requisition, and
hazard-specific reviewable restriction, each separately justified and separately
renewable. A departure that nonetheless occurs is recorded as a breach and
repaired; it is never renamed compliance, and restoration remains owed. See
[`public-safety-defence-emergency-and-external-power-decision.md`](public-safety-defence-emergency-and-external-power-decision.md).

## 9. Future Conditions Guardian

Create a collegial **Future Conditions Guardian** as an independent
constitutional advocacy and review institution. It is not the legislature,
regulator, scientific authority, court, auditor, or operator.

Appointments must follow the state-form anti-capture contract: open
nominations, reasoned qualification review, divided selectors, mixed relevant
expertise and lived knowledge, staggered nonrenewable mandates, cause-only
removal, and no majority appointment control by the current government, either
chamber, one party coalition, industry, profession, advocacy tendency, or
appointing source. Funding, disclosure, recusals, minority reasons, removal,
vacancy, and replacement must be public and challengeable.

The Guardian may:

- obtain lawful information and commission independently reviewable evidence;
- require a responsible body to publish an assessment, evidence, uncertainty,
  alternatives, and reasons;
- facilitate accessible participation and receive objections;
- publish supported warnings and recommendations;
- challenge a ceiling, permit, omission, regression, restoration failure, or
  cross-boundary evasion;
- initiate independent review and seek interim or final remedies; and
- audit whether a final decision and remedy were received and acted upon.

A present person, qualified association, or rights advocate may use its
independent initiation route to request judicial interim relief. That request
does not itself trigger an automatic constitutional pause.

A published objection by the collegial Guardian—or by its predeclared alternate
advocate acting in its place—supported by credible evidence of serious or
irreversible harm automatically pauses only the irreversible part of the
challenged authorization pending expedited independent review. The pause
requires its own fresh source-bound T3 contract, preserves essential floor
continuity, and cannot become an indefinite administrative hold. The independent
reviewer, not the Guardian or alternate advocate, decides the merits.

The stay uses one case-bound Guardian-stay replay key binding the exact
challenged authorization version, serious-or-irreversible-harm ground, and
reviewed evidence set. The Guardian, its alternate advocate, any successor
office, and every reviewer share that key. None may restart the same stay on an
unchanged key. Only materially new evidence or a materially changed authorization
creates a new key and may support a new stay.

Guardian silence, vacancy, conflict, capture, removal, refusal, or incapacity
never becomes approval. A predeclared alternate advocate preserves the
Guardian's initiation and automatic-pause route when the Guardian cannot act. A
separately predeclared independent substitute reviewer decides the stay and
merits when the ordinary reviewer is
unavailable, conflicted, or captured. A conflicted Guardian member may not
participate, and a conflict affecting the body's quorum activates the alternate
advocate. The stay's temporal contract must prevent any unavailable initiator or
reviewer from becoming either approval or an indefinite hold.

The Guardian has no final veto, policy programme, spending or taxation power,
permit-operating role, scientific-oracle status, authority to speak an unborn
person's preferences, or power to compel a private lifestyle. Its findings are
evidence-bearing public acts subject to challenge, correction, audit, and
review.

## 10. Federal, property, trade, and democratic limits

The common tier owns constitutional minima and the legal framework for climate,
interregional waters and airsheds, cross-regional ecological effects,
portability, common reporting, and external commons coordination. Regions and
localities own residual land, service, development, and local-resource policy
under subsidiarity and may enact stronger protection. They may not lower a
common minimum, export harm internally, or obstruct floor portability.

Indigenous collective title, internal government, knowledge, and the ratified
consent rules remain protected. Ecological regulation may limit a harmful use
but may not use conservation as a pretext for dispossession, assimilation, or
exclusion. Existential harms still require actual collective consent; other
material effects require consultation, information, time, accommodation,
reasons, and review.

Property is conditional on the environmental right and commons. No title,
permit, extraction concession, emissions allowance, contract, subsidy, patent,
or creditor claim creates a right to breach a ceiling or avoid restoration.
Preventing unlawful harm is not automatically a compulsory acquisition.
Compensation for legitimate reliance remains governed by the economic
decision and never purchases authority to continue an unlawful act.

Democratic law chooses the lawful mixture of prevention, standards, public
provision, regulation, taxation, incentives, rationing within the scarcity
contract, and restoration. A majority may not vote away the environmental
right, a protected common, an independently established ceiling, or the
existing Indigenous consent boundary.

No public or private actor may evade the substantive baseline through trade,
finance, procurement, contracting, an affiliate, a supply chain, import or
export, flags of convenience, or jurisdiction shopping. The
external-power ruling of 2026-08-08 decides treaty, customs, sanctions, border,
and international-enforcement authority. This ruling supplies the no-evasion
principle, not those powers or operations; that ruling generalises the principle
across every domain, subordinates treaties and trade measures to the corridor,
and adds extradition, mutual legal assistance, and transfer of a person as the
enforcement route that must not become an escape. See
[`public-safety-defence-emergency-and-external-power-decision.md`](public-safety-defence-emergency-and-external-power-decision.md).

## 11. Class 10: non-human-animal welfare and integrity

The constitutional taxonomy gains a distinct **Class 10: non-human-animal
welfare and integrity**. Its question is:

> What direct bodily, life, and species-appropriate interests must public and
> private human conduct respect in a credibly sentient non-human animal?

Class 10 is author-chosen and not derived. It is not a tenth form of human
standing or material floor. A rule belongs here only where its direct legal
effect protects an individual non-human animal or limits conduct toward that
animal. Habitat, biodiversity, species survival, and ecosystem function remain
Class 9 effects. Investigation and remedy use Class 4; public and advocate
duties use Class 6; records use Class 7; territorial authority uses Class 8.
Mixed effects require separate cards.

Class 10 protects **credibly sentient animals**. At minimum, all vertebrates,
cephalopod molluscs, and decapod crustaceans are presumptively covered. An
independent, public, evidence-responsive process must extend the presumption to
another taxon where credible evidence establishes a realistic possibility of
sentience. Conclusive proof is not required. A taxon may not be removed merely
because uncertainty remains; removal requires comparably rigorous public
evidence and the non-regression process.

Credibly sentient animals are direct protected subjects, not constitutional
persons. They receive no `person` status, human equality ground, material floor,
ballot, candidacy, public office, property title, contract capacity, or inherent
political weight. Their interests are not merely the owner's property interest,
a human preference, or an ecosystem proxy.

The direct interests are:

- bodily integrity and freedom from deliberate or reckless severe or prolonged
  avoidable pain, fear, distress, injury, disease, and deprivation;
- continued life as a serious, non-absolute interest;
- species-appropriate nourishment, water, shelter, movement, social and
  behavioural opportunity, rest, care, and a safe environment where a human
  actor controls or creates dependency;
- freedom from abandonment, exploitative overwork, extreme confinement,
  harmful breeding, and avoidable invasive intervention; and
- humane handling, transport, treatment, rescue, and death where death is
  lawfully justified.

The constitution protects the conditions for welfare; it does not certify that
an animal is happy, healthy, fulfilled, tame, useful, or behaving normally.
Protection varies with relevant evidence about the species, individual, and
effect, never with owner wealth, market price, human affection, recognition,
productivity, rarity alone, or a global animal-worth score.

Property or contract may allocate custody, stewardship, costs, risk, and
transfer. It does not make an animal an ordinary chattel, permit waiver of the
animal's interests, or give an owner a veto over emergency rescue. Custody,
seizure, transfer, rehabilitation, and rehoming must be decided through the
animal's protected interests, affected human rights, evidence, due process, and
continuity.

## 12. Minimum duties and categorical refusals

Every public or private actor controlling a covered animal owes conditions
appropriate to the animal's evidence-based needs and the nature and duration of
control. Public institutions must prevent, investigate, and remedy private
interference and provide an effective rescue, veterinary, shelter,
rehabilitation, and placement route where a controller fails or is absent.

The following are constitutionally refused:

- animal fighting, baiting, sexual use, deliberate cruelty, and punitive
  treatment;
- killing or severe or prolonged suffering solely for spectacle, gambling,
  amusement, sport, trophy, prestige, fashion, cosmetic or marketing testing,
  novelty, convenience, or profit;
- abandonment or denial of necessary care by a person who created or assumed
  control or dependency;
- extreme confinement that prevents basic movement or species-important
  behaviour and predictably causes serious harm;
- breeding or genetic selection that predictably produces serious suffering,
  disability, or inability to perform basic biological functions;
- a painful non-therapeutic mutilation without a serious lawful purpose,
  necessity, effective pain control, and no materially less harmful route; and
- an unrelieved severe or prolonged research procedure.

No property, contract, ownership form, commercial licence, research label,
religion, culture, custom, tradition, or regional heritage creates an automatic
exemption. Genuine subsistence, Indigenous and collective rights, conscience,
and religious practice receive meaningful consultation and accommodation, but
not authority to erase the severe-avoidable-suffering core. Any restriction
must remain evidence-based, equal, least restrictive of human rights, and open
to review.

The direct protected-subject status and the prohibitions on severe avoidable
suffering and dispensable killing join the unamendable democratic corridor.
Detailed species standards, regulatory methods, and uses that pass the
constitutional test remain subject to democratic revision inside that core.

## 13. Enhanced serious-purpose use

A lethal, invasive, or high-severity use of a credibly sentient animal is
lawful only where all of these are
established:

1. the purpose is serious and constitutionally compatible;
2. credible evidence shows the use is necessary to that purpose;
3. no safe, accessible, effective, and materially less harmful alternative is
   reasonably available;
4. number, duration, pain, distress, confinement, injury, and risk are avoided
   or minimized through the least-harm method;
5. species-appropriate care, monitoring, humane endpoints, and aftercare are
   secured; and
6. every high-consequence use receives independent prior review, reasons,
   records, challenge, reassessment, a source-bound end, and remedy.

Serious purposes may include immediate nourishment where the strict food rule
below is met; prevention or treatment of serious human or animal illness;
protection from a grave safety or public-health threat; evidence-based
conservation or ecological restoration; assistance or work compatible with the
animal's welfare; and qualifying scientific research under section 15. A label
does not prove the purpose, necessity, or absence of alternatives.

Companionship, rescue, sanctuary, voluntary observation, and mutually safe
coexistence are lawful without pretending that affection supplies welfare.
Working and assistance animals require workload limits, rest, care, free exit
from harmful conditions through the responsible public route, and retirement
or placement continuity.

Euthanasia is lawful only to prevent otherwise unrelievable suffering or to
address another serious purpose that passes the full test, on current qualified
evidence and by the least distressing reliable method. Owner inconvenience,
shelter delay, ordinary treatment cost, or reduced commercial value alone is
insufficient.

## 14. Strict food rule and transition

Any controlled use of a credibly sentient animal to produce food, including
nonlethal or non-severe production as well as killing or severe harm, is lawful
only where no safe, accessible, nutritionally adequate, and materially less
harmful alternative is reasonably available to the affected people.
Taste, habit, prestige, profit, advertising, or price alone does not satisfy the
test. Cost and geography remain relevant only to whether an alternative is
actually accessible without withholding the human food floor.

Where the strict test is met, breeding, housing, handling, transport, and
killing remain subject to Class 10, independent inspection, species-specific
standards, effective stunning or an independently established least-suffering
method, records, challenge, and remedy. A lawful food purpose never licenses
avoidable suffering.

The public duty is to make safe, adequate, accessible less-harm nutrition
available and to support a just, source-bound transition. No person may lose
food because an alternative system, supply chain, worker transition, or local
capacity is not yet ready. Workers, Indigenous and subsistence communities,
producers, and regions retain equality, due process, participation, livelihood
transition, and floor continuity. Delay or underinvestment remains a public
failure, not permanent permission for avoidable harm.

Book 1 establishes the destination and failure rule. Book 2 owns nutrition and
supply evidence, production, procurement, prices, transition sequences,
worker/community support, veterinary and inspection capacity, and empirical
feasibility.

## 15. Strict research and education rule

Use of a covered animal in research, testing, or education requires:

- a serious purpose expected on reviewed evidence to advance human or animal
  health, safety, or ecological protection;
- systematic replacement, reduction, and refinement, with replacement first;
- proof that no scientifically valid non-animal or materially less harmful
  method can achieve the purpose;
- independent project-level scientific and ethical review separated from the
  investigator, funder, facility, and final reviewer;
- the minimum animals and least harmful species-appropriate design compatible
  with valid evidence;
- pain prevention and relief, humane endpoints, care, aftercare, rehabilitation
  or rehoming where appropriate, and a current source-bound authorization;
- prospective registration and public result and welfare reporting, including
  negative or inconclusive results, subject only to narrow safety and privacy
  protection; and
- inspection, challenge, correction, suspension, remedy, and non-repetition.

An unrelieved severe or prolonged procedure is categorically prohibited. It
cannot be authorized by anticipated prestige, commercial advantage, general
curiosity, duplicative teaching, cosmetic or marketing purpose, or a claim that
possible benefit is impossible to quantify. Law must pursue full replacement
as soon as scientifically valid alternatives can perform the relevant purpose.

## 16. Domestic, farmed, captive, and wild animals

Companion and domestic animals receive the direct Class 10 protections. A
missing chip, pedigree, licence, owner record, or proof of purchase cannot block
emergency rescue or necessary care. Registration coordinates responsibility;
it is not the source of protection.

Farmed and working animals receive the same direct core regardless of public,
cooperative, household, customary, nonprofit, or private ownership. An
ownership label, food or labour purpose, or sector licence cannot turn a
prohibited condition into welfare.

Captive wildlife, animals used in display, and animals held for trade receive
the same direct protection plus species-appropriate habitat, enrichment,
movement, social, migration, and release considerations. Conservation is not a
label that proves captivity or breeding necessary.

Wild animals are protected against deliberate cruelty, unlawful capture,
trade, and human-caused or human-controlled harm. Public duty prioritizes
prevention and remedy for pollution, infrastructure, entanglement, habitat
destruction, introduced hazards, unlawful killing, and dependency created by
human conduct.

Ordinary natural predation is not a constitutional offence, creates no guilty
animal, and imposes no universal public duty to suppress ecological processes
or rescue every wild individual from natural illness, hunger, or death. A
bounded intervention may be lawful where evidence shows it will reduce grave
suffering without disproportionate ecological harm.

Disease control, dangerous-animal response, and management of a
human-introduced population causing grave ecological or welfare harm must:

1. establish the threat through authenticated, contestable evidence;
2. address human causes and prevention first;
3. use feasible nonlethal prevention, exclusion, treatment, vaccination,
   relocation, fertility control, or habitat measures before killing;
4. permit lethal action only where those routes are unavailable, ineffective,
   or would cause greater grave harm;
5. use the least painful reliable method with qualified oversight;
6. protect public and worker safety, Indigenous and collective rights, and
   ecosystem integrity; and
7. publish reasons, provide challenge and audit, reassess, repair, and end under
   a fresh source-bound T3 contract.

No species, population, predator, or individual animal is guilty, criminal,
inferior, invasive as a moral status, or punishable. Law manages evidenced
risk and human responsibility; it does not create an animal conviction or
social-usefulness score.

## 17. Animal Protection Advocate

Create a separate independent **Animal Protection Advocate**. It cannot be the
Future Conditions Guardian because ecological protection and an individual
animal's interests can conflict. The Advocate is not an owner, universal
custodian, regulator, prosecutor, veterinarian, scientific oracle, court, or
operator.

Appointments follow the same divided, open, mixed-expertise, staggered,
nonrenewable, cause-only-removal, anti-concentration, disclosure, recusal,
vacancy, and alternate-route requirements as the Guardian. No government,
industry, profession, animal-use sector, advocacy tendency, or appointing
source may control a majority.

An affected human, chosen supporter, qualified association, veterinarian or
other authorized professional, public regulator, or the Advocate may initiate
the appropriate protection route. An animal's owner or controller has no
exclusive claim and cannot waive or settle away the animal's direct interests.
Representation may not erase the interests of affected people or collectives.

The Advocate may obtain lawful information, request inspection and evidence,
publish supported findings, initiate review, seek interim rescue or cessation,
and pursue individual or systemic remedies. It has no final veto, permit,
budget, programme, prosecution, or adjudication power. Silence, conflict,
capture, removal, refusal, or vacancy activates a predeclared alternate and
cannot make a prohibited use lawful.

Where the Guardian's ecological position conflicts with the Advocate's animal
position, neither has priority by office. The independent adjudicator must keep
the human right, human floor, Class 9 condition, Class 10 interests,
Indigenous/collective rights, alternatives, uncertainty, reversibility, and
continuity visible as separate axes; apply every hard prohibition first; and
give public reasons for the least-harm compatible route. It may not turn them
into one utility or worth score.

## 18. Evidence, records, and remedies

Animal and ecological findings require authorized sources, writers, evidence,
visibility and privacy boundaries, independent readers, reasons, challenge,
correction, retention, deletion control, carry, end conditions, alternate
routes, continuity, remedy, audit, and failure polarity.

Species-appropriate observation, veterinary findings, behavioural and physical
indicators, ecological evidence, and affected-community knowledge may inform a
case. No provider, owner, proponent, guardian, advocate, regulator, scientist,
or complainant may self-certify the decisive conclusion. Uncertainty and
disagreement must remain visible.

No universal individual wild-animal registry is required. A missing identity,
owner, taxon classification, or record cannot block urgent rescue where a
covered interest plausibly exists. Serve or protect first where necessary, then
reconcile the record. Purpose-limited animal records may not enter the
canonical consequential person record or become evidence of a human's worth,
politics, family, migration, risk, or entitlement.

Animal remedies include cessation, rescue, veterinary treatment,
rehabilitation, sanctuary or rehoming, custody or use disqualification,
inspection, record correction, restitution for care, licence action,
structural reform, monitoring, habitat restoration, and guarantees of
non-repetition. Criminal or coercive consequences for a human require a
separately adjudicated intentional or reckless serious violation and the full
justice contract. An animal is never punished.

No animal remedy may remove a person's standing, floor, core liberty, due
process, equal political weight, or protected collective right. No human
compensation payment extinguishes the duty to rescue, treat, restore, or stop
recurrence.
