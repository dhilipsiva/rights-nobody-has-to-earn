# Credibility findings: grounds, endings and answerability

Ruled 2026-09-18 under the standing delegated approval, against the revision
tracker's *resolve before defending* rule. It changes Article 4's voiding rule
and Article 8's expungement reach, adds two duties, and records the
alternatives examined for the one limitation that survives.

## What the finding was, measured before changing it

`false/1` had five producers and five readers. Measured against the source at
`fa88f79e`:

- **Producers.** The multi-sig examination rule; the adjudicated-lie rule; the
  Article 5 conflict rule (a judge who judged their own child); the reconciled
  carried mark; and Article 9's dead amendment proposal, whose subject is a
  proposal and not a person.
- **Readers.** The three recognition doors under negation, the clawback leaf,
  and Article 9's law label under negation. Nothing else. No floor actuality,
  no `owe`, no `entitled`, no `person`, no `prisoner`, no `travel`, no `decide`,
  no `authority` and no `permits` reads it — so a credibility finding reaches
  recognition and one recorded loss, and the credential doors read the
  reconciled carried mark rather than this conclusion.

Two defects followed from that measurement.

**The finding had no ground.** Two documented examinations by two
independently credentialed, independently found signers sufficed. The sibling
rule in the same article already required `cite($auditor, $ground, $audited)`
before paying an examiner for the identical look, so the design paid for a
reason it did not require before acting on it.

**Expungement reached one route in three.** `forgive(Appeals, $x)` beside
`judge(Appeals, $x)` derives `clean($x)`, and only the carried mark read it.
Measured: with both entries recorded, `clean(Bela)` derives and `false(Bela)`
derives anyway, because the original pair of examinations is still in the
record. The appeals body could declare a voiding spent and the record would
disagree with it in the same snapshot.

A third finding was coverage rather than design. `false(Tyr)` was blocked twice
over — by the carried void the chapter argues from, and by two independence
findings nobody had written for that pair — so `counterfactual/unguarded-pen`'s
arming pin passed without testing the conjuncts it exists to justify.

## What changed

- **Both signers must cite the same ground, and the ground must be one of a
  closed list.** Two conditions. The shared half binds the pair to one subject
  matter. The closed half separates a finding from the occasion of an
  examination, and it is not optional: `cite` is also what the examiner-reward
  rule reads, an examiner is paid for looking on any recorded ground, and
  without the closure two examiners who looked on a complaint and found nothing
  would void the person they cleared. The vocabulary is
  `CredibilityGroundVocabulary` — a recorded lie, a fabricated record, a
  concealed conflict, evidence withheld — built the way every landed family
  builds one: `member` is conclusion-only and one rule per admitted value is
  what closes it. No new relation name; four new rules plus two conjuncts; rule
  count 7082 → 7088, predicates, derived predicates, strata and the floor
  unchanged. The shipped cast keeps `cite(Gia, Complaint, Bela)` — the occasion,
  which still pays — and gains the adverse ground beside it, so `false(Bela)` is
  unmoved. Every exhibit in chapter 25's pins carries its own admitted ground,
  so the conjunct each exhibit is about stays the only failing one.
- **Every route that turns on a finding reads the expungement.** `~clean` on
  the multi-sig rule and on the adjudicated-lie rule, beside the carried mark
  that already read it.
- **Article 5's conflict rule deliberately does not.** That void tracks a
  judgment still on the record and still conflicted; forgiving the judge while
  the judgment stands leaves the conflict in force and calls it repaired. The
  repair there is to withdraw the judgment. The split is asserted by membership
  in `floor_vector_tests::a_recorded_expungement_reaches_every_voiding_that_turns_on_a_finding`.
- **Two duties.** `person($x) & false($x) -> obliged(Review,
  ExplainTheGroundOfACredibilityFinding, $x)` — the audit body owes the person
  a finding was made against, with personhood keeping it off Article 9's
  proposals. It is written in the typed three-place form because the two-place
  `obliged(Review, ·)` is Article 8b's marker duty, and a second producer of it
  made chapter 30's "silent where the markers are silent" untestable; the full
  verifier caught that as two findings before the change landed. And
  `false($x) & challenge($x, $ground, CredibilityReview) -> obliged(Appeals, $x)`
  — the subject's own contest of the cited ground lands a review duty, needing
  nobody's permission. Neither withdraws the finding; neither is read by
  anything, so neither is evidence that a reason was given or a review happened.
- **The coverage repair.** `authorized(Vex, IndependentReviewer, Tyr)` and its
  pair for Wren, so the carried void is the only bar left, plus
  `counterfactual/unguarded-pen-and-no-conjuncts`, which applies the unguarded
  credential route and the stripped conjuncts together and pins `false(Tyr)`
  TRUE. The two sibling counterfactuals are armed by it rather than by a claim
  in a comment.
- **The reach is censused.**
  `floor_vector_tests::a_credibility_voiding_reaches_exactly_the_measured_set`
  asserts the reader set by membership with a sabotage control, because the
  chapter states that set as the whole consequence.

A fifth defect surfaced while repairing the first, and it is the one worth
carrying: **a ground alone does not separate a finding from a clean
examination.** Requiring both signers to cite the same ground closes the
"documented examinations with nothing behind them" hole and leaves a worse one
open, because the entry that names a ground is the entry an examiner is paid
for and looking on a complaint is a perfectly good reason to look. Two
examiners who cleared somebody would have voided them. The closed vocabulary is
the repair; the first version of this ruling did not have it.

## What was not done, and why

**`false/1` is not made case-scoped.** The ask was a case-specific finding with
a bounded consequence rather than a general statement that a person's word no
longer counts. The finding is now case-specific in its premises — a shared
cited ground, case-bound independence findings for the named subject — and its
consequence is bounded and measured. An extra place on the head would add
nothing to either: the measured reach is three recognition doors and one
recorded loss, none of which is case-shaped, and an arity change on a derived
relation measured at roughly twenty-two times the runtime of the suite it sits
in when `reward` was tried. The residual is recorded rather than renamed: the
conclusion is unary, and what makes it narrow is the reader census, not the
arity.

**Nothing expires.** A voiding ends by a positive act and not by silence. An
ending that ran while nobody acted would clear the findings of exactly the
people nobody reviewed, which is the population an instrument of this kind is
most likely to have got wrong, and the design has no clock to run it on. This
is the polarity release already has.

**A voided person can still sign, inside one record.** The alternatives were
examined and each is worse:

- *Gate the pen on the conclusion.* `permits` would read `~false`, and `false`
  reads `permits`. The stratifier refuses it, and the refusal is not
  incidental: the question would have to answer itself before it could be
  asked. This is the same wall that protects the floor.
- *Gate the pen on the raw mark.* `rotten` is one unsigned entry. A rule
  reading it would let a single write close anybody's pen — the finding with no
  finder this design refuses everywhere else.
- *Gate the pen on a witnessed order.* The order machinery can say one event
  preceded another, but the thing being ordered is still the conclusion, so
  reading it closes the same loop by a longer route.

What the pen requires instead is positive: a clearance witnessed into the
current record and agreed by two public bodies, with a carried void closing it.
So the policy holds across records and fails inside one. The window is a record
rather than an era; the cost falls on whoever is examined inside it; the other
signer must still be independently credentialed, clean, independently found and
citing the same ground; and what would close it is a representation in which a
credential can read an ordered finding without reading the conclusion that
finding is. Nothing in the design has one, and inventing it is not a prose
change.

**Nothing here decides whether a finding is true.** Two compromised signers
holding real credentials, affirmatively found independent, citing the same
ground, void an innocent exactly as smoothly as two honest ones void a fraud.
That is the ceiling on conditions of this kind, it is the same ceiling
`tests/pins/red-team/collusion-three-attesters-who-agree` pins elsewhere, and
the price the conditions exact is that every step carries a name and a route to
contest it.
