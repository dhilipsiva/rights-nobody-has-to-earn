# Book 1 Delivery and Receipt Decision

**Author-ratified 2026-08-18.** Three questions were put and answered: which
floor items receive an arrival route, who may write the recipient-side receipt
for a person who can acknowledge through no channel, and whether the routes ship
with cast arrivals. This record states the rulings, the ratified material they
rest on, and the boundary they do not cross.

The ruling is **ratified and, at the moment of writing, unimplemented**. It
creates no predicate, rule, pin, fixture, chapter, defect resolution, posture
change, or public claim by itself.

## 1. Decision

**Which items.** Delivery and receipt routes are enacted for `eats`, `secure`,
`meets`, `healthy`, and `dwell`. `learn` keeps the existing teaching route
unchanged. `believe` and `expresses` receive no delivery route and are recorded
as deliberate, reasoned omissions rather than gaps.

**Who writes.** The recipient-side record is written by an authorised writer who
is not the provider, and the constitution enforces the separation by requiring an
independent attestation distinct from the source. Where the ordinary writer
cannot act, a **predeclared** alternate carries the route, on the pattern already
ratified for the Future Conditions Guardian.

**Dormancy.** The routes ship **dormant**. No cast member receives anything, so
nothing new derives in the shipped record, and every existing "derives" sentence
in the book stays true. Whether the cast later exercises a route is a separate
decision, taken when the rules exist and the prose consequences can be measured
rather than predicted.

## 2. What this rests on, and does not reopen

- **The interface shape.** Delivery families use accessibility-neutral,
  recipient-side access or receipt evidence with a named authorised writer and a
  challenge route, and may secure conditions but may not certify learning,
  health, belief, or another compelled personal state.
- **No provider self-certification.** A provider's own assertion cannot alone
  establish delivery or receipt, and cash, vouchers or insurance count as
  delivery only where independent recipient-side evidence establishes access.
- **No floor becomes conditional.** Price and ability to pay cannot gate the
  floor, and a missing receipt invites outreach and challenge rather than
  terminating an entitlement.
- **The legacy route is not the template.** `hears` is audio-literal and
  uncontrolled and `learn` is a personal outcome, so the teaching route
  demonstrates the shape without supplying the pattern.

## 3. Which items, and why the line falls there

Three groups, and the middle one is where the ruling does work.

**Material conditions — `eats`, `secure`, `meets`, `dwell`.** Something reaches a
person and the reaching is recordable without describing an inner state. These
are the straightforward cases.

**`healthy` — care, not health.** The book's own fixed gloss of the floor reads
"To be safe. To eat. To have somewhere to live. **To be cared for when ill.** To
learn. To speak. To believe what you believe. To have the company of other
people." The floor item is care, and a care-delivery route deriving `healthy` is
therefore consistent with what the book already says the item is. It is not a
workaround for the bar on certifying a personal state: nothing derives that a
person is well, only that care reached them.

**`learn` is already routed.** The teaching route stands unchanged, and this
ruling adds nothing to it.

## 4. The items deliberately left underived

**`believe` is a category error, on the source's own account.** The comment above
its entitlement line records that a heresy law is writable in committed corpus
words with no lexicon extension, and that the entitlement line "is what refuses
it". The entitlement exists to refuse a law, not to deliver a state. Certifying
that a person believes is the compelled personal state the design refuses, and
the firewall exists to stop belief being read at all. A delivery route here would
not close a gap; it would build the instrument the line was written against.

**`expresses` is not a clean liberty case, and the distinction must be stated.**
It already has a producer — a confined person's words go into the record — and
the book treats that as a genuine arrival, not a decoration. So the claim is
narrower than for `believe`: **the liberty of expression is not delivered, while
the record of speech can arrive.** The liberty shell carries the freedom as a
person-held limit on power, using constants that are deliberately not joined to
the floor predicate. Do not wire them together.

Both omissions are recorded as closed classification decisions with risk-based
reasons, on the established pattern, so that a later reader finds a decision
rather than a silence.

## 5. The receipt writer

The general rule is already ratified and uses the operative word: no required
reviewer may turn silence into approval, and **an unavailable writer** or
reviewer may not obtain a permanent withholding veto, because every consequential
route needs a predeclared alternate or escalation path with the same limits and
independent review. A recipient who can acknowledge through no channel is an
unavailable writer. This ruling is an instantiation, not a new principle.

**The separation is enforced in the rule, not left to practice.** The
recipient-side record and the attestation are distinct facts, the attesting
witness must hold an authorised writer role for that person, and the witness must
be distinct from the source. That distinctness conjunct is the family's
load-bearing guard: it is the whole of what stops a provider establishing that it
delivered.

**The writer model is the independent inspection function, not the person's own
advocate.** The rights advocate carries no individualized adverse determination
by ratified design, and the record states that where the bounded
best-interpretation route names no maker, the silence is recorded rather than
filled by the person's own advocate. Making the advocate the receipt writer would
override that. The pattern to follow instead is the independent function whose
job is to issue **challengeable** records, barred from making a record
unchallengeable, with the inspected party barred from certifying the conclusion.

**The best-interpretation route is the right shape and the wrong inputs.** Its
qualifiers — conflict-free, decision-specific, least-restrictive, time-bounded,
independently reviewable — govern any finding here. But its mandatory inputs are
known values, past expressions and relationships the person chose, and a newborn
has none of them by construction. The shape transfers; the inputs do not, and
stretching them would be dishonest.

**Absence is a trigger, never a finding.** A missing receipt invites outreach and
challenge. It cannot establish non-delivery, because no power may treat an absent
entry as a finding, and because a marker keyed on bare absence fires on everyone
while the record holds no arrivals — the measured reason the floor-delivery
markers were refused. Absence-based marking remains refused until arrivals exist,
and its expiry condition is unchanged.

## 6. Dormancy

The routes exist and derive nothing, because no cast member receives anything.
This follows the teaching route's precedent, where cast exercise was declined so
that the chapters describing the delivery gap stayed true.

Two consequences are stated rather than left to be discovered. Every "derives"
sentence in the book remains literally true, including the shelter and recorded
speech sentences, because dormancy changes no verdict. And the prose must still
change, because a design with five dormant delivery routes is materially
different from one with none, and describing it as the latter would be a material
omission. Dormant is not blind: the routes are proven by probe, and the pins that
prove them are the evidence that the machinery is real.

## 7. What this does not establish

Nothing here makes anything arrive. The family makes an arrival **representable**
and derives a bounded consequence from supplied records. It does not establish
that any floor item reaches any person, and no claim that it does may be written.

The distinction is mechanical, not rhetorical. The claim that an interface exists
is a formal-entailment claim and may become established. The claim that a floor
item actually reaches a free person is a **liveness** claim, and liveness may
never be Derived, Checked, or Reasoned; its only established posture is Evidenced
through operational assurance, which is not available. That claim therefore stays
Unestablished, its defect stays open, and both remain so until Book 2 supplies
operational assurance in a calibrated envelope.

Production, authentication, and persistence of the records this family consumes
are outside the engine. Nothing here detects a person never entered, authenticates
a witness, proves a writer honest, or makes a service exist.

## 8. Formal migration contract

- Add exactly one predicate, the recipient-side arrival relation, curated at
  arity 3 with recipient, gift and donor places and a stative source sense. It is
  an independent relation and must never become a converse alias of a giving
  relation, because an alias would let the giver's assertion establish receipt.
- Reuse the existing two-place-and-scope attestation relation, the existing
  authorised-role relation, and the existing distinctness idiom. Add constants,
  not predicates, for the scopes and the writer roles.
- Do not reuse the record-carry relation, which is taken, and do not add a
  provider-side relation, which buys no derivation the recipient-side record does
  not already carry.
- Do not require roster membership in the delivery rules. Help is not conditioned
  on a record entry, so a person never entered whose arrival is witnessed still
  derives the actuality.
- Declaring the floor actualities conclusion-only closes the fiat **fact** and
  leaves the fiat **rule** open. It is worth doing on its own merits and must
  never be described as preventing fiat.
- The stated defence against a fiat rule is upstream evidence, and the question
  is structural: what arrival evidence sits upstream of this head? That question
  should become a checked guard with a watched-failing control.

## 9. Book 1, Book 2, and the engine

Book 1 owns the interface: who may write, what independence is required, what
follows from a record, what a missing record triggers, the challenge route, and
the failure polarity. Book 2 owns logistics, staffing, procurement, budgets,
routing, maintenance, capacity, graceful degradation, and every empirical
question about whether anything arrived. The engine consumes bounded
authenticated records; it authenticates nothing, measures nothing, and performs
no institutional act.

## 10. Evidence and limits

The measurements behind this ruling were taken on 2026-08-18 against the current
source and the engine build that curated the recipient-side name. The rule shape,
each guard's necessity, the dormancy of every route, and the stability of the
stratification were executed rather than argued. Those results establish what the
rules do over supplied records and nothing about the world.

This ruling creates no predicate, rule, pin, fixture, chapter, defect resolution,
posture change, receipt, or public claim. Every one of those is a separate act
with its own gate.
