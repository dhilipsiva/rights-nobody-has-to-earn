# Book 1 Income Security and Social Insurance Contract

**Status: Specified contract card, session-drafted and author-approved
2026-09-05, including the choices in section 12.** This is not a new author ruling. It is the
constitutional contract card that the coverage map requires before a domain is
formalised, written under the already ratified economic settlement of
2026-08-07 — its plural-provision floor section, its work section, its
protected-private-sphere care-period sentence, its taxation-and-insurance
section, and its insolvency section — and under the taxonomy's second and fifth
structural refusals. It reopens none of them. Where it chooses between shapes
those rulings leave open, the choice is named in section 12 and stands only
once approved.

## 1. Decision

The unconditional material floor stays noncontributory and is untouched by
anything in this family. Above the floor, a person's portable above-floor
supplement for a named life event derives from exactly two supplied records
read together: a purpose-limited contribution record, and an independent
adjudicated finding that the named event occurred, made at the same scheme
scope by an adjudicator who is not the carrier. The contribution record is
readable by that rule and by nothing else. The supplement conclusion is
derived-only and is read by no floor, standing, franchise, liberty,
recognition, loss, or allocation rule. When the carrier fails, the supplement
derives through the public guarantee instead of disappearing with the
carrier's record. Every failure of evidence withholds the supplement and
touches nothing else; no failure of evidence, and no accusation short of an
individualised adjudicated finding, derives a loss.

## 2. What already exists, and is extended rather than restated

The economic family already formalises the person-held barriers on which this
card rests: the floor-contribution condition, the supplement worth-score, the
care-supplement/reward conflation, the credit-or-insurance decision without
process, the private-insurance floor gate, supplement non-portability,
contribution-record purpose reuse, earned-supplement insolvency loss, the
fresh-start and floor-essentials denials, and wage-and-pension failure loss.
Its benefit carry contract, its acceptance cases for zero contribution history
and for employer insolvency with an empty estate, and scenario FS-SCN-06 all
remain in force. Those rows say what may never happen. This card supplies the
positive interface those rows presuppose and did not build: the record, the
finding, the conclusion, and the guarantee route, each executable over supplied
records.

## 4. The named events

Illness, disability, unemployment, caregiving, workplace injury, ageing, and
survivorship — the seven the economic ruling lists — are the event constants.
Pensions are the ageing and survivorship events under this family, not a
separate relation. Adding an event is a constant, not a predicate, and needs
no vocabulary widening.

## 5. Formal migration contract

- **Add exactly one writable relation.** The contribution record uses the
  corpus name `pay` at arity four: payer, payment, recipient, purpose. The
  fourth place is the scheme and is the purpose limit. It is admitted, so it
  may be written, and the roster does not gate it. `contribute`, `pension`,
  `earn`, `benefit`, `guarantee`, and `entitle` are not corpus names and were
  not available; `deserve` exists and is refused here for the reason it was
  refused before — its places are wage and work, which is the contribution-as-
  merit reading this family exists to prevent.
- **Add exactly one derived relation.** The supplement conclusion uses the
  corpus name `insure` at arity three: insurer, insured, peril. It is declared
  derived-only; a direct write is refused at assert time with the standard
  message.
- **Reuse the existing role, attestation, and distinctness idiom.** The event
  finding is the existing two-place-and-scope attestation by a writer holding
  the existing authorised-role relation, with the existing distinctness
  conjunct between adjudicator and carrier. The insolvency finding uses the
  same idiom with the court as writer and the carrier as subject. Constants,
  not predicates, name the roles, the scopes, the events, and the guarantor.
- **Two rules.** The supplement rule reads record, finding, distinctness, and
  the negated fraud finding. The guarantee rule reads the same record and
  finding, adds the insolvency finding on the carrier, and concludes the
  supplement with the guarantor as insurer. No rule reads residence,
  jurisdiction, citizenship, documentation, or time.
- **One verifier guard, with its named defect.** Measured on 2026-09-05: the
  engine accepts a rule that conditions confinement on the absence of a
  contribution record, because the record is a base relation and the
  stratifier has no negative cycle to refuse. Nothing in the engine prevents
  work-conditioning of liberty by administration. The repository guard
  therefore holds that the contribution record may be read only by the two
  supplement rules, never under negation elsewhere, and never into a floor,
  standing, franchise, liberty, recognition, loss, or allocation head. Its
  watched failing control is a counterfactual fixture that adds exactly that
  hostile rule.
- **Refused forms, each named so it is not re-proposed one at a time:** a
  wage-replacement ratio, rate, reserve, or day count in the constitution; the
  contribution record as a floor, standing, franchise, or scarcity-priority
  key; carrier self-adjudication of the event; a household, family, or
  charity presumed to supply either the floor or the supplement; a global
  capability, risk, or worth score derived from contribution history; a
  reader of the supplement conclusion anywhere but the audit inventory; a
  retraction route, which derivation cannot express and which would be a
  clock in disguise.

## 6. Failure and edge cases, with their formal behaviour

| Case | Formal behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Zero contribution history | No record, so no supplement; every floor entitlement and standing conclusion is unchanged. This is the positive form of the floor-contribution-condition barrier and of scenario FS-SCN-06. |
| Missing or corrupt record | A malformed or unadmitted entry is refused at assert time; an entry at the wrong scheme derives nothing. Neither derives a loss or touches the floor. |
| Stale record | A record with no current finding withholds the supplement. Absence is a trigger for outreach and challenge, never a finding of non-entitlement. |
| Employer or carrier insolvency, estate empty | The guarantee rule derives the supplement with the guarantor as insurer from the same record and finding. The supplement itself survives, not merely its record. |
| Family or jurisdiction transfer | No rule reads residence or jurisdiction, so the supplement derives wherever the record and finding are supplied. This is the positive form of the non-portability barrier. |
| Lawful correction | A replacement record derives on its own terms. No penalty, repayment, or floor effect attaches to the correction. |
| Separately adjudicated fraud | An individualised adjudicated fraud finding about that person's record blocks that person's supplement and nothing else. An accusation, a pattern, or a carrier's own assertion blocks nothing. |
| Refusal of work | Not a state of this family at all; the record is about contributions made, not about willingness. |

## 7. What this does not establish

The supplement conclusion is a legal effect over supplied records. It does not
establish that any contribution was made, that any adjudicator exists or acted,
that any carrier is solvent or insolvent, that any amount is adequate, that any
payment reaches anyone, or that the guarantee is funded. Amount, rates,
reserves, take-up, actuarial adequacy, administration, and every empirical
question about arrival are Book 2's, and the arrival of a supplement to a
person is a liveness claim that may never be Derived, Checked, or Reasoned. No
sentence in Book 1 may say a supplement arrives.

## 8. Book 1, Book 2, and the engine

Book 1 owns the interface: which records exist, who may write each, what
independence the rule enforces, what follows from the pair, what a missing or
contested record triggers, and the failure polarity. Book 2 owns actuarial
assumptions, rates, reserves, take-up, scheme administration, employer-
insolvency operations, guarantee funding, and shock testing. The engine consumes
supplied records and derives a bounded conclusion; it authenticates nothing,
computes no amount, advances no clock, and performs no institutional act.

## 9. Assurance posture

The interface claim — that the supplement conclusion derives from the named
pair and from nothing else, and that the floor and recognition are unmoved by
every state of this family — is a safety claim, Derived through formal
entailment over supplied records, with an enumerated non-extension clause: it
does not extend to amount, adequacy, solvency, funding, arrival, or operation.
The operations claim is Unestablished and routed to Book 2. The two are
separate ledger rows because a row carrying both would be two claims.
