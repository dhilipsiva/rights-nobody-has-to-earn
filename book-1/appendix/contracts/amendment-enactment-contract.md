## Scope and authority

**2026-09-19 substantive supersession, under delegated author approval:** the
separate Article 9 proposal-label rules are removed. They duplicated amendment
status without candidate binding, let a targetless name receive a law label,
and used the personal credibility relation for proposal rejection. A simple
target requirement would leave the effect gap and personal-name collision;
separating the rejection relation alone would leave the unsupported law label.
The narrower resolution is to retain this exact-change interface as the
constitutional amendment route. No constitutional commitment requires the
weaker parallel mechanism. Its docket, tally and target examples remain as
refused shortcuts, with an explicit counterfactual reproducing both old failures.

Amendment thresholds, the democratic corridor and the ordinary-fact result
certificate interface are unchanged. Political consent and compatibility
remain separate requirements. Certification, publication and effective status
belong to the exact record and candidate. `become` remains derived-only and
unread, with no producing rule; neither it nor a proposal's declared target
supplies enactment authority or a personal credibility finding. The retained
`permanent` names identify protected commitments, without claiming to inspect
or prevent an edit to the source file.

The holders remain those in FSPOW_037. The source-binding authority, independent
effect reviewer, publisher and effective-version selector are bounded functions,
not newly sovereign institutions. Their authority must be supplied for the exact
record. The authoring fixture reuses the existing state-form result pipeline;
it does not assert its derived results as facts.
FSPOW_037's Assembly, referendum, Council and affected-region interfaces carry
the exact base/candidate identities as configuration terms, rejoined through
the roster/submission/result and certificate records. Downstream certification
cannot substitute a new candidate behind an unchanged proposal name.

## Required record and stages

The record identifies the proposal, political result and certificate, exact
base and candidate, transition, jurisdiction and legal scope, bounded effect
review, vocabulary disposition, current review/end evidence, responsible
operator, challenge reader, independent alternate, correction and remedy.
Independent source and effect attestations must agree. Their identities cannot
be the operator's or each other's. The challenge reader and alternate are
separate from those actors and from each other.

- Certification joins the actual current FSPOW_037 result and Assembly authority
  to the same proposal, result certificate, base version, jurisdiction and legal
  scope. Exact-byte binding, bounded semantic agreement and positive corridor
  compatibility are separately supplied. Neither consent nor a clean scan
  substitutes for effect review. No declared-target label supplies any of them.
- Publication needs the certified candidate and a separately authorised
  attestation that those exact candidate bytes were published. Certification
  alone imposes a publication duty; it proves no publication.
- Effective-version status additionally needs an authorised, independently
  reviewed selection for a named jurisdictional slot and selection generation,
  naming the predecessor and exact published candidate. The host must perform
  an atomic compare-and-select against its current predecessor. A publication
  alone is not selection, and a supplied selection fact is not deployment.
- Supersession ends the old selection's current status. It does not delete the
  old source, its findings, challenges or consequences. A rollback is a fresh
  transition with a fresh version occurrence and current exact-change authority;
  old bytes, signatures or consent cannot activate it by replay. This contract
  supplies no freestanding operator rollback power and does not automatically
  undo personal judgments or obligations.

These are identifiers over supplied records, not cryptographic primitives.
Nibli neither compares files nor authenticates identity, time, completeness,
publication, selection or deployment. Legal currentness is scoped to supplied
evidence; it does not observe elapsed time or discover a competing installation.

## Conflicts, failure, continuity and remedy

Conflicting single-valued fields from authorised attesters withhold this
record's permission. Conflicting supplied selections in the same slot and
generation withhold both, rather than arbitrarily choosing the first or last.
Two pending candidates are not inherently unlawful; at the host boundary only
one can succeed against the same effective predecessor. The other needs fresh
authority against the new base. Repeated transition or version-occurrence IDs
cannot be used to rewrite history, even if bytes happen to match.

Stale base, wrong proposal/certificate, missing consent, missing or incompatible
review, unauthorised vocabulary widening, wrong publication bytes, replay and
wrong-source queries confer no new permission. Failure to enact does not enact
the candidate and does not itself invalidate the existing effective source.
This is an explicit host state-preservation rule, not an inference from FALSE.
If the existing source is itself disputed, continuing operation needs its own
lawful continuity basis; this module does not invent emergency power.

Anyone can request independent review without first obtaining a valid
candidate certificate or the operator's consent. A current independently
reviewed source defect withdraws permission for the affected record and creates
review, preservation and correction duties. A positively certified failure to
respond after authenticated notice, real opportunity and the source-bound
deadline routes the duty to the independent alternate. Silence alone creates
neither approval nor guilt. An alternate reviews the same requirements; it
cannot waive consent, compatibility or source binding. Constitutional challenge
and final court review remain available; neither reviewer has a final veto.
These duties neither authenticate delivery nor make a reader act.

No rule in this family derives a personal verdict, loss, deprivation, score,
floor reduction, vote loss or end of permanent public answerability.

## External host boundary

The local executable model takes deliberately supplied, trusted evidence. It
compares complete UTF-8 source bytes directly, admits only ground evidence,
runs bounded checks in fresh Nibli sessions and refuses non-definitive results
or incomplete contradiction scans. It binds the checks and vocabulary review
to the exact candidate; a different source requires another review. The model
keeps immutable source occurrences and a single effective pointer in memory.
It verifies publication bytes before selection and checks source identity and
selection generation before every query. Rejected operations leave its state
unchanged. It writes no constitution, production registry or publication.

This is a single-process reference model. Its input file is a test/trust-root
input, not a signed certificate. A malicious caller controlling admitted
evidence can lie. Real authentication of digests/signatures, key custody,
independent review, durable atomic storage, cross-process conflict resolution,
public availability, deployment acknowledgement, clocks and crash recovery
remain Book 2 responsibilities. No hash, receipt, report, freshness or Git
history gate is added to routine verification.

Bounded effect checks must name their limits. Passing them does not prove
semantic completeness, entrench all future source changes, or authenticate
external truth. Source admission changes need explicit vocabulary review;
the local check compares the compiler's admission declarations, not a claim
that a new word is constitutionally permissible merely because it parses.
