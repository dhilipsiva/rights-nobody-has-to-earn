# Official statistics and planning information

Implementation contract for the author-directed TODO, under the equality
decision's sections 6, 8, 11 and 13 and the 2026-09-12 verification ruling.
This implements statistical authority and diagnostic evidence, not the rest
of the equality programme. It establishes no operating statistical agency.

## Authority and permitted uses

The supplied record can authorize a census, representative sample,
administrative statistics, public planning data or an equality diagnostic.
The purposes are official statistics, public planning and equality diagnosis.
Authorization concerns a named operator, dataset, purpose, source version,
method version, evidence period, jurisdiction, legal scope and end condition.
It never authorizes compulsory personal response or individual adjudication.

Necessity, minimization, accessible participation, privacy and security,
retention/deletion control, independent methodology, uncertainty, publication,
correction and challenge are positive prerequisites. The publication duty
covers methods, uncertainty, exclusions and known undercount. Disclosed
undercount is not automatically a disqualifying defect: independent review
must establish that a defect makes this particular use unsupported.

A source, an independent methodological evidence writer and an independent
reviewer must agree. Their supplied identities differ from one another and
from the operator. The challenge reader and alternate have distinct,
record-specific authorizations, are separate from the authorizing attesters,
and cannot be the operator. The final route
remains constitutional review or its independent substitute, not self-review.
These are conditions on supplied credentials, not authentication or proof of
independence in practice.

## Data separation and consumers

Statistical evidence is separate from the constitutional consequential person
record and from eligibility and enforcement records. The record must name
distinct containers and attest purpose-limited, non-reusable linkage. The
formal model contains opaque dataset and case handles, never person-level
diagnostic attributes, identity lists, scores or computed disparities.
Small groups and unusual intersections require explicit re-identification
protection; a safe aggregate release needs its own reviewed permission.

Every downstream use reads the current authorization and the same dataset,
purpose, method, source version and evidence period. Conflicting authorized
values block the record rather than letting a reader select whichever value
would work. A fresh authorization for one version cannot license another.
There is no consumer for individual standing, floor, liberty, political
weight, sanction, risk, entitlement, worth, guilt or punishment.

The existing person-held `EqualityDiagnosticRecordReuse`,
`DiagnosticIndividualVerdict` and `DiagnosticNonparticipationPenalty`
protections are retained, not replaced. They name legal protections, not a
compiler ban on every conceivable future reader. A development test watches
the actual statistical producers and consumers and rejects a deliberately
hostile person-consequence rule. No repository audit or freshness gate is
added to ordinary verification.

## Diagnostic evidence, burdens and challenge

A credible, independently reviewed aggregate or intersectional pattern tied
to a protected-ground barrier can establish a rebuttable presumption in a
named civil, administrative or constitutional remedial equality proceeding.
The legal scope identifies the challenged measure and aggregate evidence
basis, not a reusable classification of a person.
It can open independent systemic audit and require the respondent to disclose
the measure's basis and answer the applicable justification test. The reader
owes consideration of rebuttal and correction. This is not a finding of
individual discrimination and never shifts a criminal burden.

A request opens a duty to review access, correction, deletion or challenge
without requiring the challenged statistical authorization to remain valid.
Actual access to private evidence additionally requires a separately reviewed,
case-bound entitlement to that evidence. This entitlement authorizes access
to that record only; it is not a general identity or programme-eligibility
finding. No raw data or private identity is published by either route.

Positive, independently reviewed nonresponse after notice, a real opportunity
to act and a source-bound deadline creates the independent alternate's duty
directly. Silence alone establishes neither response, misconduct nor an
adverse fact about a participant. Nonparticipation and a challenge cannot
reduce anyone's constitutional rights. Anti-retaliation duties protect the
requester, supporters, witnesses and investigators.

## Defects, correction and time

A current reviewed defect can withhold the affected use and require
independent correction and privacy-safe publication of the correction.
The defect kinds cover unsupported undercount, classification harm, political
manipulation, stale data, method drift, suppression, identity reuse,
individual-consequence reuse, re-identification, blocked access or correction,
retaliation and unlawful retention. A separately reviewed rebuttal can
displace a diagnostic presumption. Neither route adjudicates an individual.

The `contradict(record, StatisticsUseAuthorization)` incompatibility marker is
a named legal conclusion, not a
`:contradictions` scanner finding. Its target is the exact use/finding record,
so an unrelated use survives. A missing or withheld finding creates no defect.
Request and alternate-review duties do not disappear with use permission.

Currentness is a supplied source-bound certificate, not an observed clock.
Expiry, correction and replacement require a fresh, reconciled snapshot;
adding a new certificate does not silently erase the old one. A conflicting
current record fails closed. No field proves that data was deleted, a result
was published, a request was answered or a remedy arrived.

## Classification and Book 2 handoff

Statistical authorization and oversight are Class 6 public functions;
privacy, access, non-use and non-retaliation protect Classes 1 and 3 standing
and liberty; the remedial burden and rebuttal are Class 4 fair process;
diagnostic evidence and record challenge are Class 7 records and assurance.
Floors and recognition remain non-consumers. Planning
information does not itself allocate Class 8 territorial power or resources.
No new taxonomy class or omnibus equality predicate is introduced.

Book 2's democratic/integrity/statistics operations item owns collection,
sampling, linkage, privacy technology, calibration, thresholds, institutional
capacity, authentication, publication, deletion and empirical evaluation.
It remains inactive until Gate C. Nibli consumes bounded supplied findings;
it computes no population disparity, authenticates no identity and proves no
institutional action. The chapter 1 reader section received separate
exact-version author approval on 2026-09-12.

## Executable cases and reader review

`statistics-source.json` is the semantic input to `./generate.sh statistics`.
It writes the `OFFICIAL-STATISTICS-RULES` block and ordinary isolated cases
under `tests/pins/statistics/`. The verifier loads the actual constitution;
only declared counterfactuals remove a review, purpose, record-separation or
remedial-proceeding restriction. The paired positive controls show what each
restriction prevents. No hash, receipt or report gate is involved.

Every record kind has positive, withheld, missing-field, unauthorized-writer,
self-review, alternate-fusion, stale, mismatched and conflicting-value cases.
Every dataset, purpose, pattern, proceeding, request and defect kind is
exercised. Stateful cases check an affected use stopping while personal
standing, floors, ballot, speech and permanent answerability survive; a
different use survives, and a finding for a different method, source or period
cannot stop the current one. A consumer loses permission when its upstream
authorization is withdrawn. Rebuttal is limited to an aggregate presumption,
not general statistics or a finding of individual liability.

The existing chapter 1 data boundary, chapter 8 equality paragraph, opening
note, Part V and method remain accurate at their stated scope. The new rules
do not enact a positive measure, operate a diagnostic system, prove a
population pattern, or establish delivery. The old method's description of
the separate person-held barrier family is retained; the statistical-use
family is not that barrier family and does not borrow the custody clock.
The additional chapter 1 section "Counting without ranking people" was
separately approved on 2026-09-12 and inserted unchanged before "Facts and
conclusions are kept apart". Its supply is `session-drafted, author-approved`;
`statistics-reader-draft.md` preserves the approved wording, and the chapter's
paired pins map its supporting cases.
