**Continuing duties — 2026-09-19 supersession.** Protective duties attach to
the exact recorded undertaking independently of its surviving permission or
the current authority of a parent. Withdrawing a declaration cannot erase the
operator's inventory, accounting, return or compensation duty for its
requisition. Withdrawing a defence structure cannot erase Assembly ceilings,
appropriation and audit. The same separation applies to the instruments'
other care, notice, cessation and accountability duties.

The duty producer rejoins the instrument's positive fields, named executor and
independent attesters, without consuming current completion or the absence of
a withdrawal or conflicting later entry. It creates neither a permission nor
evidence that an act occurred or a duty was performed. Restrictive effects
continue to require the full current authority, dependency and temporal joins.
Missing all evidence remains an input limitation, not a constitutional waiver.

## Common power contract

Every record names the democratic legal source, exact mandate, holder, affected
subject or object, case, jurisdiction, legal scope, source version and epoch,
start/window/end, evidence, independent authorizer and reviewer, execution
actor, public reasons, private evidence boundary, notice, challenge, appeal,
correction, alternate, audit, continuity and remedy. Currentness is a positive,
source-bound externally supplied finding. Conflicting authorized identity or
status observations withhold the affected legal conclusion; uncredentialed
noise supplies neither authority nor a veto.

Policing, prosecution, adjudication, custodial execution, external defence and
security intelligence are distinct Class 6 mandates. They have separated
holders/functions, civilian command, independent conflict/recusal review,
non-delegable limits and distinct expiry. Appointment is not evidence of an
individual arrest, deployment, search or surveillance authorization.

No raw certificate or compact completion tag may replace the complete current
upstream contract. Each declaration, renewal, measure and alternate binds its
own supplied temporal window and exact parent version; custody T3 is not
borrowed. Missing currentness withholds authority, never writes cessation.
An identical frozen record can still produce the identical result on replay.

## Structural and operational boundary

instrument, with the existing custody refusals re-measured. The family-status
and parent-absence attacks need their own discriminating controls. None of this
creates family facts or modifies the conviction rule by an unrelated conjunct.

The instrument may have readers for movement, safeguards and review. The
capability it restricts must remain a no-reader endpoint. Formal non-cascade
does not prevent practical harms: continuity, compensation, preservation,
release, correction and remedy remain duties with separately evidenced acts.

Book 2 owns operations, staffing, funding, training, equipment, actual evidence
authentication, clocks, publication, delivery and institutional liveness.
Foreign cooperation, recognition and readmission remain named external
assumptions, not guaranteed Book 2 deliverables. No hashes, receipts or report
gates are introduced into routine verification.
