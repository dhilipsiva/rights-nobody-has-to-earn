# The ordinary half of life course, family, care and reproduction

Implementation contract for the last domain the reader ledger showed only under
strain, under the 2026-08-08 family/dependency/reproduction ruling and the
2026-09-12 verification ruling. That baseline landed 106 person-held barriers
and no interface underneath them: the design could say a great deal about what
may not be done to a child, a dependant or a person who needs help deciding, and
nothing about the ordinary case. This supplies the ordinary case.

It establishes no service, no capacity finding, no clinical result, no family
status in the world, no institution and no delivery. It supplies no age.

## It sits beside the barriers, not on top of them

The barriers live in the `FAMILY-LIFE-COURSE` block and are hand-written. This
family writes a separate `FAMILY-LIFE-ORDINARY` block and touches neither the
barriers nor Article 4's legacy absence-based `parent`, `married` and `sibling`
independence checks, which the ruling still lists for replacement.

**That separation was learned the hard way and is recorded rather than
smoothed.** The generator was first given the barriers' own block markers, and
generating silently replaced all 106 of them. Nothing in the pin suite noticed:
the constitution still loaded, the new family's cases passed, and the deletion
surfaced only because the spine's predicate count fell from 95 to 92 — `home`,
`married` and `sibling` had lost their last reader. A family name that collides
with an existing block deletes that block's contents. The same hazard exists for
a family name colliding with an existing `tests/pins/<dir>`.

## What the eleven contracts are

**Role.** Parentage, caregiving, household, material support, property and
succession, and decision agent are a closed vocabulary and stay separable: one
does not carry the others. Each completion is a scoped, challengeable fiduciary
power over a named decision, never ownership; no marriage or household label
confers it by itself; every dependant and caregiver keeps separate standing,
voice, privacy, help and exit; and kinship alone creates no compelled personal
care duty and no inherited support debt. It is the authorization the dependent
records rejoin.

**Participation.** The affected person is heard, with no minimum speaking age,
their own account recorded without certifying a preference or a relationship,
independent assistance where the adults' interests conflict, and
decision-specific increasing authority rather than a global capability score. It
rejoins the exact role — decision, domain, actor, version, period, jurisdiction,
legal scope, end and role kind — so a role reviewed for one decision domain
licenses nothing in another.

**Support** is chosen, changeable and refusable, forbids a substituted decision
while support can still be given, refuses plenary guardianship, status-based
incapacity and any global capability score, and requires accessible formats and
time. Its conclusion is the supporter's authority to assist, and its own case
pins that this is not a role over the person and not the interpretation route.

**Initiation (added 2026-09-18).** The ordinary half's answer to the person
nobody has come for. Article 1c owes the initiation of assistance and
representation to every person, without a request, reading personhood and
nothing else; this contract is what discharging it must contain. A trigger from
a closed vocabulary — nobody is recorded as acting for the person, the only
recorded supporter is the accused party, the person cannot be reached in an
accessible language or form, the person is held or placed and unrepresented, an
earlier appointment ended without a successor — so the appointment is not a
general power over somebody's life. A representative appointed for the named
matter only and never as a status or guardianship, distinct from the acting
body, the source, the evidence attester, the reviewer, the challenge reader, the
alternate, and the person themselves. A named receipt witness, which is the
constitutional answer to the delivery family's open question: the acting body is
**obliged to authorise** the named witness rather than the record concluding the
authorisation, because concluding `authorized` closes a negative cycle through
`contradict` and the engine refused it in those words. No request from the
affected person and no capability finding. The person's own voice retained and
never replaced. Minimum necessary information, no reusable classification, no
enrolment. Care continuity and the floor unaffected by any gap in
representation. A source-bound end that does not renew in silence.

**Interpretation** is the bounded best-interpretation route, and it is gated on
positive evidence that support was actually provided first, together with will
and preferences remaining unascertainable after it. It is conflict-free,
decision-specific, least-restrictive, time-bounded, independently reviewed
before and during, and it ends when preferences become ascertainable. Remove the
support-first attestation and the route does not derive; that is its own case.

**Continuity** is the one the barriers most obviously presupposed. It is
immediate and non-delegable, it is not conditioned on a relationship, a
supporter or a record entry, and no family, household, association or charity is
presumed to supply the floor. Its case pins the structural claim: the record
completes with **no family role in it at all**, and confers no role over anybody.

**Reproductive and bodily care** requires no third party's authorization, holds
that current informed choice controls including refusal, forbids a route from
that choice to a punishment, and concludes a duty that no bodily decision is
recorded as an adverse fact about the person.

**Directive** fixes the treatment order in advance: current choice, then a valid
advance directive on its own terms, then a chosen representative implementing
known will and preferences, and only then the bounded route. Age, diagnosis and
residence setting remove the choice from nobody.

**Origin** keeps birth, adoption, donor and gestational-origin records accurate
and gives access and correction to the person the record is about. It hands back
no automatic contact or relationship right and erases no lawful history; its
case pins that the acting body does not get the access permission.

**Defect** withdraws exactly the record it names, through the same contradiction
the other families use, and lands review, correction and publication duties.
**Nonresponse** moves the review duty to an independent alternate on a positive
certified finding, with no silence-as-approval and no care suspension.

## Polarity, separation and the corridor

Every conclusion is positive; a withheld record concludes nothing and a missing
field concludes nothing, which the per-contract `withheld` and per-scope
omission cases pin. Three mutually distinct attesters — source, independent
evidence, independent review — must agree and none may be the acting body; a
challenge reader and an independent alternate carry their own record-bound
authorizations and are distinct from each other, from the attesters and from the
actor. Two attesters who disagree about a single-valued scope raise a record
ambiguity and nothing completes. Every head is `derived_only` upstream, so a
forged entry is refused at assertion. These are conditions on supplied
credentials, not proof of independence in practice.

The family introduces **no new relation name**: predicates stay at 95, derived
predicates at 51, strata at 9 and the floor at its eight rights; only the rule
count moves. Nothing concludes about a person. The sequence case pins it: a role
and a participation both standing, a defect against the role stopping both, and
the person named throughout keeping personhood, the floor debt and the ballot
while `false`, `prisoner`, `lose` and `mature` stay FALSE.

## It ships dormant

The shipped cast carries no record of this family. Every completion in the case
inventory comes from a fixture, so nothing in the supplied record derives a role,
a support arrangement, a continuity or an origin-record access for anybody named
in the book. The interface exists; the record does not exercise it.

## What it does not do

It decides no best interests, no capacity, no consent, no pregnancy, no
parentage, no death and no collective identity. It supplies no age, and nothing
in it reads `mature`, unary `family`, `home`, `work(Care)`, `healthy`, `free`,
`public` or `owe`. Book 2 owns service capacity, family and care proceedings,
clinical and death-record operations, land and title administration, language
services and consultation. No record here proves that a child was heard, that a
supporter arrived, that care continued, that a correction was published or that
anything at all was carried out.
