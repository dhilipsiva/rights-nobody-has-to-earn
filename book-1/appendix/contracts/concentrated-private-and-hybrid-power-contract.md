# Book 1 Concentrated Private and Hybrid Power Contract

**Status: Specified contract card, session-drafted and author-approved
2026-09-06, including the choices in section 11.** This is not a new author
ruling. It is the contract card for the named vocabularies of the public-scale
private-power finding under the ratified economic settlement of 2026-08-07 — its
protected-private-sphere section's public-like-duties sentence, its federal
competence section's cross-regional sentence, and the taxonomy's statement that
the equality ruling's named private domains bind without a dominance finding.
It reopens none of them. Where it chooses between shapes those rulings leave
open, the choice is named in section 11 and stands only once approved.

## 1. Decision

The public-scale finding names its own legal categories in the constitution
rather than accepting whatever token three attesters agree to repeat. The
trigger kind, the function class, and the tier allocation each become a closed
vocabulary of constitutional constants, and the finding derives only when the
token the attesters agree on is a member of the vocabulary its scope names.
Membership is a conclusion, never a supplied record: a record cannot write its
own ninth trigger kind. Nothing else about the finding changes — the same three
independent attesters, the same temporal contract, the same expiry, the same
contradiction bindings, the same seven calibrated duties reading the same
conclusion. What changes is that the constitution can now tell a network-effect
finding from a revenue-threshold one, a delegated public function from a
private one, and a cross-regional gatekeeper from a single-region one.

## 2. What already exists, and is extended rather than restated

The finding, its duties, and its walls are landed and executable. FS-POW-064
carries the finding under its own temporal contract with three attesters, an
expiry consequence, and sixty-one contradiction bindings, nine of them
specific to this card, that fail closed on disagreement. Seven duties — fair access, continuity, reasons and transparency,
portability and interoperability, independent audit, challenge and correction,
and effective remedy — derive from that conclusion, each with a positive pin and
negatives for a missing power and a conflicting selection, fair access
additionally carrying the mismatched-duty and mismatched-standard pair. FS-POW-065 through FS-POW-071 carry the access mandate, public option,
structural separation, breakup, receivership, licence withdrawal, and public
acquisition, each with a transition duty protecting workers, users, and floor
recipients, and savers where they are actually at risk. The barrier effects
already refuse an actor-wide finding, a label as proof, an intimate-dependency
presumption, duty-scope spillover, remedy discontinuity, a wrong-tier finding,
beneficial-control concealment, confidentiality as oversight evasion, civic-
association relabeling, and economic function fusion. The acceptance matrix
already executes seven named actors with and without the trigger, an
equality-bound actor without it, an expired selection, and a regional
misallocation. The obligations family already derives, from a delegated bearer
mode, both the bearer's duty and the principal's retained duty, so delegation
never moves the duty.

What did not exist was any constitutional content for the three tokens those
walls talk about.

A finding whose only stated ground is annual revenue derives the full result and
every duty hanging off it. The rule requires a trigger to be present, requires
the three attesters to name the same one, and requires each of them to attest
`AllowedPublicScaleTriggerEstablished` and

the ratified trigger kinds is a constitutional constant;
`FunctionIsPublicFacingDelegatedEssentialGatekeepingOrSystemic` bundles five
function classes into one token; and `RegionalOrCommonTierMatchesActualReach` is
the only tier content in the rule, which binds no tier and no reach value at all.

This is the house shape rather than an anomaly: the compensation family's
instrument kinds are free variables too, and their discipline is matching across
independent attesters rather than membership in a closed set. The reason to
depart from it here is that the eight trigger kinds, the five function classes,
and the tier allocation are legal categories written in the ratified text, not
empirical facts about the world. Naming them asks the engine to know nothing it
does not already know — the same move as the floor's named debts and the
placement destinations.

The trigger list is the economic decision's own list. Gatekeeping appears in
both the trigger and the function lists because the ruling puts it in both; the

than a tier and a reach separately, so the unlawful combinations are refused by
absence rather than by a pairing rule that a fourth observer could satisfy.

## 6. Formal migration contract

- **Add exactly one derived relation.** `member` at arity two, corpus `cmima`,
  places member and set. It is `derived_only` and is not admitted: measured, a
  direct assertion is refused with "declared derived-only". `qualify`,
  `classify`, `belong`, and `category` are not corpus names.
- **Add one vocabulary rule per member.** Each reads an observation of its own
  constant at its own scope and concludes that constant's membership. The head
  is ground, so the vocabulary can never name a token no rule mentions.
- **Add three gate conjuncts** to the FS-POW-064 reviewed-result rule, one per
  vocabulary, each binding the same variable the three attesters already agree
  on. One conjunct in one rule reaches both conclusions and all seven duties,
  because the holder-authority rule reads the reviewed result.
- **Replace two bundled slogans with bound variables.** The function class and
  the tier allocation become free variables at their existing scopes; the
  trigger already is one. The slogans stay as additional required attestations.
- **One verifier guard.** The assertion surface reconciles relations, not heads,
  so a ninth vocabulary rule would pass it silently. A producer-set check names
  the permitted heads, with a hostile added-member fixture as its watched
  failing control.
- **Refused forms:** `admits("member")`, which would let a supplied record write
  its own trigger kind; any weight, tier, score, or ordering over the
  vocabularies; any membership conclusion naming a person; a vocabulary read by
  any rule other than the finding; gating the holder-authority conclusion, which
  names who holds the power rather than whether a finding is sound.

## 8. What this does not establish

No membership conclusion proves that the named ground actually holds of any
actor. The engine still cannot tell whether a network effect exists, whether an
exit is meaningful, whether a function is essential in fact, or whether a
gatekeeper's reach really crosses a region. It can now tell which legal test the
record claims to have applied, and it refuses a record that claims none of them.
Whether any body makes a finding, acts on one, or enforces a duty remains a
liveness claim that may never be Derived.

## 9. Assurance posture

The interface claim is a safety claim, Derived through formal entailment over
supplied records, with an enumerated non-extension clause: existence of the
ground, truth of the attestation, reach in fact, operation, and enforcement. The
operations claim is Unestablished and routed to Book 2. They are separate ledger
rows, and neither upgrades the posture of FS-POW-064 or of any duty.

They are superseded by the family's pins, which now carry the same results

same premises with every token named. The spine's stratification moved when the
vocabulary relation joined it, and was refreshed before the freeze rather than
discovered in the receipt.

## 11. Choices made under the ratified text, for approval

1. `member` at arity two as the vocabulary relation, conclusion-only.
2. The gate sits on the reviewed-result rule only, and that is sufficient
   rather than partial: the holder-authority rule reads the reviewed result, so
   measured, both conclusions withhold together on an unnamed token. The
   current-selection conclusion feeding the reviewed result is untouched — it
   says the temporal selection is current, never that the grounds are named.
3. Two tier allocations only, so a common-tier finding over a single-region
   gatekeeper is refused alongside a regional finding over a cross-regional one.
4. The existing slogans are kept as additional required attestations rather than
   replaced, so no landed pin loses its premise.
5. Prose in the chapter on what a person is owed and the chapter on when the
   system notices it broke, one Part V sentence, one method paragraph, and
   glossary entries for the finding's named grounds.
