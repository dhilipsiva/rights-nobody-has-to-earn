# Substantive Equality — the Ordinary Half

> **Status: implemented 2026-09-17 under the ratified 2026-08-07 baseline; no
> new author ruling was taken.** The baseline
> ([`../decisions/substantive-equality-and-anti-subordination-decision.md`](../decisions/substantive-equality-and-anti-subordination-decision.md))
> landed fifty-four person-held barriers and no interface underneath them — the
> shape the life-course baseline had before its ordinary half landed on
> 2026-09-16. This card owns the interface. `book-1/source/equality-source.json`
> and `./generate.sh equality` produce its rules and cases in the block
> `SUBSTANTIVE-EQUALITY-ORDINARY`, a new block name beside the barriers' own;
> reusing an existing block name silently deletes it.

## 1. What the family carries

Six records, each completing only with three mutually distinct attesters (a
source, an independent evidence authority, an independent review authority), a
challenge reader and an independent alternate, none of them the acting body or
the person the record is about, and no authorized conflicting value on any
single-valued scope:

- **A reviewed positive measure** (`ReviewedPositiveMeasure`): its kind from a
  closed vocabulary — barrier removal, targeted outreach, investment, support,
  restitution, training, a placement target, a bounded quota — with the
  evidenced continuing disadvantage, the insufficiency of ordinary equality,
  accessibility and accommodation, an eligible class with minimally necessary
  contestable evidence kept outside the person record, the expected effect on
  equality and on other rights, the political-measure limits (one equal ballot,
  proportionality, the anti-capture controls), and an end that is the
  objective's sustainable achievement and never a calendar. It concludes the
  actor's authority for that measure kind, permits the actor to apply it, and
  obliges the reader to review it and end it when the objective is met.
- **A continuation finding** (`ReviewedMeasureContinuationFinding`), which
  rejoins the exact reviewed measure — by decision, domain, actor, version,
  period, jurisdiction, scope, end and measure kind — and completes only with
  current, independently reviewable evidence that the objective remains unmet,
  the measure still necessary and proportionate, no new preference or enlarged
  scope, and review silence certifying nothing. A superseded measure record
  continues nothing.
- **A reasonable accommodation** (`ReviewedReasonableAccommodation`): the
  barrier kind from a closed vocabulary — physical, sensory, cognitive,
  linguistic, digital, geographic, financial, procedural — the person it is
  for, consultation and their stated requirements, an effective adjustment
  with reasons and a prompt challenge route, no cost and no inferior status,
  the undue-burden test read across the institution as a whole, and an
  equivalent alternative through public continuity where the provider genuinely
  cannot. It obliges the actor to provide the adjustment or secure the
  alternative, permits the person to use the right with it, and obliges the
  alternate to secure the alternative route without restart.
- **A remedial proceeding** (`ReviewedRemedialEqualityProceeding`): the form of
  discrimination from a closed vocabulary, the claimant, purpose-or-effect with
  no intent or single comparator required, an aggregate pattern creating only a
  rebuttable presumption and an audit, no criminal burden reversal, guilt or
  punishment, individual relief and related-case re-audit kept separate, and no
  remedy that takes another person's standing, floor, liberty, process or
  ballot. It permits the claimant to pursue the proceeding and obliges the
  reader to hear it and give case-specific relief.
- **A record defect** and **a certified nonresponse**, in the shape every
  landed family uses: positive independent evidence of the exact defect,
  withdrawal of only the affected reliance, no adverse fact about the subject;
  and a deadline positively found to have passed, moving the duty to the
  alternate and escalating to the court.

The recursive interface instantiated is right → duty → accessible delivery →
breach → continuity → remedy → review, and it stops at the review duty: no
record here proves that an adjustment was provided, a measure achieved its
objective, a pattern was real, or a claim was heard.

## 2. What the cases establish

Three hundred and seventy-seven executable cases. Per record: the positive
completion; the withheld record; each field omitted in turn; each attester
unauthorized in turn; every vocabulary member and one unapproved kind; a stale
disposition; self-review, actor-review, a fused alternate, the source as
challenge reader and the reviewer as alternate; a mismatched source version and
a period drift, each also as an authorized conflict; and for the continuation,
the missing authorization and the wrong decision, domain, version, period and
measure kind. Then the sequences:
`measure/defect-and-rights-sequence` (a measure and its continuation stop
together on a defect, and Cira keeps personhood, the floor debt, liberty and
credibility throughout), `measure/unrelated-defect`,
`measure/does-not-travel-between-domains`,
`measure/expired-continuation-stops`, `accommodation/confers-no-status` (an
accommodation completes for a person the roster never entered and hands back no
status, verdict or classification), `proceeding/presumption-from-pattern` (a
pattern opens a proceeding and convicts nobody),
`equality/request-and-certified-nonresponse`, and
`equality/forged-conclusions`. Four counterfactual bases strip the independence
conjunct, the domain vocabulary, the measure vocabulary and the requester
separation, so each removed conjunct is shown to have been load-bearing.

## 3. What this does not establish

No barrier, disadvantage, need, pattern, adjustment, measure, hearing or remedy
exists or operates because a record here completes. The family authenticates
nothing, measures nothing, decides no undue burden in the world, and proves no
arrival. Book 2 owns collection and statistical methods, accommodation
operations, monitoring, enforcement workflows and empirical evaluation. The
diagnostics half of the equality baseline belongs to the official-statistics
family; the barriers stay in `SUBSTANTIVE-EQUALITY`.

## 4. Where it is rendered

The chapter *The Same Route for Everyone*, whose sections "A measure with an
end" and "Accessibility is not a ninth floor item" narrate a measure ending and
an accommodation owed on these cases and on nothing else.
