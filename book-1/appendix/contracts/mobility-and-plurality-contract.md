# Mobility, differentiated collective rights, and external no-evasion

Implementation contract for the completed mobility/plurality item. The
controlling policy is the
family/dependency/plurality decision, sections 12–18; the public-safety and
external-power decision, sections 11–13, 17 and 19; and the residence-first
state-form decision. This is a constitutional interface, not an operating
border, membership registry, land office, consultation service or court.

## Work and scope

The bounded authoring slices are: individual mobility protections and process;
membership, differentiated capacities, title and internal government; exact
consent/consultation gates and external no-evasion; then failure, correction,
continuity, tests and reader projection. Each slice uses the actual constitution
for positive, withheld, mismatched, conflict and counterfactual tests. The exact
Book 1 additions were displayed, approved under the author's 2026-09-13
delegated approval, and inserted in chapters 8 and 9.

This item implements limits on removal and evacuation, not a power to detain,
deport, compel movement, deploy force, ratify a treaty or enact an emergency.
Those instruments and their physical-custody protections remain with the
separate public-safety item. A compatibility conclusion below is expressly not
an executable order. The existing `travel`, custody, standing, adulthood,
general-government franchise and negotiated-secession rules are retained.
No new predicate, taxonomy class, collective personhood or population score is
introduced. The explicit `mobility` authoring command owns the new rule block
and ordinary cases; routine verification gains only pins and contradictions.

## Common consequential-record contract

Each record names its holder, object, lawful source and version, jurisdiction,
legal scope, evidence record, current window and source-bound end, operator,
independent reviewer, audit reader, challenge reader, independent alternate,
challenge, correction and remedy. Source, evidence and review writers must have
record-specific authority, agree on every field and be distinct from each other
and the operator. Review, challenge, alternate and audit readers cannot be the
interested holder or the record's named representative or access requester.
They are independently authorized and separated from the deciding actors. An
affirmative conflict check is required; absent kinship or absent misconduct
is not independence evidence.

Permitted effects use only a current, reconciled, source-bound finding. Every
field has an authorized-conflict check: neither a second writer nor a second
value lets a reader select a convenient tuple. A downstream record must consume
the actual completed prerequisite and rejoin its holder, object, source version,
jurisdiction, scope and window. A convenient token naming a prerequisite is not
its completion. This includes the title and representative process underlying
consultation and consent and the project that uses them.

These are externally supplied, authenticated-in-operation premises; Nibli does
not authenticate them. Raw private membership, ancestry, religion, risk and
enforcement records remain outside the formal record. Only opaque, case-bound
handles and minimally necessary legal findings enter. No identity list, tally,
worth/risk classification, personal sanction or reusable eligibility score is
produced. Actual access to private evidence requires its own reviewed,
purpose-limited entitlement; asking for review does not publish it.

## Mobility and the public duty

Existing encounter and birth routes continue to establish standing inside the
supplied model. Public duties and person-held protections prohibit withholding
help for missing documentation, nationality, immigration classification or
manner of arrival. No record can notice a person never supplied to it. Outside
the model the duty is serve first, reconcile afterwards, not find a missing row.
Enrolment, collection and transmission for enforcement through floor services,
care, education, courts and crime reporting are separately prohibited. Scarcity
must include everyone within jurisdiction or effective control; arrivals cannot
be excluded from its population or ranked by status.

Asylum carries accessible information, advocate, interpreter, hearing, reasons
and suspensive appeal. Removal/transfer compatibility needs an individualized
judicial decision, meaningful suspensive appeal, family/child/residence review,
and positive review of the full onward-transfer chain and categorical bars.
Non-refoulement is absolute; silence or diplomatic assurance cannot replace
that review. Collective expulsion, child immigration detention and nationality
deprivation as punishment or into statelessness remain prohibited. The birth-
and-otherwise-stateless finding requires recognition of nationality, never a
new citizenship condition on standing or the floor. Adult detention retains
its separate necessity, court, maximum, review and real-alternative limits;
no detention authority is supplied here. The existing former-resident return
right is preserved and creates no nonresident ballot.

## Membership, autonomy, title and consent

Self-identification and lawful collective acceptance must both be supplied;
neither the state nor a convenient spokesperson can create the relation alone.
Multiple memberships coexist. A person's recorded free exit ends only the
named membership occurrence, without an official veto, falsifying origin or
extinguishing a distinct title. A later re-entry needs a fresh occurrence and
both sides' evidence. Procedural disputes leave individual rights and services
intact and create an independent review duty, not public identity disclosure.

Indigenous and minority capacities are differentiated. Minority culture alone
does not authorize territory. Internal selection and customary law require
lawful scope, voluntary practice, rights of children and nonmembers, individual
voice, dissent, confidential help, reasons, appeal and final constitutional
review. Nonmember residents keep common services, property/housing protection
and their ordinary residence-first general-government ballot.

Permanent relocation, title extinguishment, irreversible title impairment,
sovereignty transfer over collective lands, sacred-site destruction,
hazardous-material placement and comparable existential harm each require
free, prior, informed, actual collective consent. Title and consent bind the
same collective, land, project and project version. The representative must
derive authority from the collective's lawful process, not state appointment.
External administration, independent completeness assurance, result service
and institutional execution stay separate. The certificate names its roster,
classification, lawful decision rule, completeness and result: missing, empty,
tied, conflicting, coerced or withheld participation never counts as consent.
No fixed witness pattern is called a generic majority. A predeclared independent
alternate completeness assurer may supply the same record-specific role under
a fresh reviewed configuration; no primary absence or uncredentialed statement
substitutes for that attestation. Completeness concerns the classified record
under the lawful collective decision rule, not a new universal unanimity rule.

Other material effects require good-faith consultation before commitment,
accessible information, adequate time, affected-member participation,
alternatives, accommodation, mitigation, public reasons, burden review and
independent challenge. This does not create a blanket veto. An existential
effect cannot be relabelled consultation-only. Temporary lifesaving evacuation
needs the separate emergency declaration and measure contract, independent
review, safe continuity, return, repair and preserved title; this family checks
only compatibility with collective rights and authorizes no forced movement.
Consent never waives individual bodily rights, a commons ceiling or direct
animal protection. Conservation never excuses dispossession or assimilation.

## Failure, continuity, remedy and time

A positive current independently reviewed defect withholds only the exact
affected record's permission and requires preservation, correction and remedy.
It does not establish a personal wrongdoer. A challenge requires neither an
existing permission nor the operator's approval. Reader nonresponse requires
positive evidence of notice, a real opportunity and a source-bound elapsed
deadline; then the separately authorized alternate owes action. Silence alone
never supplies guilt, consent, passage or renewal. Constitutional review remains
available; a conflicted or unavailable reader is not the final authority.

`contradict(target, MPEffectPermission)` is this scoped legal incompatibility
marker, not a `:contradictions` scanner finding. A missing finding establishes
neither misconduct nor an affirmative finding that an arrangement is lawful.

Individual and collective remedy records allow cessation, restitution,
restoration, compensation/reparation and bounded non-repetition duties while
protecting every affected person's rights and valid collective title. A duty,
permission or completed finding proves no delivery, action or completed remedy.
Independent public continuity duties remain when a finding is missing or ends.

Window/currentness evidence is supplied, never calculated. Ended, superseded,
disputed or conflicting records cannot supply current authority. Fresh renewed
or corrected records need new source-bound review; they cannot silently erase
history, carry old consent to a changed project, or revive an exited membership.
Restoration and record preservation are positive duties, not an inference from
`FALSE`. The host must supply reconciled snapshots and authentic event evidence;
this family does not defeat an externally replayed frozen snapshot or advance
a clock. No custody-T3 lease is borrowed as a general temporal permission.

## Validation — 2026-09-13

The completed whole-book run passed 7,930 pins across 1,579 cases, with complete
contradiction scans and no findings, in 531.67 seconds (8m52s) using four
workers. The nine pre-existing known-defect pins still reproduce. The new
family contributes 361 cases; no pre-existing case, expectation, suite setting
or verifier/dependency file was changed. The performance target remains unmet.

All six mobility development tests pass in release mode, including repeatable
isolated generation, exact dependency joins, actual-source consumer checks,
scoped-versus-permanent authority and interested-party self-review. Existing
authoring/runner development checks also pass; manual benchmarks remain ignored.
The focused forgery file passes 118 pins, and membership, consent and private-
access independence controls pass against the actual constitution.

During development an interested member could review their own record despite
being separate from the operator. A failing regression reproduced that defect
before the rules were fixed; review/challenge/alternate/audit roles now exclude
the interested holder and the record's named representative or requester.
Earlier malformed refusal directives were corrected without changing their
substantive expectations. Neither issue was an engine bug. The final full run
above includes both fixes and the added independent public-duty checks.
