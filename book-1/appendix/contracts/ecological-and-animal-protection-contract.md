# Environmental rights, commons and direct animal protection

Status: **Implemented and verified against the repository sources, 2026-09-14;
not an operational or empirical claim.**
Session-drafted choices and exact retained reader text have delegated author
approval under the 2026-09-13 instruction. The controlling policy remains the
[2026-08-08 decision](../decisions/ecological-future-generation-commons-and-non-human-animal-decision.md).
This contract creates no measurement, authenticated finding, institution in

## Holders, scope and vocabulary disposition

There are three independently enforceable effects, never one aggregate:

| Holder or protected object | Direct effect | Not conferred |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Each present person | Environmental-right protection, information, assessment, participation, reasons, challenge and remedy | A new material-floor item or an environmental-worth score |
| Each protected common and future ecological capability | Separate non-destruction, ceiling, precaution, non-regression and restoration constraints | Ownership transfer, nature personhood or an unborn person's preferences |
| A credibly sentient non-human individual | Direct bodily, continued-life and species-appropriate interests | Human personhood, equality classification, floor, ballot, office, title or contract capacity |

The material-floor inventory, Article 1 anti-sanction wall, recognition's
non-operation, `admits` and existing derived-only declarations are unchanged.
`person`, `entitled`, `healthy`, `cruel`, `public`, `authority`, `work`,
`reward`, `family`, `home` and `owe` keep their existing meanings. A bare
occurrence of one is never a scientific, animal-use, appointment or ecological
authorization finding. Actual existing court, appointment, collective-consent
and scarcity contracts may be consumed only with their full keyed witnesses.

No `environment`, `ecology`, `sustainable`, `animal`, `sentient`, `balance`
or `worth` predicate is introduced. The existing corpus supplies `observe`
for admitted observations and `authorized` for caller-supplied credentials.
`complete` carries an exact named contract conclusion; `prevents` a named
legal bar; `obliged` an actor-facing duty; and `permits` only an exact named
bounded use. These conclusions do not establish that an act occurred.
Derived `member` enumerations distinguish legal categories, never rank people
or animals. Intermediate `related` and `contradict` markers are not scanner
contradictions and are not adverse personal findings.

Direct animal classification uses the existing corpus relation
`class(animal, ECCrediblySentientProtectedAnimal, finding)`, not the `member`
relation used by record-validation vocabularies. A rigorously reviewed taxon
presumption removal uses the derived-only relation
`narrow(taxon, constitutionVersion, ECAnimalTaxonPresumption)`. Its primary
rule reads its own raw independent evidence, not a general completion result.
This separates taxon-presumption revision from the `oppose` relation used for
categorically prohibited activities and keeps the dependency graph stratified.
The existing custody `limit` relation is unchanged. None of these predicates
advances a clock, authenticates a record or establishes animal personhood.

Each primary conclusion requires the complete raw contract, including every
upstream source and independent review. A consumer reads the actual derived
parent and rejoins all of that parent's own unambiguous fields, credentials
and currentness. It need not duplicate the parent's foreign institutional
proof: the parent conclusion still requires that whole proof. Record-keyed
direct effects use the same pattern. A globally keyed taxon or amendment
barrier cannot certify another finding's duties: those effects read their own
complete raw finding. No derived conclusion is admitted as evidence. Every
dependency has an actual-review withdrawal case, and all eight independent
ecological-axis requirements remain.
The authorization basis explicitly records each axis's protected condition,
joined to that axis's actual compliance record. Execution consumes those eight
positive bindings before testing non-substitution bars. An unconstrained
condition variable is not evidence that the affected condition is unbarred.

The corpus predicate `oppose(condition, project, axis)` is a narrow derived-only
dependency marker for a fully reviewed non-substitution finding: the protected
condition opposes the project's offset-as-permission claim on that axis. It is not an
omnibus environmental verdict. It gates the corresponding residual measure
and the exact axis condition in high-consequence execution, avoiding a
negative dependency on the broad `prevents` predicate,
which also contains the prospective measure's own legal effects. A finding on
one axis cannot be laundered into another axis's compliance or permission.
No new lexicon word is introduced. The initial invented marker name was
correctly refused by the closed lexicon and removed before canonical generation.

Current stays use the separately derived-only corpus predicate
`interrupt(operator, record, ECGuardianAutomaticStay)`; the distinct
active-stay relation is `oppose(authorizationRecord, authorizationVersion,
ECGuardianAutomaticStay)`. The existing generic qualification-certificate
predicate `grant` is not a stay producer or consumer: even a certificate naming
the stay action cannot replace its full legal gate. Execution and residual-measure
permissions use `permits`, separately from their reviewed bases. The derived
`end(candidateRecord, marker)` records a finality or conflicting-registry bar,
not the passage of time or the performance of cessation. Finality consumers
join the case, authorization record and version, evidence version and ground
to an actual independently reviewed final decision. The four conceptual replay
dimensions therefore use five ordinary fields. No event-shaped or opaque
office-local key is used. The canonical rules and cases are exported; complete
verification and its measured limits are recorded below.

Existing liberty/ecology barriers remain. They are not replaced by a new
record-dependent version that would make rights disappear with paperwork.
New findings supply distinct case-level consequences and institutional duties.

## Common finding and temporal contract

Every substantive card below incorporates this contract plus its row-specific
premises. Its exact source names and fields are retained in the explicit
`ecology` authoring module and ordinary exported Nibli fixtures.

- Identity: record, record revision, case, affected subject or common, place,
  territory, jurisdiction, affected population, legal scope, constitutional
  source/version, evidence record and evidence/method version.
- Accountability: authorized writer and independently authorized reader;
  separately named evidence producer, decision actor, executor, challenge
  reader, alternate reader and auditor where that function is exercised.
  Interested actors cannot review their own decisive finding. Independence
  requires actual distinct bindings and affirmative conflict/recusal evidence.
- Agreement: decisive identity and content fields must be jointly observed by
  the authorized writer and independent reader. Conflicting authorized values
  on a single-valued field withhold reliance; uncredentialed noise does not
  veto a valid record. Multiple legitimate claims or ecological axes remain
  separate records, not conflicting values in one identity slot.
- Public/private boundary: accessible reasons, uncertainty and material
  limitations; necessary purpose-limited private evidence; lawful independent
  access; challenge, correction, retention, deletion control and audit.
- Continuity: a named alternate, action recipient and remedy. Missing records
  cannot block urgent human or animal protection. A permission's absence is
  not proof of safety, guilt, delivery, cessation, expiry or successful repair.
- Time: each card binds its own contract kind, revision, window, start, end,
  fresh evaluation and independently supplied currentness. No custody T3 is
  reused. No silence-to-approval, automatic carry, implicit clock or unbounded
  renewal exists. A corrected expired authority requires a new current basis;
  it is not extended by renaming the record.

Formal reliance uses only a definitive top-level `TRUE`. `FALSE` is
non-derivability in the supplied snapshot. Invalid, incomplete, `UNKNOWN` and
resource-exceeded results authorize nothing. Authentication, classification,
novelty, scientific validity, actual time and institutional results stay
external, as required by the completed
[versioned-finding capability boundary](../../source/nibli-versioned-ecological-and-animal-finding-capability-audit.md).
The historical hash/receipt workflow in that record is retired.

A frozen admitted record can still produce the same conclusion on replay.
The formal model cannot force a later evaluation or withdrawal to arrive.
Pins must demonstrate both that limitation and the changed consequence when
an exact new evaluation or correction is actually supplied.

An independently authenticated source-window end is a past fact, not a new
time-limited permission. The exact end finding rejoins the affected source,
record, revision, kind, subject, case, source version, place, territory,
population, jurisdiction, scope, actor, window, endpoint and evidence identity.
It withdraws that reliance and marks the identified window ended. A newly named
record cannot use that same ended window; a fresh permission still needs every
substantive condition and its own independently established usable window.
Window identities must denote the exact authenticated intervals before
admission; the engine supplies neither their identity nor their chronology.
Ordinary expiry is not itself misconduct or proof that an activity stopped.

Climate, water, air, soil, biodiversity, habitat, extraction and waste remain
separate axes. A record for one can neither establish another's compliance nor
waive it. The broader protected objects include ecological systems, resilience,
regeneration, waste absorption and future ecological option-space. Qualitative
non-destruction duties do not disappear because quantitative evidence is absent.

No budget choice, monopoly, artificial withholding, refusal to procure or delay
establishes genuine physical/ecological scarcity. The actual economic scarcity
interface still governs allocation. Neither reproductive coercion, family
penalties, discriminatory migration/custody nor human scoring is permitted.
Emergency procedure does not derogate from a ceiling or the animal core.

The shared Guardian-stay key is the tuple **case, challenged authorization
(record identity plus version), reviewed evidence version/set and ground**.
It is not a hash, private
office counter or per-reviewer key. Guardian, alternate, successor, primary and
substitute reviewer all use it. A final disposition blocks a same-key restart
through every route. A changed office, record label or review window is not
novelty. Only materially new authenticated evidence or materially changed
authorization can justify a different key and fresh stay. The decision-maker
still determines whether those material facts are true; Nibli does not.

An expired stay is not approval of the challenged activity. Continued judicial
interim protection requires its own independently justified current order, not
an automatic extension of the expired objection. Neither an unavailable
initiator nor reviewer can create approval or an indefinite hold. A final
court outcome that actually restricts the challenged act remains a separate
legal bar after Guardian expiry; it does not wait for an additional enforcement
record. An outcome requiring protection duties is not automatically a ban on
the entire activity. Both ordinary and substitute judicial routes preserve
this distinction and the exact authorization identity.

The legacy power ledger contains historical holder labels that conflate some
scientific, legislative, advocate and adjudicative roles. They are not authority
inputs. The controlling decision and actual separated function contracts govern:
evidence FSBOD_28, regulation FSBOD_29, inspection FSBOD_30, Guardian FSBOD_22,
Animal Advocate FSBOD_23, ordinary/constitutional courts FSBOD_17/18, and distinct
predeclared alternates/substitute review. Current projections must state those
roles accurately without rewriting the historical rationale or asserting acts.

### Historical finality is not current judicial force

An authenticated historical-final-disposition finding has a different temporal
meaning from a current merits order. It binds the original final record,
revision, case, authorization identity/version, evidence version, ground,
outcome, court, source version, jurisdiction, scope and at-decision interval.
It joins the original writer's and independent reviewer's actual identity and
disposition observations. Separately authorized replay-source and independent
replay-review functions authenticate the published decision and full original
judicial proof at that source version, including the actual ordinary or
substitute mandate and safeguards. Interested original actors and advocates
cannot serve as those historical reviewers.

This retrospective authentication is a bounded external finding under the
capability audit, not an engine reconstruction of a previous world-state or a
claim that a proof was actually stored. Nibli does not authenticate the past
judgment, persist its own verdict, operate an archive, or make a stale court
mandate current. Its caller must retain lawful history and deliberately resolve
incorrect or conflicting historical evidence before reliance. Ordinary ending
of current legal force does not itself revoke evidence that the disposition
occurred. The historical finding's recorded interval scopes that past evidence;
it is not a continuing authorization interval.

The finding closes replay across both advocates and both judicial routes even
when the original merits record's present reliance ends or is corrected. It
never continues the original restriction or grants any current permission.
Four separate Guardian/alternate material-authorization/material-evidence paths
can consume this actual historical finding, but still require their own current
mandate, independent substantive old/new comparison, shared registry and fresh
stay window. A changed label, office, reviewer, ground or window remains
insufficient. Current-merits paths continue to consume the full existing court
contract directly; the historical path does not weaken that contract.

## Separate remedy and record cards

The source's FS-POW-145 through FS-POW-159 remain separate named effects:
activity-licence action; rescue; custody; rehoming; cessation; veterinary
treatment; rehabilitation; sanctuary; use disqualification; record correction;
care restitution; structural reform; monitoring; habitat restoration; and
non-repetition. Each order has its own actor, subject, evidence, independent
decision, current interval, privacy, appeal, continuity and action recipient.
No compact animal-protection token grants all of them.

Urgent protection does not await a controller's paperwork or a final liability
judgment. Coercive consequences for a human require the actual independent
justice contract; punitive/criminal liability additionally requires separately
adjudicated culpability and full due process. No animal remedy removes human
standing, floor, core liberty, political weight, due process or collective title.

Independent defect, withholding, nonresponse, correction, cessation and end
findings join the exact affected record/revision. They can withdraw its legal
reliance and trigger named review/continuity duties; they never invent a past
act, erase lawful history, extend harmful authority or establish successful
rescue/restoration. Purpose-limited animal records have no consequential-human
worth, risk, politics, family, migration, reward or entitlement reader.

Independent defect and correction findings do not await the original actor's
record or acknowledgement before reader, alternate, audit and continuity duties
arise. Withdrawal itself rejoins the exact affected source identity; a
different subject, record, revision, version, place, jurisdiction, scope,
window, kind or evidence packet is not withdrawn. Nonresponse requires an
actual request and independently established elapsed deadline; silence alone
creates neither a stay nor a permit. Minimal urgent human/animal requests have
a direct public protective-care duty without a completed finding, owner or
taxon record. None of these routes supplies search, seizure, custody, guilt or
proof that care was delivered.

## Verification, projection and handoff

### Actual amendment corridor

Exact amendment certification separately requires positive preservation of
direct credibly sentient animal subject status, the non-waivable severe
avoidable suffering prohibition and the dispensable killing prohibition.
Three independently reviewed breach contracts bind the actual amendment
record, binder, effect reviewer, base, candidate, proposal, transition,
effect review, jurisdiction and legal scope. They produce the existing
`contradict(record, AmendmentSourceAuthorization)` bar, which the real
certification, publication and effective-selection path consumes. Generic
compatibility, a majority, an emergency or another purpose supplies no waiver.

The breach producer reads raw exact target witnesses, never the target's
completion, and does not negatively consume its own withdrawal. It therefore
cannot obtain authority merely because the candidate was already certified.
Independent findings have their own full scoped evidence and currentness,
challenge, correction, history, continuity and reader duties. Their admission,
supersession and external authenticity remain the trusted caller's responsibility.
No missing breach is proof of compatibility or an exhaustive semantic audit.
The host remains the separate trusted-input, in-memory reference model.

Live sequences begin with all three amendment stages supported, then withdraw
their reliance on each actual core breach. Wrong identity and actor findings
cannot disable an unrelated candidate. Paired source counterfactuals remove
only the actual certification consumer's guard and expose that dependence.
The historical `permanent` label mechanism is unchanged and is not this gate.

### Execution and projection

Every card needs a positive example and independently isolated negative cases
for required fields, unauthorized/fused actors, exact scope/version/window
mismatches, missing/withheld evidence, conflicting authorized observations,
unrelated noise, challenge/correction and source-bound end. Composed cases must
exercise the actual upstream contracts, not favorable stand-in completion tags.
Explicit counterfactuals demonstrate the contribution of the load-bearing
guards. Scoped controls must use real retraction, not an approximation.

The complete routine verifier executes all substantive pins and contradiction
checks. Generator regression tests are development checks; generation is an
explicit authoring command. No hashes, receipts, freshness audits, report gates
or persistent verdicts are introduced. Completion requires a separately
reviewed, exact retained reader projection and the complete measured verifier.

Affected reader sites include chapters 8, 9, 12 and 14, the opening-note coverage
boundary, Part V, method and spine. Their claims must distinguish a legal
interface from an institution, empirical condition or completed act.
