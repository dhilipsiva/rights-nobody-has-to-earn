## Rights before a favourable record

Every person is owed accessible notice, information, interpretation,
accommodation, independent assistance, a hearing, challenge, reasons and an
effective remedy. Missing documents, money, immigration status, cooperation,
forgiveness, a successful claim or a conviction cannot purchase these rights.
Survivor support is not a finding of the accused's guilt. Confidentiality and
protection do not cancel fair disclosure and a meaningful defence: conflicts
need independently justified, necessary protective arrangements preserving
both sides' rights. Children have independent representation where interests
conflict; a representative is not the source of the child's standing.

The proceedings vocabulary is closed to the seven named domains. A favourable
finding is about an exact process or remedy record, not a person. Its absence
withholds only its affirmative effect, not any person's standing, floor,
political voice, liberty or right to challenge.

## Exact record contract

Each finding binds the affected subject, other affected party, case, domain,
source version, epoch, jurisdiction, legal scope, window and end condition.
Its separately authorized source, evidence assurer and independent reviewer
agree on every common and kind-specific field. The operator, challenge reader,
independent alternate and auditor are named. Sources, evidence, operators and
review roles are distinct; independent reviewers cannot be the interested
parties or the process actors whose work they review. Affirmative case-bound
conflict/recusal review is required in addition to distinct names.

Common fields supply the evidence record, accessible reasons, public reasons
with private evidence protected, purpose/retention/correction limits, challenge,
remedy, independent alternate, rights continuity and current reconciled status.
No automatic carry extends a finding to a new source, person, case or window.
An authorized conflict blocks the record instead of offering a convenient
alternate tuple. Uncredentialed noise supplies neither authority nor a veto.

## Direct effects

- Access and assistance: duties to supply accessible process and independent
  counsel/advocacy; confidential defence and independent child assistance.
- Investigation: compatibility of lawful evidence handling, preservation of
  inculpatory and exculpatory material, least-intrusive means, disclosed reasons
  and independent complaints. No arrest, search, surveillance or interrogation
  authority follows.
- Prosecution: independent, reasoned charging/non-charging procedure under the
  applicable burden; no investigation, adjudication, execution or final-review
  fusion. Neither survivor support nor aggregate patterns prove guilt.
- Hearing: timely effective notice and preparation, access to usable evidence,
  fair participation, applicable burden, a reasoned determination, privacy and
  effective appeal. Criminal guilt requires the ordinary criminal proof route;
  this family never writes a conviction or reverses that burden.
- Survivor protection: support, safety planning, confidential independent
  advice and non-retaliation without compulsory reporting, testimony,
  reconciliation, forgiveness or a predetermined verdict. Physical restrictions
  still require their separate lawful instrument.
- Restoration: a bounded optional route with actual, informed, revocable
  consent, independent advice, a complete externally assured participant record
  and a lawful rights-respecting agreement. Refusal or withdrawal cannot supply
  guilt, punishment, loss of services or a duty to forgive. It cannot silently
  settle another person's claim or erase public accountability.
- Individual relief: restitution, reparation, compensation, cessation,
  performance or a rights-bounded protective arrangement requires the actual
  current ordinary-court power, a fair hearing, a reasoned wrong/remedy basis and
  the exact affected case and parties. It does not establish payment or action.
- General invalidation: the actual current Constitutional Court power is
  required; ordinary case relief cannot substitute. Composition challenges
  consume the uninvolved alternate-panel power, never the challenged court's
  own decision about itself.
- Enforcement: only the specified non-coercive remedy may be implemented,
  under its source, actor, notice, contest, protected-floor and proportionality
  conditions. A remedy is not an arrest warrant, detention order, sentencing
  fact, or permission to seize essentials.
- Appeal, correction and nonresponse: anyone may seek review without the
  original operator's permission. An exact, independently reviewed positive
  defect withdraws affected reliance and creates preservation, correction and
  remedy duties. Positive evidence of notice, opportunity and elapsed deadline
  routes an unfulfilled response duty to a separated alternate. Silence alone
  does not establish a defect or legal outcome.
- Custodial safeguards and release continuity: bodily integrity, humane
  conditions, confidential communication, counsel, health, education and voice
  survive custody. Review must address continuing necessity and proportionality,
  alternatives and the current lawful end; it cannot renew custody or delay
  lawful release. Housing, care, documents, education, work access and social
  participation continue after release without debt, labour or obedience tests.

## Acceptance and limits

Pins cover every domain and direct effect; each missing common/specific field;
missing writer authority; interested or fused review; stale and conflicting
records; uncredentialed noise; omitted and mismatched dependencies; actual
court-authority withdrawal; general/case-specific separation; coerced or
withdrawn restoration; claimant/defendant, child and survivor continuity;
rights across custody and release; open challenge, positive nonresponse,
alternate action, exact defects and fresh correction. Counterfactuals remove
specific guard conjuncts from the actual constitution and must flip the
corresponding refusal. No pre-existing expectation is discarded.

The model checks supplied facts and record-bound legal consequences. It does
not authenticate evidence, calculate a deadline, decide a real dispute, compel
an institution, deliver a service, physically release someone or prove an
effective remedy. No hash, receipt, report gate or verification-time generator
is introduced. The author pre-approved recommended prose on 2026-09-13;
canonical additions still require a separate source/prose consistency review.
