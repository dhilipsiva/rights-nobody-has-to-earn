# The appendix — the design's paper trail

This directory carries the planning record behind Book 1: every author
ruling (`decisions/`), every rule family's legal contract card (`contracts/`),
the neutral briefs that preceded a ruling (`briefs/`), and the three maps —
the constitutional coverage map, the taxonomy and the red-team index
(`maps/`). They moved here from the former `new-book-plans/` on 2026-09-17,
with the `book-1-` prefix dropped from each name; the map of what was called
what is `tools/maps/2026-09-17-fold.json`. The formal source, its pins, the
generator inputs, the generated reports and the frozen audits moved beside
them to `../source/`.

**What this directory is, by ruling** (R4 of the 2026-09-16 rebuild, recorded
in `decisions/reading-order-and-appendix-decision.md`): a carried archive,
not a fourth channel of the book. The narrative-register ruling refuses "a
fourth exempt element" by name, and that refusal is overridden here narrowly
— for the *directory*, not the *book*. Nothing in this directory is a
passage, joins the register, is an ordered input to `combine.sh` or the pilot
builder, sits inside the edition boundary until a Gate B/C decision binds it,
is classified in the coverage ledger, counts in the length measurement, or may
be the sole support for a claim a chapter makes. The book's exempt elements
remain three: the opening note, Part V, and the method part.

Each file's SPDX header governs it wherever it lives; see `../../LICENSING.md`.
