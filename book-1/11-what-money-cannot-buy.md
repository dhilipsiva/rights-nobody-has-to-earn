# What Money Cannot Buy

Price is allowed to do a great deal in this design. It may mediate ordinary
exchange above the floor; it may decide who gets a particular house, a
particular machine, a particular evening's entertainment; wealth lawfully held
may be kept, spent, invested, and passed on. What price may never do is gate
the floor, and this chapter is about the walls that keep it from trying — and
about the public powers over money, property, and private power that the
design permits, each of them bounded by a record with an end.

The first wall is that no form of provision is privileged. Public, cooperative,
commons, mutual, nonprofit, household, and private arrangements are all lawful,
and no form is promised a share of anything, a profit, a rescue, an immunity, or
a monopoly; the barriers against each of those promises hold for every person.
The second is that ability to pay cannot decide access to what the floor owes:
price, wealth, credit, contribution, insurance status, and every other economic
factor are barred as a gate, and a cash payment, a voucher, or an insurance
approval is an instrument and not a delivery — it counts only when independent
evidence from the receiving side says the thing actually reached the person.
Those two walls are the floor's, and this chapter does not move them. Everything
below is what the design says about the money on the far side of them.

## Owning without owning the floor

Possession, use, transfer, and inheritance are protected conditional liberties.
A person may not be arbitrarily deprived of what they lawfully hold; the
barriers against denying lawful possession and lawful inheritance, and against
arbitrary deprivation, hold for every person. Personal possessions, ordinary
home use, and lawful productive activity receive strong security. None of this
is a right to a particular asset.

The housing floor is the sharpest place to see the difference. It guarantees an
adequate secure dwelling, not ownership and not perpetual possession of one
building, and the barrier against conflating the two holds for every person.
Rental, cooperative, customary, public, household, and private tenure are all
lawful, and no form may be suppressed. Informal or imperfect title does not
erase a person's dwelling claim. Before an eviction or a foreclosure may
threaten the floor, there must be legality, notice, a hearing, proportionality,
review, and a real public continuity route; the barrier against eviction or
foreclosure without continuity holds for every person, and the housing
continuity duty is one the record can name. A high-value asset need not be
preserved where adequate secure housing and due process remain real — that is
the reachable luxury of the acceptance cases, and the protected dwelling beside
it.

Compulsory acquisition is a public power with its own source-bound contract.
It needs law, a compatible public purpose, necessity, public reasons, a hearing,
review, and compensation calibrated to reliance, actual use, and hardship — and
the barrier against miscalibrating that compensation, and the one against
paying for a publicly created windfall, hold for every person. Illegal title,
unlawful privilege, a valid penalty, and remediation liability generate no
compensation claim merely because they have economic value. Public power,
political weight, immunity, and guilt cannot be inherited; an heir owes nothing
beyond what the estate was worth.

Knowledge exclusivity — a patent, a copyright — is a time-bounded statutory
incentive and not natural property. It may never block a floor or a commons
duty, and where the conflict arises the law must supply an effective compatible
access route; the compulsory access licence is its own power, and the barriers
against perpetual exclusivity and against exclusivity blocking the floor or the
commons hold for every person.

## A promise is not a trap

People may contract or refuse to contract, and the barriers against obstructing
either hold for every person. A signature is not consent where deception,
coercion, unconscionable dependency, material non-disclosure, or the absence of
a meaningful exit controls the transaction, and no enforceable contract may
waive the floor, equality, labour rights, core privacy, commons duties, due
process, or an effective remedy — the barrier against a contract waiving the
corridor holds for every person. Public-facing services require accessible
terms, safety, correction, cancellation where appropriate, collective redress,
and continuity where the service is essential; the barriers against consumer
abuse and against essential-consumer discontinuity hold for every person.

An enterprise is a tool, not a person. It receives exactly the functional
capacities needed to own, contract, associate, and litigate, and the barriers
against denying it those four and against giving it any capacity beyond its
function hold on both sides. It has no human standing, no floor, no ballot, no
candidacy, and no inherent political weight, and the barrier against enterprise
human standing holds for every person. Limited liability is a conditional
privilege that can be withdrawn for fraud, evasion, asset stripping, abusive
control, deliberate undercapitalisation, or liability dumping, and beneficial
control cannot be concealed. An enterprise may petition, testify, and publish
attributed views, and the barriers against suppressing any of the three hold;
it may not fund a candidate or a party or buy independent electoral advocacy,
and an association that fields candidates cannot escape that rule by relabelling
itself — the chapter on the vote carries the finding that makes the treasury
barrier catchable.

## When a private power owes public duties

The power to bind concentrated private power is not open-ended, and this is
where it stopped being a form of words. A finding that a private function
carries public duties has to state its ground, and the grounds are named in the
constitution itself: that the function is essential, that one actor dominates
it, that it is the gate others must pass, that people depend on it, that they
are locked into it, that its worth to each user comes from all the others
already being there, that one side knows what the other cannot, or that leaving
is not really available. A finding that states some other ground states nothing
this design can read. Being large is not on the list. Neither is being
profitable, nor being organised one way rather than another — a private body may
be enormous and bind nobody, and a small one standing in a doorway may bind a
great deal.

The same holds for what may be bound. A finding reaches a function that faces
the public, or is carried on the public's behalf under delegation, or is
essential, or is the gate, or steers a whole system — and reaches nothing else,
so ordinary private life cannot be relabelled into range. And it holds for who
may make it: a gatekeeper whose reach stops inside one region is that region's
to find, and one whose reach crosses regions belongs to the common tier. Pair
them the other way and the finding carries nothing at all. What none of this
reaches is whether the ground is true of anybody.

The vocabulary is executable, and its pins are the chapter's. Every ratified
ground is a member of the trigger vocabulary and nothing else is, however it is
attested; the function classes and the tier pairings are closed the same way;
and the three sets do not leak into one another — a function class attested as
a ground is not a ground. No supplied record can write a membership. The finding
itself, with its actor, function, affected people, evidence, source, scope,
review route, and end, is a public power with a source-bound contract, and the
remedies that can follow it — an access mandate, a public option, structural
separation, breakup, receivership, licence withdrawal, public acquisition — are
each a separate power rejoining that exact finding. Every remedy must protect
workers, users, and floor recipients and preserve essential-service continuity.
And when the finding expires, its restrictive authority ends while ordinary
rights and the public continuity route survive; the acceptance cases run that
expiry, and the counterfactual that removes a power's current review shows what
each power loses when it loses its currency.

Nothing here reaches a household, a friendship, or an intimate relationship.
Dependency or information asymmetry inside one does not make it a public
service; coercion, captivity, violence, and obstructed exit there are the
justice and protection rules' business, not this finding's, and the barrier
against treating the voluntary sphere as a social score holds for every person.

## Public money

Taxation is a public power, and the system as a whole must reflect capacity to
contribute — the barrier against breaching that principle holds for every
person, as does the one against denying the procedural rights every tax
carries. Democratic law chooses rates, bases, exemptions, and mixtures, and none
of those choices is in this record. Collection may reach above-floor income and
assets and may not reach the floor's essentials or an adequate dwelling, may not
imprison a debtor, and may not remove standing or a remedy.

Taxation, appropriation, spending, guarantees, and borrowing each require a
legislative source, complete reporting, audit, and fiscal-risk disclosure. Each
is its own power with its own contract, and the barrier against a fiscal act
with no authority, the one against concealing the reporting, and the one
against borrowing a fiscal source across tiers hold for every person. There is
no balanced-budget rule and no numeric debt ceiling in the constitution, and
the barrier against writing one in holds; public debt cannot waive
constitutional authority, subordinate the floor to creditors, or erase a commons
duty, and the barrier against a public-debt corridor waiver holds. A budget
deadlock continues the prior essential authority and the floor and creates no
new spending power; the barrier against essential discontinuity under deadlock
holds for every person.

## Money you can hold in your hand

The common tier maintains a public unit of account and an accessible settlement
backbone, and the backbone must include a route that is not digital; the
barrier against a digital-only backbone holds for every person, because a floor
that can only be reached through a device has a gate in it. Complementary
payment instruments may be regulated, and each of the three — the unit of
account, the backbone, the regulation of complements — is a power with its own
contract.

A monetary authority may be operationally independent only inside a
democratically enacted mandate, and its independence is bounded on every side
the barriers name: it must publish reasons, undergo distributional review and
audit, use cause-only removal, and hold no veto over lawful fiscal policy. The
barriers against unbounded monetary independence, against a monetary veto over
fiscal policy, against the arbitrary removal of a monetary officer, and against
denying the reasons and the review hold for every person.

There is no general right to credit, and the barrier against reading one in
holds. A credit or insurance decision is a power that needs lawful criteria,
reasons, equality, correction of the data it relied on, and a challenge route;
the barrier against a decision without that process holds, and no floor service
may depend on private insurance. A contribution record may not be reused as a
standing, worth, risk, or political score — the same purpose limit the previous
chapter guards on the record itself.

## Which tier decides

The common tier owns the unit of account and the payment backbone, floor
finance and equalisation, portability, interregional commerce and competition,
insolvency baselines, cross-regional private power, and the common labour and
consumer minima — and nothing more. It has no general commerce, spending,
taxation, or pre-emption power. Residual ownership, land, enterprise, service,
cooperative, municipal-enterprise, and local-development policy is regional or
local, and the barrier against erasing that residual competence holds for every
person. A region may protect more strongly, and the barrier against displacing a
stronger regional protection holds; it may not lower the common floor or minima,
obstruct portability, impose a waiting period or a property qualification, or
use fiscal competition to defeat equality, and the barriers against each of
those hold for every person.

## When an economic power ends, and what does not

Every temporary economic power in this chapter — an acquisition, a licence, a
private-power finding and each of its remedies, a tax, an appropriation, a
borrowing, a monetary decision, a credit decision — carries its own source-bound
temporal contract, and none of them may borrow the custody contract's clock.
That is the strain this family is built to be under. The counterfactuals remove
each power's current independent review in turn, and each power's conclusions
stop with nothing else moving; a missing source, trigger, scope, review, or end
closes the restrictive or managerial authority rather than extending it. Review
silence never becomes approval; a required reviewer who is unavailable is
replaced through a predeclared alternate route, and an alternate's absence is
neither approval nor an indefinite hold.

What does not end with the power is the floor and the essential service. When a
manager's authority lapses, the independent public floor and the essential-
service continuity duty survive and transfer through the alternate route; the
barrier against erasing a remedy through enterprise failure and the one against
interrupting an essential service hold for every person. A power that has lost
its currency has lost the ability to restrict. It has not gained the ability to
stop anything the floor owes.

## The child with nobody

For the child with nobody, this chapter reaches nothing, because the child
holds nothing: no property, no contract, no enterprise, no debt, no account.
What it does instead is refuse, for Nell by name, every way that money could
have been made a condition: no economic factor may gate what the floor owes, a
payment instrument is not a delivery, no household is presumed to provide, and
no voluntary sphere can be turned into a score. The pins hold each of those for
the one-entry record. The child cannot buy anything, and there is nothing here
the child would have to buy.

## What none of this counts

This chapter values no property, calculates no tax, clears no market, measures
no dominance, forecasts no supply, authenticates no financial record, and
operates no payment. A power that completes here proves that the record for it
was complete, not that anything was acquired, taxed, spent, lent, or paid; a
finding that a private function carries public duties proves that its ground
was one the design can read, not that the ground is true of anybody; a barrier
that holds proves that the rule is in place, not that a court applied it. The
engine can say which named ground a finding claims and refuse one that claims
none; it cannot tell whether a network effect exists or whether an exit is
meaningful. Rates, budgets, models, prices, production, inventories, monetary
instruments, and every question of feasibility belong to the second book. No
sentence here says that an economy exists, works, or was ever operated.
